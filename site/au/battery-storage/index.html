No single official national list exists. For the NEM (QLD, NSW/ACT, VIC, SA, TAS) AEMO's quarterly NEM Generation Information xlsx lists each battery unit with status, MW AC, MWh and owner (58 units across 'In Service' and 'In Commissioning', Oct 2026); NEMWEB MMS tables add registered participant, station address and registration dates. Neither gives coordinates and commissioning dates are mostly blank. For WA's WEM, AEMO's daily facilities CSV gives only code, participant and System Size (combined import and export rating). Open Electricity (non-official, CC BY-NC) covers NEM+WEM with coordinates, MW, MWh, owner and start date. NT/off-grid not verified.

Confidence reason: NEM and WEM official files were downloaded and cross-checked; NT/off-grid, AER and DCCEEW pages could not be opened (403/timeouts) and Overpass was unavailable.

Local search terms

  • NEM Generation Information — AEMO's quarterly xlsx of existing and proposed NEM generating/storage units; filter Technology Type 'Battery Storage'.
  • DUID (Dispatchable Unit Identifier) — Unit code that joins GI, MMS tables, SCADA and Open Electricity.
  • Bidirectional unit (BIDIRECTIONAL) — MMS DISPATCHTYPE for batteries registered under the IESS rules (from mid-2024).
  • Electric Storage Resource (ESR) — WEM term for storage; battery facility codes contain '_ESR' (or '_BESS').
  • System Size — WEM facility capacity in MW; for storage it adds withdrawal to sent-out capacity.
  • Full Commercial Use Date (FCUD) — GI field: date units are expected to become operationally available after NER 5.7.3 tests.
  • In Commissioning / In Service — GI statuses: hold point >=30% capacity passed / commissioning signed off by AEMO.
  • BESS — Battery energy storage system; common in AEMO site names (e.g. 'Orana BESS').

Main caveats

  • Registration is not operation: MMS START_DATE and 'COMMISSIONED' mark registration (often the 2024 IESS re-registration); GI 'In Service' needs AEMO sign-off after commissioning tests.
  • GI status can lag: Waratah Super Battery (WTAHB1) is 'Committed' in the Oct 2026 release while MMS SCADA shows it discharging up to 556.8 MW in Oct 2026.
  • WEM 'System Size' adds withdrawal to export capacity: KWINANA_ESR1 System Size 200 vs ~100 MW peak in Oct 2026 SCADA; use a documented export rating. SCADA shows observed output only.
  • GI and MMS give MW and MWh per generating unit (DUID row); a site or hybrid spans several rows and 'Max Site Capacity (AC)' caps the site.
  • Official files name owner, MSATS custodian or registered participant, not an operator; they can differ (MREHA3: owner Equis SPV, participant SEC Energy).
  • Open Electricity is non-official, CC BY-NC 4.0 and excludes AI training/grounding from its standard licence; its MW, MWh and dates can differ from AEMO.
