There is no single national list. Best official sources: the NSW Major Projects register (102 stand-alone BESS cases across stages with stage, LGA, address, map point, MW in text, decision date once determined) and the national EPBC referrals ArcGIS layer (104 battery-named federal referrals across stages with polygon, state and federal stage). Other states differ: WA's DAP list and VIC permit pages give stage, dates and applicant; QLD and SA lists are script-rendered and refuse automated clients. Developer and open-stage dates are mostly inside PDFs. AEMO's NEM xlsx helps discovery but its commitment status is not a permit stage.

Confidence reason: NSW, EPBC, WA, ACT and AEMO were tested by query or document; QLD and SA rows could not be read (403/script), so coverage outside NSW is partial.

Local search terms

  • State Significant Development (SSD) — NSW state assessment pathway used by large BESS; case numbers SSD-… in the Major Projects register.
  • Electricity generation - Other / Electricity Supply — NSW Major Projects development_type values under which BESS cases are filed.
  • battery storage facility — Queensland Planning Regulation land use; SARA is assessment manager since 12 December 2025.
  • utility installation — Victorian land-use term in BESS permit descriptions ('utility installation comprising a Battery Energy Storage System').
  • Development Facilitation Program (DFP) — Victorian pathway where the Minister decides; shown as 'Program' on ministerial permit pages.
  • Development Assessment Panel (DAP) — WA panels deciding large BESS applications; references DAP/YY/NNNNN.
  • EPBC referral / controlled action — Federal environmental referral and the decision that full assessment is needed.
  • Commitment Status — AEMO development stage (Publicly Announced, Anticipated, Committed); not a planning stage.

Main caveats

  • Federal EPBC referral status is a separate environmental process: a 'Completed' referral says nothing about state development consent, and many BESS are never referred.
  • MW basis differs between sources: grid export, installed rating and AEMO AC nameplate (Wattle Creek: 350 MW export, up to 516 MW rating, 372.6 MW AC).
  • Hybrids: QLD assesses BESS ancillary to wind/solar inside that application; NSW splits hubs into separate cases; WA's list mixes both. Filter by description.
  • VIC, QLD, SA and TAS planning sites and the NT EPA register answered 403 to automated clients on 2026-11-26; VIC was read only through archived copies.
  • AEMO commitment status is not a permit stage: Tramway Road (VIC permit 31/10/2025) is still 'Publicly Announced' in the October 2026 release.
