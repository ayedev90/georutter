ONS publishes half-hourly wind and solar constrained-off estimates for covered plants or groups on Brazil's interconnected system (SIN). The CSVs give subsystem, state and connection point. Multiply each nonempty half-hour average-power estimate by 0.5 hours for MWh. The series can be revised. A separate redispatch-volume series remains unverified.

Confidence reason: Both October 2026 CSVs and their dictionaries were tested; redispatch and calculation details remain open.

Local search terms

  • constrained-off — ONS operational restriction of generation; the files estimate output not generated.
  • geração não realizada apurada — GNRa, non-negative reference minus actual output during restricted intervals.
  • redespacho — Redispatch; not identified with GNRa.
  • subsistema — ONS regional subsystem.

Main caveats

  • Wind and solar files cover specific ONS operating categories. The figures can change with revisions and do not represent all renewable generation in Brazil.
  • GNRa is estimated curtailed generation, not redispatch or financial compensation.
  • The dictionary and tested ENE rows disagree on whether final reference is populated only for REL.
