Confidence
Medium

No public list of individual connection requests was found in the sources checked. Enedis publishes quarterly aggregates by department, technology and power band since 2020. Its queue field sums to 32,789 on 2026-09-30 (about 32.8 GW if the field is in MW; the schema does not state the unit). Caparéseau gives queue power per substation, ODRE regional totals and EDF SEI figures for Corsica and overseas territories. The CRE procedure describes how distributors send project and client names to RTE.

Confidence reason: Enedis, ODRE, Caparéseau and EDF datasets were queried and CRE's procedure read; the absence of a request list rests on these pages, not on a registry statement.

Main caveats

  • The checked datasets give aggregates. None lists a project name, a node, a status or an event date per request.
  • Enedis counts only its own network; RTE, local distributors and storage or consumption queues need other sources.
  • 'In development' differs by operator: RTE counts queue entry or accepted technical proposals, Enedis counts requests qualified complete.
  • The Enedis power unit is not declared in the schema (MW consistent); ODRE has one annual date.
  • Caparéseau queue power covers S3REnR generation only; summing substations was not reconciled across RTE and distributor views.
