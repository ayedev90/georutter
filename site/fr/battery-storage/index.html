The official answer is the Registre national des installations de production et de stockage d'électricité on ODRÉ (RTE with Enedis, EDF SEI and local DSOs). Filtering codeFiliere STOCK gives 938 in-service storage rows (including 10 flywheel and 7 'Autre') at 30/09/2026 (2,453 MW, ≈3,347 MWh) with commune, charge/discharge kW, storable kWh, connection and commissioning dates. It does not publish the battery operator (only the grid operator), gives no coordinates, has no MWh for the 7 ZNI rows and no Mayotte row. Operators come from CRE ZNI deliberations, owner releases and OSM (23 large plants, joinable by EIC code).

Confidence reason: Register downloaded and profiled, fine print quoted from glossary and arrêté; operator gap is legal. ZNI checked only through register copies and one CRE case; Corsica EDF document blocked.

Main caveats

  • gestionnaire is the network operator (RTE, Enedis, EDF-SEI, ELD), not the battery operator; the exploitant is collected but not public.
  • dateMiseEnService is the grid operator's view: Cernay-lès-Reims shows 12/01/2026, the owner reports commercial operation since May 2026.
  • Hybrid batteries sit inside PV, wind, hydro or thermal rows (39 individual rows with typeStockage BATTE outside STOCK; small PV only as aggregates).
  • ZNI: only 7 EDF SEI rows without MWh or names; Mayotte absent (CRE deliberations fill part); EDF island batteries on Sein/Ouessant absent.
  • Row counts are not site or company counts: 706 rows are distributed 'NWJ' units of ≈1.25 MW; some sites have several rows.
  • Totals differ by source: register 31/12/2025 mainland 741 rows/1,531 MW/1,762 MWh vs CRE 758 projects/1,512 MW/1,720 MWh.

Universe of battery operators unknown; the ATEE/CRE observatory identified 45 organisations for HTA/HTB storage in mainland France (31 responded). Tested: 2 mainland RTE sites, NW, and 2 of the 7 EDF SEI ZNI rows (Corse, Martinique).

Search '<commune> stockage batteries MW MWh mise en service' or the operator named in OSM; owner press releases and the Banque des Territoires 'réalisations' pages carry dates.
