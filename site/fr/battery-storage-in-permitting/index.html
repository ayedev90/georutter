No unified French registry tracks all stand-alone BESS currently in permitting. Nationally, the Ministry portal NOVAE exposes an open API and Excel export for R.122-2 environmental screening (cas par cas) and opinions, including open cases, though MW and developer appear only inside attached PDFs and smaller sites below thresholds are omitted. Major full-EIA consultations appear on projets-environnement.gouv.fr. Most BESS (>600 kW Li-ion) file an ICPE 2925-2 Déclaration whose receipts are scattered across 101 departmental Préfectures, while pending building permits are excluded from Sitadel (CRPA L.311-2).

Confidence reason: Verified across Ministry portals, live NOVAE/projets-environnement/ODRÉ/Enedis/EDF APIs, Géorisques/Sitadel docs and files, DDT77 BESS guide, 4 Préfecture 2925-2 pages/PDFs and commercial pages.

Not published: Pending urbanism application filing/instruction dates remain unpublished in Sitadel and offline at town halls/DDTs.

Main caveats

  • NOVAE inProgress=true omits 'À COMPLÉTER' dossiers (missing open standalone BESS 005778/KK P in Œuilly, 5 MW); query without inProgress or filter documentStatus manually.
  • Most 1-20+ MW standalone BESS require only ICPE 2925-2 Déclaration (>600 kW) plus Permis de construire/DP, so projects below R.122-2 thresholds never appear in NOVAE unless filed voluntarily.
  • Sitadel excludes all pending building permits ('en cours d'instruction') under CRPA art. L.311-2, omits permits without floor area, and strips project descriptions.
  • Géorisques ICPE covers only active/ended Autorisation and Enregistrement facilities, excluding 2925-2 Déclaration-only BESS and pending applications.
  • Grid queue datasets (ODRÉ, Enedis data-fair, EDF Corse-Outremer, RTE Cartostock, Caparéseau) publish substation MW headroom or regional totals, never named permitting projects.
