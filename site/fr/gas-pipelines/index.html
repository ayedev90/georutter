France gas pipelines data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of natural gas transmission and distribution pipelines in France, with operator, diameter or pressure, and status?

Answer

No single public GIS registry covers all French gas pipelines. Official open data is split: ODRÉ publishes simplified (~250 m) in-service transmission routes for NaTran (11,615 lines) and Teréga (1,298 lines), while Agence ORE aggregates 13 DSOs (4.24M distribution lines; 99.2% of served commune-entries) and GRDF publishes 3.75M in-service rows. Official GIS omits diameter, numeric pressure (97.0% 'Inconnu' on ORE; 3.0% qualitative across 4 ELDs), 11 small ELDs, and non-operating lines. Precise plans are restricted via INERIS Guichet Unique (DT-DICT) and DREAL (HTTP 403). Crowd-mapped OSM (Postpass) and SciGRID_gas (2021) partially fill technical tags.

Local search terms

  • canalisation de transport (de gaz naturel) — High-pressure natural gas transmission pipeline (e.g., operated by NaTran or Teréga, regulated as TDM/TMD under Code de l'environnement).
  • canalisation de distribution (de gaz) — Medium- or low-pressure local gas distribution pipeline operated by GRDF or an ELD.
  • GRT (Gestionnaire de réseau de transport) — Gas transmission system operator (TSO): NaTran (formerly GRTgaz) and Teréga in France.
  • GRD / ELD (Gestionnaire de réseau de distribution / Entreprise locale de distribution) — Distribution system operator (DSO): GRDF nationally plus ~22–25 historic/new local municipal/mixed utilities (ELDs).
  • guichet unique (DT-DICT) — Mandatory INERIS declaration portal (`reseaux-et-canalisations.ineris.fr`) for groundworks; replaces open maps with operator-supplied precise plans.
  • DN (Diamètre nominal) / PMS (Pression maximale en service) — Nominal pipe diameter in mm and maximum operating pressure in bar, standard in PDDs and environmental filings.
  • SUP I1 / I3 (Servitude d'utilité publique) — Public utility easement zones around gas/hydrocarbon pipelines (I3 for transport, I1 for hydrocarbons/certain pipelines) recorded in urban plans.
  • En service / arrêt définitif — Operational (`en service`) vs permanently decommissioned (`arrêt définitif`) pipeline status in French filings.

Main caveats

  • No official public GIS layer includes pipe diameter or numeric operating pressure; NaTran/Teréga open routes are generalized to ~250 m (1:250,000) and omit lines <250 m.
  • Official open datasets explicitly cover in-service infrastructure only (`en service`), exclude 11 small ELDs (76 commune-entries), and warn they cannot legally substitute for INERIS Guichet Unique (DT-DICT).
  • In Agence ORE `infrastructures-reseau-gaz`, 97.0% of 4.24M segments have `niveau_de_pression='Inconnu'` and operator `date_maj` ranges from 2021-04-01 to 2026-06-02; GRDF's `propane` field is 100% null.
  • Non-official SciGRID_gas `IGGIELGNC-3` (2021) has `country_code=['ES','PT']` corrupted across all European segments—filter France by joining `node_id` to `IGGIELGNC3_Nodes.csv` (`FR`).
  • Several public Overpass API endpoints timed out or returned HTTP 403/504 during testing; Geofabrik's Postpass SQL API (`postpass.geofabrik.de`) succeeded and returned 5,524 FR-operator features.
