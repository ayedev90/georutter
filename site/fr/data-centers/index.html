France data centers data sources

Where can I get a list of data centers in operation in France, with location, operator and capacity (IT power in MW or floor area)?

Answer

No public national registry lists operating data centers in France. EU EED Art. 12 and French L. 236-1 / SDES (471 sites) / ARCEP publish aggregates only. Officially, Institut Paris Région's open GIS API covers 149 active Île-de-France sites with coordinates, operator, grid-MW class, and IT/building m², while EDF publishes 2 corporate sites on data.gouv.fr. Nationwide, DCmag (307 neutral sites), uMap/OSM (354 sites), and PeeringDB offer open locations and operators with partial or no capacity, alongside gated/paid directories (Baxtel 243 sites, GuideDC login DB, and blocked Data Center Map).

Main caveats

  • EU EED 2023/1791 Art. 12(3) and ecologie.gouv.fr restrict the European Data Centre Database to aggregated statistics; SDES's 471-DC inventory and ARCEP's ~160-DC survey publish aggregates only.
  • Institut Paris Région's official FeatureServer covers only Île-de-France (149 active out of 209 total; ~1/3 of French sites), and its 'puiss_mw_classe' field measures electrical connection capacity bins rather than IT load.
  • DRIEAT Île-de-France's data.gouv.fr mirror (6a1f7068092c5d59f2777f04) has broken Atom ('Accès non autorisé') and WFS endpoints; Institut Paris Région's geoweb.iau-idf.fr FeatureServer is the working official feed.
  • Nationwide non-official maps (DCmag 307 sites, PeeringDB) exclude private corporate/public sites such as EDF or banks, and DCmag exposes capacity only on individual copyrighted HTML pages rather than its bulk REST endpoint.
