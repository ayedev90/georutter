France electrical substations data sources

Where can I get the locations of electrical substations in France (transmission and distribution), with voltage level and operator?

Answer

France has no single open substation dataset covering both transmission and distribution with coordinates, voltage, and operator. Distribution (HTA/BT) is well covered by Agence ORE (1,033,309 public substations across 47 operators, ~98.9% of communes) plus Enedis (976,678) and EDM Mayotte (673). For transmission/primary (HTB), RTE and EDF SEI removed GPS coordinates from Open Data for public security: ODRÉ keeps all 5,042 RTE nodes with voltage by département only, Caparéseau gives coords and HTB class (u_max) for 3,116 continental S3REnR substations (40 bad coords, 1,285 RTE sites missing), and IGN BD TOPO gives 4,133 footprints (>=63 kV, DROM included) without voltage.

Main caveats

  • RTE (ODRÉ), EDF SEI, and Enedis deleted/restricted HTB poste-source GPS coordinates for public security; no official coordinate feed covers all 4,232 RTE transformation substations.
  • Legacy ODS APIs on Agence ORE, Enedis, and EDF SEI return 404/410; platforms migrated to Data Fair (/data-fair/api/v1/datasets), while ODRÉ and EDM Mayotte still use Opendatasoft v2.1.
  • Caparéseau excludes Corse (filtered in UI JS), DROM-COM, and 1,285 RTE HTB sites, merges 225kV and 400kV into u_max=6, and contains 40 invalid coords near (-1.36, -5.98).
  • IGN BD TOPO includes all >=63kV & rail substations across Metro, Corse, DROM, St-Martin/St-Barthélemy (4,133 polygons) but omits voltage and leaves sources blank on 81.1%.
  • Agence ORE HTA/BT omits ~63 small ELDs (~1.1% of communes), Mayotte (673 posts on EDM), COM (NC, PF, SPM, WF), and private HTA/BT substations; SRD's ORE table has 4x duplicate rows.

Local search terms

  • Term
    Poste de transformation
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Electrical substation housing transformers and switchgear connecting different voltage levels (4,232 in RTE ODRÉ; 4,133 >=63 kV polygons in IGN BD TOPO).
  • Term
    Poste source
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Primary HTB/HTA (or HTB/HTB) interface substation connecting the high-voltage transmission grid to medium-voltage public distribution (3,116 S3REnR sites in Caparéseau; 3 in EDM Mayotte).
  • Term
    Poste HTA/BT (poste de distribution publique)
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Public medium-to-low voltage distribution transformer substation (typically 20 kV to 400 V; 1,033,309 in Agence ORE).
  • Term
    Point de piquage
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Tee/tap junction point on a transmission line (810 nodes in RTE ODRÉ), not a standalone transformation enclosure.
  • Term
    HTB / HTA / BT
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    French voltage tiers: HTB (>50 kV transmission: HTB1 63/90kV, HTB2 150/225kV, HTB3 400kV), HTA (1–50 kV, typically 20 kV), BT (<=1 kV, typically 400/230 V).
  • Term
    GRT / GRD / ELD
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    GRT = transmission operator (RTE); GRD = distribution operator (Enedis on 95% + ~150-160 ELDs / Entreprises Locales de Distribution on ~5%).
  • Term
    ZNI
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Zones non interconnectées (Corse, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Guyane, Réunion, Mayotte, St-Barthélemy, St-Pierre-et-Miquelon, Wallis-et-Futuna, îles du Ponant; excludes NC and PF).
  • Term
    S3REnR
    Language
    fr
    Meaning
    Schéma régional de raccordement au réseau des énergies renouvelables; governs substation hosting capacities published on Caparéseau.
