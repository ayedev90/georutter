France has no single open substation dataset covering both transmission and distribution with coordinates, voltage, and operator. Distribution (HTA/BT) is well covered by Agence ORE (1,033,309 public substations across 47 operators, ~98.9% of communes) plus Enedis (976,678) and EDM Mayotte (673). For transmission/primary (HTB), RTE and EDF SEI removed GPS coordinates from Open Data for public security: ODRÉ keeps all 5,042 RTE nodes with voltage by département only, Caparéseau gives coords and HTB class (u_max) for 3,116 continental S3REnR substations (40 bad coords, 1,285 RTE sites missing), and IGN BD TOPO gives 4,133 footprints (>=63 kV, DROM included) without voltage.

Confidence reason: All core official portals, Data Fair/ODS APIs, Caparéseau JSON endpoints, IGN WFS layers, and overseas catalogs were directly queried and cross-checked locally on 2026-11-15.

Main caveats

  • RTE (ODRÉ), EDF SEI, and Enedis deleted/restricted HTB poste-source GPS coordinates for public security; no official coordinate feed covers all 4,232 RTE transformation substations.
  • Legacy ODS APIs on Agence ORE, Enedis, and EDF SEI return 404/410; platforms migrated to Data Fair (/data-fair/api/v1/datasets), while ODRÉ and EDM Mayotte still use Opendatasoft v2.1.
  • Caparéseau excludes Corse (filtered in UI JS), DROM-COM, and 1,285 RTE HTB sites, merges 225kV and 400kV into u_max=6, and contains 40 invalid coords near (-1.36, -5.98).
  • IGN BD TOPO includes all >=63kV & rail substations across Metro, Corse, DROM, St-Martin/St-Barthélemy (4,133 polygons) but omits voltage and leaves sources blank on 81.1%.
  • Agence ORE HTA/BT omits ~63 small ELDs (~1.1% of communes), Mayotte (673 posts on EDM), COM (NC, PF, SPM, WF), and private HTA/BT substations; SRD's ORE table has 4x duplicate rows.
