Partial coverage
Non official data

Confidence
Medium

Caparéseau (RTE with Enedis and local distributors) publishes generation hosting capacity per substation: 3,116 mainland substations in a national CSV without login, with per-substation update dates up to 2026-11-10. It covers production only and is indicative; no per-node consumption capacity was found (RTE Cartostock covers batteries, viewer only). There is no history file: annual 31 December tables sit in RTE's regional reports. Corsica and overseas are on EDF's portal (101 rows, quarterly).

Confidence reason: National CSV, regional JSON and two RTE reports were downloaded and compared; consumption-side tools (Cartostock data, Enedis collectivités map) and other regions' reports were not read.

Main caveats

  • Caparéseau shows capacity reserved under the S3REnR schemes, not physical grid headroom, and is indicative and non-binding.
  • INFO_NA, RTE_CDR and GRD1_CDR differ: a zero can be saturated or not reserved; installed transformer power is not available capacity.
  • No demand-side capacity per node was found; Caparéseau and the Enedis zone map are production-only.
  • History exists only as annual PDF tables in RTE reports (rounded to integers in 2024) and needs manual extraction.
  • Mainland Caparéseau excludes Corsica and overseas; data.gouv.fr links to the EDF dataset are dead redirects.
