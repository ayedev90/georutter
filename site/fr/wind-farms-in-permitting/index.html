Confidence

For onshore wind, the national OREOL table published daily by Géorisques (WFS parc_wfs) lists 2,566 parks with capacity, stage and event dates: 478 in instruction (about 8.9 GW), 2,085 authorised. The developer is anonymised for most parks (ICPE joins name operators of authorised parks) and refusals are missing; some statuses lag. Offshore is separate: Géolittoral polygons (39) and eoliennesenmer.fr pages.

Confidence reason: OREOL counts and fields were downloaded and cross-checked with ICPE and one prefectoral order; offshore and regional parts were sampled, and API v2 of Géorisques was not used.

Main caveats

  • Developer is anonymised or blank for 2,350 of 2,566 OREOL parks; ICPE names operators only for authorised parks, and operators are not necessarily the original developers.
  • 'En instruction' can be stale: 260 of 478 have date_maj before 2025 and Bois des Saules (order of 30 April 2026) is still 'En instruction'.
  • Refused and abandoned projects are absent nationally; BFC regional data keeps them (refusée 596, abandonnée 165).
  • Onshore and offshore have separate circuits: OREOL/Géorisques for land, Cerema/Géolittoral and eoliennesenmer.fr for sea.
  • Capacity of a park in instruction is the applied capacity; one park has several rows (one per municipality).
