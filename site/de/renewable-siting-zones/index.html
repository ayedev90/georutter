Confidence
Medium

UBA publishes a national 2025 snapshot of designated onshore wind polygons, with plan and category fields; regional authorities publish newer plan geometry and some acceleration-area information. Lower Saxony also publishes a wind-specific sensitivity model. These do not form a complete current national register across technologies or prove acceleration status.

Confidence reason: Three actual polygon downloads were inspected; the regional acceleration layer and full national coverage were not verified.

Main caveats

  • Regional plan geometry is not parcel-accurate or necessarily legally binding; consult the enacted plan and notices.
  • Priority zones, modelled potential and legally designated acceleration areas are different categories.
  • The UBA snapshot is as of 2025-12-31 and may omit local data and subsequent changes.
  • Acceleration status and nationwide solar and offshore coverage remain incompletely checked.
