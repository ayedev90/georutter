Germany has no unified official substation hosting-capacity dataset across its 4 TSOs and ~865 electricity DSOs (inferred by subtracting 4 TSOs from 869 active Stromnetzbetreiber in BNetzA's MaStR). At TSO level, 50Hertz exposes a JSON API for 75 substations with available, reserved and requested MW for generation and demand (and an undocumented-unit constructionCostContribution field); TransnetBW embeds 52 substations in HTML with MW bands; Amprion and TenneT list 10 substations each without available MW. At DSO level, Geoportal Saarland maps renewable feed-in MW for 6 DSOs and E.ON DSOs publish qualitative maps (HTTP 403). Only paid aggregator GridChecker (844 sites) keeps history.

Confidence reason: All 4 TSOs, BNetzA, SMARD, VNBdigital (814 DSOs), EnWG/BMWE's 2026 Netzanschlusspaket bill, Capacitypedia, Saarland Geoportal, 18 major DSOs and GridChecker were directly inspected.

Main caveats

  • No single national official portal exists: publication is split across 4 TSOs and ~865 electricity DSOs, and VNBdigital (814 DSOs) only hosts territories, Netzausbaupläne and portal links.
  • Since 1 April 2026 all 4 TSOs allocate large load and battery connections via cyclic Reifegradverfahren (first-ready, first-served); published MW values are non-binding maximum indications.
  • Only 50Hertz (exact MW) and TransnetBW (MW bands for 2032/2035) publish node-level generation and demand MW at TSO level; Amprion and TenneT publish bay/site availability without MW.
  • Saarland Geoportal and Netze BW's Mittelspannungsauskunft cover generation feed-in only; urban DSO Repartition/Fair-Grid notices (Hamburg, Düsseldorf, Berlin) give only grid-wide MVA totals.
  • 50Hertz's JSON API sets available*CapacityMw to null (not 0) on 60/75 unavailable nodes and duplicates Eulenberg (EUB) and Eulenberg (EUB) Neubau with identical 1,800/2,000 MW values.
  • Official portals overwrite snapshots in place with no history; TenneT and E.ON DSO portals (Bayernwerk, E.DIS, SH Netz, Westnetz) block non-browser HTTP clients via Cloudflare HTTP 403.

Universe of 865 German electricity DSOs is inferred by subtracting the 4 balancing-zone TSOs from the 869 active Stromnetzbetreiber returned live by BNetzA's MaStR (consistent with BMWE's Aug 2026 Netzanschlusspaket draft bill citing 866 DSOs on p. 41 / 851 on p. 25, and VNBdigital's 814 DSO profiles). 18 major regional and urban DSOs were tested directly here (plus 6 Saarland/Pfalz DSOs covered in de-saarland-geoportal-umspannanlagen).

Look up the local DSO on https://www.vnbdigital.de, then search its website for 'Kapazitätskarte', 'Online-Netzauskunft', 'Mittelspannungsauskunft', 'SNAP', 'Zuteilungsverfahren' or 'Repartierungsverfahren'.

Caveats

  • Open MV self-service tools return single-coordinate feasibility up to 10 MW, not bulk substation tables.
  • E.ON DSO portals (*.zuteilungsverfahren.de) and Westnetz reject non-browser HTTP requests with HTTP 403.
  • Urban Repartition/Fair-Grid portals (Hamburg, Düsseldorf, ENERVIE, Berlin) give only grid-wide MVA totals or suspended publication.

Document access

Where a tested DSO publishes no substation capacity map or a TSO omits numerical MW (Amprion, TenneT), look up the responsible DSO via VNBdigital and extract substation expansion deltas (+/- MVA) and bottleneck flags from its biennial § 14d EnWG Netzausbauplan PDF and annual Repartition/Fair-Grid capacity notices, or submit a formal Netzanschlussbegehren.
