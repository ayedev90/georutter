Germany publishes no open nationwide list of grid connection requests; applications (Netzanschlussbegehren) are tracked bilaterally per applicant. Under § 9 KraftNAV, >=110 kV operators keep a joint Kraftwerksanschluss-Register (via BDEW) for >=100 MW generation (excluding storage since 2025-12-23), restricted to eligible applicants and authorities; only TenneT posted an open 2020-02-18 PDF (93 entries: 8 requests, 8 commitments) lacking request dates. Current 2025-2026 releases (BNetzA/SMARD battery survey, 50Hertz 75-node API) give only aggregates where DSO respondent counts, net/gross MW basis and bidirectional storage double-counting must be inferred.

Confidence reason: Verified directly in EnWG, KraftNAV (incl. 2025-12-23 amendment), MaStRV, BT-Drs. 21/7866, EU 2024/1747 & 2024/1711, BNetzA/SMARD, all 4 TSOs, VNBdigital, GovData, GridChecker, and 10 sampled DSOs.

Main caveats

  • No open request queue exists: under EnWG § 17, EU Reg. 2019/943 Art. 50(4a) and BT-Drs. 21/7866 (§ 17d EnWG-E), operators report request status bilaterally to applicants, not in a public register.
  • § 9 KraftNAV covers only generation >=100 MW at >=110 kV for eligible applicants/regulators, and since 2025-12-23 (BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 368) expressly excludes battery storage, where most new queue volume sits.
  • SMARD counted 18,158 new battery requests in 2025 (573.5 GW, including duplicates by project). Of these, 1,468 received offers in 2025 (33.9 GW). The 1,937 offers issued in 2025 (54.2 GW) also include older requests.
  • BNetzA/SMARD omits the DSO respondent count and net/gross MW basis; cross-checking Monitoringbericht 2026 p. 96 implies 4 TSOs and up to 813 of 851 DSOs reported in 2025, not all 842 DSOs registered in 2026.
  • 50Hertz's live 75-node API sums requestedGenerationCapacityMw (59.8 GW) and requestedLoadCapacityMw (67.7 GW); both match at 40 nodes, implying bidirectional storage is counted in both (summing to 128 GW double-counts).

Members summary
Total: 846 · Listed: 14 · Tested: 14 · Data found: 1

Universe comprises all 4 control-area TSOs and 842 electricity DSOs registered with BNetzA in 2026 (Monitoringbericht 2026, Table 21, p. 97; only operators of >=110 kV networks fall under KraftNAV § 2 Nr. 3). All 4 TSOs and 10 major/regional DSOs across Germany were tested; only TenneT exposed an open (2020) § 9 register PDF, while none published a current itemized request list.

On operator portals (discoverable via vnbdigital.de), check 'Netzanschluss' -> 'Kraftwerk ab 100 MW' or 'Veröffentlichungspflichten' -> 'KraftNAV'; public pages normally contain only § 3 Abs. 1 forms, model contracts, and schematic maps, while request submission/tracking sits behind digital 'Einspeiseportal' / 'Netzanschlussportal' logins.

Caveats

  • Not open to the general public (§ 9 Satz 3 KraftNAV) and excludes all energy storage since 2025-12-23, demand, <100 MW plants, and <110 kV grids.
  • Operator KraftNAV webpages publish § 3 forms and schematic maps rather than the § 9 register itself.

Document access

Because current per-request queues are confidential, individual connection requests and commitments can only be reconstructed piecemeal from BNetzA § 31 EnWG dispute decisions, municipal Bebauungsplan / UVP permitting dossiers near substations, and Bundestag parliamentary inquiries (for regional/policy aggregates).
