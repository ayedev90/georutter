Germany's official Energy Efficiency Register for Data Centres (RZReg, rechenzentrumsregister.bund.de) exports 323 operating facilities >=300 kW with operator, 5-digit postal code, IT load in kW (18 'n.v.'), and floor area in m². However, § 13 EnEfG allows external disclosure: 163 of 486 DCs in BAFA's 2025 statistics (and major international operators) omit RZReg publication, vs 2,980 MW estimated nationwide (Bitkom 2025). Combine RZReg with PeeringDB (328 DE sites, 321 with coordinates, no MW/m²), OpenStreetMap (273 telecom=data_center objects), and operator/BImSchG/PfA records.

Confidence reason: EnEfG § 13 law, BAFA/BfEE technical guides, RZReg XLSX/JSON (323 public; 486 in 2025 stats), UBA evaluation, PeeringDB DE API (328), OSM Taginfo/Geofabrik, operator pages, and Baxtel all directly verified.

Main caveats

  • RZReg exports 323 public DCs (593.38 MW IT load): 163 of 486 DCs in BAFA's 2025 statistics omit RZReg publication (including Equinix, Digital Realty, AWS, Google, Microsoft), vs 2,980 MW national IT capacity (Bitkom 2025).
  • RZReg exports 5-digit postal codes only (no street address or coordinates) and lists campus buildings separately, whereas PeeringDB often collapses multi-building campuses (e.g., Digital Realty FRA1-27) into one record.
  • RZReg loads are in kW (divide by 1,000 for MW): 18/323 rows report 'n.v.' for IT kW, 21 rows have IT kW >= non-redundant total load (UBA flagged a 12% error rate), and Vantage BER12 lists 1 kW on 12,080 m².
  • The EU European Database on Data Centres legally keeps facility-level records confidential (Reg. (EU) 2024/1364 Art. 5(5)) and publishes only Member State and EU aggregates.
  • BImSchG/UVP environmental permits show diesel backup thermal capacity (MWth FWL) rather than IT MW and cover both planned and operating sites; commercial trackers either block bots (HTTP 429) or sell site MW behind paid requests.
