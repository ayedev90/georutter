Confidence
Medium

ATKIS provides open vectors for high-voltage overhead lines. BNetzA’s project layers cover transmission expansion sections with voltage, operator and status. JAO’s Core static grid model gives circuit voltage and operator without geometry. OpenStreetMap adds some distribution routes and cables, with variable completeness. VDE FNN serves generalised 380 and 220 kV routes. No complete open distribution dataset was verified.

Confidence reason: All listed sources opened and queried (national OSM, DLM250, three Länder, JAO, BNetzA); DSO publication only sampled (WEMAG NAP, Bayernwerk blocked) and GDI-DE catalogue unreachable.

Not published: No official lifecycle field for the existing grid (ATKIS maps existing lines only); DSO planned measures only in NAP spreadsheets without geometry and UVP-Verbund notices.

Main caveats

  • The ATKIS sources checked focus on overhead lines, with a documented capture threshold of 110 kV. Voltage, operator and status were unavailable in the samples. These sources do not provide a distribution cable inventory.
  • BNetzA layers cover only BBPlG/EnLAG expansion sections; 46 of 157 BBPlG features are 'Luftlinie' or 'Korridorverlauf', not routes.
  • VDE FNN geometry is generalised (about one vertex per 4.4 km), has no operator field and states no licence.
  • JAO/TSO static grid models list circuits by substation pair with TSO and kV but no geometry; the bundled HTML map draws straight lines.
  • TenneT's WMS returns images only; its 2018 terms require written permission to store, digitise or republish the data.

Document access

Routes of planned or approved 110 kV–525 kV lines and DSO plans appear in approval documents (Lagepläne 1:2000, Masttabellen) and §14d network plans; these give operator, voltage and status.
