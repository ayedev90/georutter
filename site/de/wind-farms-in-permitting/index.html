No single official dataset covers all wind permitting stages in Germany. The Bundesnetzagentur Marktstammdatenregister (MaStR) lists 8,808 pre-operational wind turbine units in status 'In Planung' (8,720 onshore, 88 offshore; 55.2 GW in total). Of these, 8,571 units have a permit identifier, with 4,843 distinct identifiers. Under § 5(4) MaStRV, registration is mandatory once a required federal permit is granted. Pending applications ('im Genehmigungsverfahren') are published as structured turbine data by 6 of 16 Länder (NRW, BB, SH, HE, northern RLP, SL; 3,249 pending turbines), mostly without developer names. EIA portals publish notices for larger cases.

Confidence reason: Verified against MaStRV statutory text, live queries fetching all 8,808 MaStR 'In Planung' wind units, bulk XML schemas, all 16 Länder portals/WFS, UVP portals, BSH/EMODnet offshore WFS, BNetzA awards and Overpass.

Main caveats

  • Under § 5(4)-(5) MaStRV, wind units enter MaStR as 'In Planung' only within 1 month after BImSchG/WindSeeG permit notification; unapproved applications ('im Genehmigungsverfahren') are absent unless registered voluntarily.
  • MaStR counts turbines. Of 8,808 units in 'In Planung', 8,571 have a GenehmigungsMastrNummer (4,843 distinct identifiers). The other 237 lack that field. WindparkName is free text (2,833 distinct state/name pairs).
  • Capacity units vary across official sources: MaStR, NRW, SH, TH, RLP and BNetzA report kW (e.g. 5700 = 5.7 MW), whereas Brandenburg, Hessen, Saarland, Baden-Württemberg and EMODnet report MW.
  • No official onshore register separates 'authorised' from 'under construction' ('im Bau'); refusals, withdrawals and § 16b BImSchG repowering are structured only in NRW GenMon, Hessen (gvs.csv/klagen.csv) and RLP SGD Nord.
  • UVP-Verbund and UVP-Portal cover only EIA-mandatory (>=20 WEA or offshore) and EIA-screened (>=3 WEA) cases as HTML/PDF notices, missing 1-2 turbine simplified § 19 BImSchG applications without UVP screening.

All 16 German Bundesländer were checked via GDI-DE CSW, GovData and state portals/WFS: 8 publish structured pre-operational wind turbine data (6 including pending 'im Verfahren' applications; 2 only granted/Vorbescheid) and 8 do not expose open structured permitting lists.

Search GDI-DE (gdk.gdi-de.org) or each Land's Geoportal/OpenData portal for 'Windenergieanlagen' or 'Windkraftanlagen' + 'LIS-A' / '4. BImSchV' / 'Genehmigungsverfahren'.
