Germany’s Bundesnetzagentur publishes the Kraftwerksliste for units at sites of at least 10 MW, with fuel, gross and net capacity, operator and status. SMARD provides site coordinates and publishes annual net generation for units of at least 100 MW. MaStR is the underlying unit register. No nuclear unit remains in operation.

Confidence reason: Kraftwerksliste, SMARD metadata, MaStR export and JSON, UBA list and Energy-Charts files were downloaded and counted; SMARD unit series match Energy-Charts within 0.5%; the ENTSO-E API (token) was not queried.

Not published: No per-unit annual generation found for units < 100 MW: MaStR has no generation field, Destatis publishes only totals by energy source, SMARD/ENTSO-E stop at 100 MW.

Main caveats

  • Do not count reserves as operation: 1,488 thermal units (incl. biomass) 'InBetrieb', 57,399 MW net, vs about 14.7 GW in grid/capacity reserve, KVBG phase-out or temporary closure; MaStR shows such units as 'In Betrieb'.
  • Kraftwerksliste reports the Bundesland without coordinates. In the MaStR export tested, coordinates, municipality and postcode were absent for sampled units of at least 10 MW. SMARD provides one point per site.
  • SMARD reports annual MWh from 100 MW. ENTSO-E reports average MW per interval and requires a token. This review found no per-unit generation figures for smaller CHP and industrial units.
  • Bases differ: Kraftwerksliste gross and net MW, MaStR kW, UBA gross MW, ENTSO-E net MW; LEAG plant totals run 3.5-4.6% above ENTSO-E-based sums without stating gross or net.
  • All 9 nuclear rows in the Kraftwerksliste are 'endgültig stillgelegt' (2015-2023); historical nuclear generation per unit is in Energy-Charts nuclear_unit files.
