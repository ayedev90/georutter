Germany electrical substations data sources

Where can I get the locations of electrical substations in Germany (transmission and distribution), with voltage level and operator?

Answer

OSM is the broadest tested national source for transmission and distribution locations, but only 16.25% of mapped substations have voltage and 33.49% have operator. Official national Basis-DLM geometry exists behind a paid licence, with free regional services, but its inclusion rule misses much distribution and it lacks these electrical attributes. JAO supplies official selected transmission names, voltage and asset owners; VDE and TSO maps supply geometry. No tested source answers the whole question completely.

Main caveats

  • Basis-DLM is a national official dataset, not a complete all-voltage registry: its substation land-use class follows recorded overhead lines and has no voltage/operator fields.
  • OSM counts are mapped objects, not a completeness estimate: 90,928/108,567 lack voltage and 72,208 lack operator; nodes, areas and multi-site relations need careful interpretation.
  • VDE is an association publication, not official operator data; it mixes existing and planned sites, has no voltage column and leaves Eigentum blank on 473/557 points.
  • JAO workbook has no coordinates. Its HTML substation markers are unlabelled; a transformer or line owner is not automatically the operator of every asset at a co-located station.
  • The research exceeded the 80-opening limit (117 reconstructed requests); remaining leads are explicitly unchecked. Distribution completeness and full official attribute coverage remain unresolved.
