Germany gas pipelines data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of natural gas transmission and distribution pipelines in Germany, with operator, diameter or pressure, and status?

Answer

Germany publishes no open national GIS dataset of gas transmission or distribution pipeline routes: § 15a (4) EnWG restricts the KO·NEP NEP-Gas-Datenbank to confidential use; § 23c (4)&(6) EnWG require only aggregate length tables and municipal presence maps; BIL requires project-specific excavation inquiries; and gasnetzkarte.de closed in 2011. Best open transmission option is SciGRID_gas IGGIELGN (1,087 DE 2-vertex segments, 31,272 km, DN & bar gap-filled, CC-BY-4.0) paired with LKD_Raw (1,037/1,045 operator_name, 2017) plus OSM/Geofabrik for exact mapped paths. Official open multi-vertex WFS geometries exist only in Hessen (no DN/bar/operator); full distribution+TSO GIS requires paid WGI.

Local search terms

  • Fernleitungsnetz / Fernleitungsnetzbetreiber (FNB) — High-pressure long-distance gas transmission network and its 16 operators in Germany
  • Verteilernetz / Verteilernetzbetreiber (VNB) — Regional and local gas distribution grid and operator (~740+ companies in Germany)
  • Netzentwicklungsplan Gas und Wasserstoff (NEP) — Biennial § 15a EnWG national transmission development plan (KO·NEP / BNetzA)
  • Leitungsauskunft / Planauskunft — Site-specific underground utility location inquiry required before excavation (e.g. BIL eG)
  • Planfeststellungsbeschluss (PFB) — Formal § 43 EnWG planning approval decree publishing approved corridor plans and technical specs
  • Nennweite (DN) / Maximaler Betriebsdruck (MOP) — Nominal internal pipeline diameter in mm (classed A–G under § 23c EnWG) and maximum operating pressure in bar

Main caveats

  • No open official national GIS: § 15a (4) EnWG restricts NEP-Gas-Datenbank (confidentiality/legitimate interest), BIL handles excavation inquiries only, and gasnetzkarte.de shut down on 2011-01-01.
  • SciGRID_gas IGGIELGN & LKD_Raw cover transmission only (0 distribution), freeze at 2017–2021 snapshots, and store 100% of German pipes as 2-vertex straight lines between abstracted nodes (0/1,087 have >2 vertices).
  • IGGIELGN stripped operator_name before release (0/1,087) and gap-fills 43% of diameter_mm and 47.5% of max_pressure_bar with Median/Lasso heuristics; use LKD_Raw (1,037/1,045 raw operator_name, 2017) for operators.
  • Operational status is missing in SciGRID (start_year only on 25/1,087 IGGIELGN) and sparse in OSM; official Hessen WFS distinguishes Bestand vs Planung regionally without DN, bar, or operator.
  • Distribution networks (~740+ DSOs) publish § 23c (6) Nr. 4 EnWG municipal presence maps only; parcel-level pipe maps require signed Nutzungsantrag/login (NBB, enercity, BIL) or commercial WGI (80,924 km).
