Confidence

No nationwide public register of active mines and quarries was found. Hessen, Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, Brandenburg and some Lower Rhine districts publish site records with varying attributes and status definitions. Hessen’s 276 records include a planned site and provide operators and output classes. No annual production figures per site were verified.

Confidence reason: Each Land checked with services queried; MV and Sachsen-Anhalt could not be inspected, city-states not checked, and GEM/OSM could not be tested from this environment.

Main caveats

  • 'Active' differs by source: BW 'in Betrieb' layer includes 'Abbau ruht' and 'in Herrichtung'; Saxony MR.Mine 'operating' covers closure plans (Abschlussbetriebsplan); BY status is from the last aerial photo.
  • Mining-authority layers cover only BBergG sites; most gravel, sand, limestone and natural-stone quarries fall under Land/district permits and appear only in geological-survey or district layers.
  • The sources checked did not provide actual annual production per site. Hessen provides output classes without a reference year. USGS capacity and planned extraction in permits describe different measures.
  • BGR KOR250 'Abbaustelle' points are from map sheets of 1989-2024 and often mark the company seat; BSK1000 still shows mines closed by 2018.
  • Coverage is uneven: structured site layers were found for 5 of 16 Länder plus 3 NRW districts; national aggregates (BGR, SdK) are not site lists.

laender_extraction_sitesLänder extraction-site inventories (Rohstoffgewinnungsstellen / Abbaustellen)

State geological surveys (LGRB BW, LfU Bayern, HLNUG Hessen, others) and NRW district permit authorities via KRZN · Official · ArcGIS REST JSON/GeoJSON (HE), OGC WFS GML (BW, KRZN), GeoPackage (BY), WMS (BY) · API · License: Varies: CC BY 4.0 (BY), Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero 2.0 (KRZN), © HLNUG with no licence stated (HE), LGRB AGB (BW)

Regional extraction-site layers include Hessen (276 records, including a planned site), Bavaria (1,299 records) and Baden-Württemberg (545 records). The status categories differ by dataset.

Members summary

16 Länder; 13 listed (Brandenburg covered by mining_authority_registers; Berlin, Hamburg, Bremen not checked). Structured active-site data found in 4 (BW, BY, HE, part of NRW); 2 could not be inspected (ST blocked, MV not responding).

Look on the Land geological survey's geodata page for 'Rohstoffgewinnungsstellen', 'Abbaustellen' or 'Gewinnungsstellen' layers, or in district (Kreis) 'Abgrabungen' services; GovData search for these terms finds some.

See member links and checks in the detail (laender_extraction_sites)

Caveats

  • Each Land defines 'active' differently (BW includes paused sites).
  • Operator only in Hessen and NRW districts; production only as Hessen output classes.

mining_authority_registersMining-authority (Bergbehörde) layers of establishments and operating plans under the Federal Mining Act

Mining authorities of the Länder (e.g. LBGR Brandenburg, Sächsisches Oberbergamt) · Official · OGC WFS 2.0 GML (Brandenburg), WMS with GetFeatureInfo (Sachsen) · API · License: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung 2.0 (Brandenburg, Sachsen)

Brandenburg's LBGR WFS lists 241 mining establishments with operator and mineral, joinable to valid main operating plans (129); Sachsen publishes mine-plan polygons via WMS.

One mining authority per Land (some shared: LBEG also for HH, HB, SH; LBGR also for Berlin). 7 listed and tested; establishments with operator found in Brandenburg, mine status without operator in Sachsen; others publish rights or overlap layers.

Search the Land mining authority's INSPIRE 'Bergbau' or 'Mineralische Bodenschätze' services for Betriebsstätten or Betriebsplan layers; Berechtsamskarten show rights only.

See member links and checks in the detail (mining_authority_registers)

Caveats

  • 'operating' in INSPIRE MR.Mine includes closure plans.
  • Excludes most quarries permitted under non-mining law.

Example request

https://inspire.brandenburg.de/services/bergbau_wfs?SERVICE=WFS&VERSION=2.0.0&REQUEST=GetFeature&TYPENAMES=app:bgbetriebsst&COUNT=2

Document access

Per-site production and, outside a few Länder, operator and location must be taken from documents: mining-authority annual reports, BGR commodity studies, operator websites and permit files.
