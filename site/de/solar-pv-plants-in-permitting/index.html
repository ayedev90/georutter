Germany has no complete national register of solar PV projects in permitting. Bundesnetzagentur's Marktstammdatenregister (MaStR) publishes 63,257 solar units in status 'In Planung' (1,094 ground-mounted), with kWp/kW capacity and WGS84 coordinates above 30 kW, but pre-commissioning registration is voluntary for solar PV under § 5(4) MaStRV and merges planning, permitting and construction into one status. Bundesnetzagentur's EEG tender XLSX files add awarded kW, bidder, parcel location and award date for subsidised projects. Binding zoning stages (Aufstellungsbeschluss, Auslegung, Satzungsbeschluss) sit as PDFs across 16 Länder Bauleitplanung portals and ~11,000 municipalities.

Confidence reason: Statutory inclusion/privacy rules (§§ 5, 15 MaStRV; §§ 35, 37 EEG; § 10a BauGB), live MaStR API/bulk exports, BNetzA tender XLSX files, all 16 Länder Bauleitplanung portals, UVP-Verbund, GEM and OSM were directly tested.

Main caveats

  • Under § 5(4) MaStRV, pre-commissioning MaStR registration ('In Planung') is mandatory only for federal-law permits (e.g. BImSchG, WHG); standard solar PV is registered voluntarily until 1 month after commissioning.
  • MaStR has only one pre-commissioning status ('In Planung', code 31), lumping early concepts together with projects that already hold a Satzungsbeschluss, Baugenehmigung or are actively under construction ('Im Bau').
  • Large solar parks are often split across multiple MaStR units (e.g. PVA Schafhöfen Anlagenteil 1 at 17.6 MWp and Anlagenteil 2 at 200 MWp vs 268 MWp grid reservation in the municipal Bebauungsplan).
  • MaStR redacts coordinates, street and Flurstück for solar units <=30 kW (58,539 of 63,257 planned units) and masks natural-person operators under § 15(1) MaStRV.
  • BNetzA EEG tender lists cover only awarded subsidised bids (excluding subsidy-free PPA parks and pre-tender projects), omit coordinates, and split multi-Gemarkung bids across rows in Zuschläge_Detailliert.
  • Privileged ground-mounted PV within 200 m of motorways or double-track railways (§ 35(1) Nr. 8b BauGB) and rooftop PV skip Bebauungspläne; county Baugenehmigungen are not publicly listed.

Total: 16 · Listed: 16 · Tested: 16 · Data found: 16

All 16 German Bundesländer central Bauleitplanung / GDI portals were opened and verified on 2026-11-25; none publishes a standalone structured solar-PV-in-permitting table (all expose map viewers, XPlanung/WMS plan boundaries or links to municipal procedure pages).

Search '<Bundesland> zentrales Internetportal Bauleitplanung' or query https://portal.diplanung.de/, then follow municipal links to 'Bauleitpläne im Verfahren' / 'Öffentliche Auslegung' for 'Sondergebiet Photovoltaik'.

Caveats

  • Web viewers and municipal link directories only; no downloadable structured list of solar PV projects or MW capacities.
  • Covers non-privileged ground-mounted PV requiring a Bebauungsplan; excludes § 35(1) Nr. 8b BauGB corridor permits and rooftop PV.
  • Tracks zoning up to Satzungsbeschluss, not subsequent county Baugenehmigung or construction start.

Document access

Because MaStR 'In Planung' is voluntary and merges all pre-commissioning phases while BNetzA lists only EEG tender winners, exact permitting stages (Aufstellungsbeschluss, § 3(2) Auslegung, § 10 Satzungsbeschluss), council decision dates, Vorhabenträger and grid-reservation MWp for non-privileged ground-mounted solar parks must be extracted from municipal Bebauungsplan PDFs via the 16 Länder Bauleitplanung portals and municipal websites.
