Germany has separate transmission and distribution plans. BNetzA's Monitoring_gdb FeatureServer maps 183 BBPlG/EnLAG transmission sections (Luftlinie, Korridorverlauf, Trassenverlauf). Project sheets and NEP 2037/2045 PDFs add commissioning dates and 871 substation measures. BSH's FEP 2025 WFS/GeoJSON maps offshore platforms and cables. For distribution, 95 of 814 VNBdigital operators have an active plan service. The 2026 XLSX examples checked lack coordinates.

Confidence reason: Directly queried BNetzA ArcGIS FeatureServers, BSH FEP 2025 GeoJSON/WFS, VNBdigital GraphQL and 2026 DSO XLSX tables, NEP 2037/2045 PDFs, ENTSO-E TYNDP 2026 JSON API, OpenStreetMap Overpass, and EnWG §§ 12b, 14d–14e statutory texts.

Main caveats

  • BNetzA and NEP cover transmission. 95 of 814 VNBdigital listings have an active distribution plan service. The exemption for operators below 100,000 customers has an exception for qualifying curtailment under § 14d(8) EnWG.
  • Substations lack unified official GIS: BNetzA maps 205 unattributed nodes (Vorhabenpunkte) and embeds HVDC converters in line sheets, while NEP's 871 substation Punktmaßnahmen and DSO Umspannwerke are published without coordinates.
  • Corridor vs route geometry: BNetzA's Monitoring_gdb mixes straight-line node pairs (Luftlinie, 34), federal corridors (Korridorverlauf, 13) and concrete/approved routes (Trassenverlauf, 136); always filter by Typ and Vorhabenst.
  • Missing dates and voltages in GIS: BNetzA Monitoring_gdb omits commissioning dates (shown on netzausbau.de HTML sheets and NEP PDFs) and sets Spannung='unbekannt' on 23/157 BBPlG features; BSH FEP GeoJSON omits voltage and year.
  • VNBdigital 2026 XLSX traps: DSO spreadsheets lack coordinates, encode start/commissioning months as Excel serial integers, report voltage tiers (HS, UW HS auf MS, MS, NS) not kV, and some DSOs (e.g. Netze BW) anonymize asset names.

95 of 814 listed electricity DSOs have an active Netzausbauplan ('nap') service on VNBdigital. Ten major regional or urban DSOs across all six planning regions were tested directly. The active count does not establish that every operator made an XLSX file available.

Query vnb_gridExpansionDocumentsExternal(vnbId) on https://www.vnbdigital.de/gateway/graphql or open https://www.vnbdigital.de/vnb/<vnbId> to download NAP_<id>_2026_Entwurf.pdf and NAP_Massnahmen_<id>_2026_Entwurf.xlsx.

Caveats

  • No geographic coordinates are published (substation/line names or anonymized regional labels only; grid maps are static images in PDFs without existing-grid topology).
  • Exempts DSOs under 100,000 customers unless renewable curtailment exceeded 3% (§ 14d Abs. 8 EnWG); XLSX dates are Excel serial numbers and include completed/cancelled rows.

Example request

curl -sL -X POST 'https://www.vnbdigital.de/gateway/graphql' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"query":"query { vnb_gridExpansionDocumentsExternal(vnbId: \"1068\") { uploadDate url metadata { filename version } } }"}'
