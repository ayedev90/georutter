Spain network tariffs data sources

Where can I get the current electricity network access tariffs and charges in Spain, by voltage level and period, with their history?

Answer

Peajes (network access tariffs) are set yearly by CNMC resolution and cargos (system charges) by MITERD order, both in BOE, with 2.0TD to 6.4TD by period P1-P6. Current 2026 values: MITECO peajes page (both) and BOE-A-2025-26348 (peajes), BOE-A-2025-26705 (cargos). History since 2021-06 is the chain of BOE resolutions/orders; 2014-2021 frozen old-structure orders. No CSV/API of the series was found.

Local search terms

  • peajes de acceso (a las redes de transporte y distribucion) — Network access tariffs set by CNMC
  • cargos del sistema electrico — Charges for other system costs (renewable support, deficit, capacity) set by the Ministry
  • peajes T&D, TD — Transport and distribution tariff groups 2.0TD to 6.4TD
  • segmento tarifario de cargos — Cargo segments 1-6 matching the peaje groups
  • termino de potencia / termino de energia — Fixed power term (EUR/kW year) and energy term (EUR/kWh)
  • periodo P1-P6 (punta, llano, valle) — Tariff periods by season, day type and hour
  • TDVE — Peajes for public electric vehicle charging points
  • exceso de potencia — Excess over contracted power, billed with its own price

Main caveats

  • Peajes (CNMC, networks) and cargos (Ministry, other system costs) are separate documents; a full regulated price needs both. Do not add transport and distribution parts on top of the T&D line.
  • No official CSV, API or time series exists in what was found: values sit in BOE text tables and an HTML page. History needs one BOE document per year; BOE legal status flags do not show supersession.
  • MITECO page shows 2,051434 for cargos segment 3 power P3-P5; the BOE order text says 2,054134. Take the BOE as the legal text.
  • Periods P1-P6 depend on season, day type and territory (Peninsula, Balearics, Canary Islands, Ceuta, Melilla); 2.0TD has 3 energy periods and 2 power periods. Hours are in Circular 3/2020 art. 7, not in the price resolutions.
  • 2025 has two peajes documents: Res. 4-12-2024 (from 2025-01-01) and Res. 6-3-2025 replacing only Annex II (excess power) from 2025-04-01. Cargos 2024 order was published 2024-02-14.
