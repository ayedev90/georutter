Spain energy auctions data sources

Where can I get the results of renewable and storage auctions in Spain, with awarded projects, capacity (MW), price, technology and location?

Español

Answer

Spain has held four REER (renewable) price auctions: 26-01-2021, 19-10-2021, 25-10-2022 and 22-11-2022 (none since). Each is resolved in a BOE resolution (PDF annex: awardee, CIF, technology, offer-unit code, EUR/MWh, kW), mirrored on MITECO. No storage auction has been held yet; no project location is published.

Main caveats

  • Latest REER auction is 22-11-2022; MITECO's REER page lists none after it. A new auction or the first capacity-market auction (storage eligible) would add a BOE resolution not yet in this card.
  • Annex units: kW, dot as thousands separator (10 = 10 kW, not 10 MW). Sum of Jan 2021 annex = 3,034.178 MW; Oct 2021 = 3,123.77 MW; Oct 2022 = 177 MW; Nov 2022 = 45.5 MW.
  • Awarded capacity is not installed capacity: awardees later register installations; the BOE rows are bids per awardee (CIF + UA code), so no plant name or site can be read from them.
  • Storage: not auctioned in REER; MITECO/IDAE storage awards are grants (PINALM, 126 projects, 818.3 M EUR) and access-capacity tenders, which are not price auctions.
  • CNMC supervision reports black out bidder volumes ([CONFIDENCIAL]); only awarded totals and weighted prices are readable.
