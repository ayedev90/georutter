Spain airports data sources

Where can I get a list of airports in Spain, with location, operator and annual passengers, cargo and aircraft movements?

Answer

Aena publishes annual traffic for its airports; DGAC reports also include some non-Aena airports. ENAIRE AIP gives reference coordinates and airport administration. Eurostat supplies comparable airport traffic but excludes or omits some airports; no checked source covers every requested field for every airport.

Main caveats

  • Eurostat category labels are not observations: the previous query returned no data because schedule=TOT was invalid.
  • Eurostat and Aena give different Madrid 2025 totals; definitions and revisions require checking.
  • Aena tables separately rank each metric, and include foreign airports; join on airport, not row.
  • Smaller airports and different reporting scopes leave coverage gaps.
