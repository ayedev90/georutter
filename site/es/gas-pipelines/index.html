Spain gas pipelines data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of natural gas transmission and distribution pipelines in Spain, with operator, diameter or pressure, and status?

Answer

IGN specifies a national topographic gas pipeline line layer, but its files could not be inspected. Balearic and Andalusian authorities describe regional line layers; Enagás lists major transmission sections and diameters and pressures. GEM maps selected transmission lines with status, excluding distribution and sometimes approximating routes.

Main caveats

  • The national IGN and regional spatial files could not be inspected; their actual coverage and accuracy remain unverified.
  • An authorization or a planned route does not prove the pipeline is operating.
  • GEM routes may be approximated and exclude distribution; Enagás table lacks line geometry.
  • CNMC receives distribution route coordinates, but public access to the individual submissions was not verified.
