Spain wind farms data sources

Where can I get a list of wind farms in operation in Spain, with location, capacity (MW), number of turbines, operator and commissioning date?

Español

Answer

There is no tested single public source supplying all requested fields for every operating Spanish wind farm. Regional government datasets cover several major regions; Navarra includes turbine count and commissioning year. AEE publishes individual park records for all 17 communities, but its operating labels can be stale and its developer field is not necessarily the operator.

Main caveats

  • The AEE map labels Es Milà as operating although a 2026 report says dismantling is complete and generation ceased in 2023.
  • Promoter or applicant is not necessarily the current operator.
  • 229 of 305 tested Castilla y León AEE records share a location with another record; do not assume exact sites.
  • Regional records use different definitions; some have multiple rows per park or municipality.
  • Castilla y León CABEZA MESA reports 29,600 kW although 15 turbines × 1,800 kW gives 27,000 kW.
