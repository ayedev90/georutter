Spain data centers data sources

Where can I get a list of data centers in operation in Spain, with location, operator and capacity (IT power in MW or floor area)?

Español

Answer

No free national list of operating data centers with location, operator and capacity was found. The best free combination is PeeringDB (72 facilities, 69 with coordinates, no capacity) plus operator pages that give MW or m2 for their own sites (Templus, Merlin, Iron Mountain, Digital Realty). National totals come from SpainDC (439 MW IT, end 2025, about 97 sites of 50 kW or more) and Colliers (360 MW IT), which disagree. Site-level datasets are paid.

Main caveats

  • Totals disagree: SpainDC 439 MW IT (end 2025, 50 kW threshold, hyperscale partly estimated) vs Colliers 360 MW IT (Oct 2025-Mar 2026 snapshot). Do not add or mix them.
  • PeeringDB lists interconnection facilities, not all data centers, and has no power or area field; its AUP forbids passing the data on in bulk.
  • Operator pages mix operating and planned MW and use different units (Templus Total power, Merlin MW IT, Iron Mountain MW approved and planned, Digital Realty building size).
  • Official per-site data exists in the EU ReportENER database (sites of 500 kW IT or more) but is confidential; only aggregates are public.
  • Planned campuses (Aragon PIGA, Colliers pipeline) are large and widely reported; they are not in operation and must not be counted.
