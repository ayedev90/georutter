Spain desalination plants data sources

Where can I get a list of desalination plants in Spain, with location, capacity, technology, operator and status?

Answer

No official national list of desalination plants was found. The best single source is the EMODnet WFS (GWI DesalData, CC-BY): 1143 Spanish rows with municipality-level position, capacity m3/d, technology, owner and status, but a 2020-21 snapshot and 21% of rows pinned to Madrid. AEDyR 2024 tables give the 88 plants above 10,000 m3/d. Exact data from owners exist only for Baleares (8 IDAM), MCT and ACUAMED pages.

Main caveats

  • EMODnet rows are units or projects, not plants: 1143 rows for 159 location IDs, 129 duplicate keys, and 232 rows are wastewater-fed reuse plants, not seawater or brackish desalination.
  • Positions are municipality centroids; 235 of 1143 Spanish rows (20.6%) sit on the Madrid centroid because GWI coordinates were judged inaccurate. Do not measure distances from them.
  • Data are dated: EMODnet rows last updated up to 2021-03-27 (Aguilas 180,000 m3/d vs 210,000 in AEDyR 2024). Status values Presumed Online/Offline are unconfirmed.
  • Plant counts disagree: AEDyR more than 765 plants >100 m3/d, MITECO PDF more than 900 (undated), SINAC 2024 328 ETAP of type desaladora, EMODnet 159 locations.
  • AEDyR lists only plants above 10,000 m3/d (about 77% of national capacity by its own estimate) and carries no operator, technology, status or coordinates.
