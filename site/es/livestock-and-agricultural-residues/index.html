Spain livestock and agricultural residues data sources

Where can I get livestock numbers and agricultural residues by municipality in Spain, by species or crop and year?

Answer

INE publishes 2020 municipal livestock tables; Navarra also lists annual municipal census workbooks, including 2025. Direct workbook extraction could not be tested. Provincial reports estimate crop residues by municipality; national annual crop-specific residue coverage remains unverified.

Main caveats

  • 2020 livestock date is 30 September; results are not live animal registers.
  • Municipal census cells can be suppressed and records swapped for confidentiality.
  • Biomass estimates depend on assumptions and do not establish actual collected residue.
