Spain oil pipelines and refineries data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of oil product pipelines and a list of refineries in Spain, with location, operator, capacity and status?

Answer

Refineries: CORES publishes an xlsx with the 10 Spanish refineries by owner group and crude capacity (kt/y, to 2025); PRTR-España gives operator, point and status on each complex page. Pipelines: the IGN BTN has national oleoducto lines (vector tiles, GeoPackage), without owner or status; CNMC says Exolum owns the network (about 4 000 km). Pipeline capacity and in-service status are not published.

Main caveats

  • BTN oleoducto lines carry no owner, product (crude vs refined) or in-service flag; the whole-country GeoPackage from CNIG could not be fetched with curl, only the vector-tile service was tested.
  • CORES capacity is in thousand tonnes per year; Tenerife keeps 4 500 kt although it is not operational (CORES English note), so exclude it for operating capacity.
  • Concawe's Petronor point is 15.9 km off the PRTR point and its terms allow only personal non-commercial use; Climate TRACE's Cartagena point is 7.9 km off. PRTR and OSM are the reference.
  • Only Andalucía's regional WFS was found with named pipeline sections; the other regions were not checked individually.
  • Moeve's site blocks automated clients, so Moeve figures come from CORES and Concawe, not from the company.
