Spain protected species areas data sources

Where can I get the areas designated to protect sensitive species in Spain, such as bird protection zones for power lines or species recovery plan areas, as geometry, with species and legal effect?

Answer

MITECO publishes a national download (April 2026) of the bird protection zones for high-voltage lines (RD 1432/2008) as geometry, but one dissolved polygon per region, with no species or legal act. Species-level recovery-plan and critical areas, with species and legal act, exist only as regional services: Aragon is best (area critica, ambito de plan, BOA link); Andalucia, Murcia, Galicia and Navarra also publish them. No national layer of recovery areas found.

Main caveats

  • MITECO layer is dissolved: one multipolygon per region (Comunidad, Superficie in ha). It cannot say which species or act created a zone.
  • The zip named geojson contains two GeoPackage files, not GeoJSON; Canarias is a separate file in EPSG:32628, the rest EPSG:25830.
  • Recovery plan areas are designated by regions, not the State; coverage, species and legal fields differ per region and there is no national recovery-area layer.
  • Legal effect is mostly technical: RD 1432/2008 imposes line-design measures inside zonas de protección; área crítica effects come from Ley 42/2007 art. 59 and regional plans.
  • Madrid, Castilla-La Mancha, Cataluna and Extremadura: no layer found; Ceuta's zones are its ZEPAs; Melilla has none; La Rioja was blocked (HTTP 403 to automated clients). Pais Vasco's shapefile is lines, not zones.
