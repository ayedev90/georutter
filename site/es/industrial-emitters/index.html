Spain industrial emitters data sources

Where can I get a list of large industrial facilities in Spain, with location, sector, operator and annual emissions by pollutant, including CO2?

Answer

PRTR-España publishes an industrial complex inventory and annual pollutant releases, including CO2, with coordinates, activity and parent company on individual records. Full inventory and annual emissions ZIPs are linked, but could not be downloaded or joined in this environment; coverage is limited to the regulated scope. The EEA republishes harmonised European industrial data with a different scope. Climate TRACE offers modelled asset estimates, though Spanish records were not tested.

Main caveats

  • The industrial complex is a reporting unit; it can contain several installations.
  • PRTR scope follows specific activities and capacity thresholds, not all large facilities.
  • Individual records show kg/year, while aggregate tables use tonnes/year; do not treat missing pollutant records as zero.
  • Bulk files and the map service could not be queried from this environment; fields, joins, completeness and coordinate quality remain unverified.
  • Spanish national data since 2017 include validated positive emissions; European reporting uses pollutant thresholds.
