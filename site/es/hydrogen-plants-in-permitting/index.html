Aragón publishes cases in current public participation, and Castilla y León published a project list in 2025. Sondeva reports a commercial national hydrogen permitting index, with limited free results. Current status, electrolyser technology, capacity and national completeness need further checking.

Confidence reason: Research is in progress; current status and national coverage remain unverified.

Main caveats

  • An old administrative act does not establish current permitting status.
  • The national environmental search covers environmental procedures, not necessarily all permit types.
  • Hysencia capacity differs: 20 MW in the January 2026 Aragón act versus 35 MW on the developer page; verify the applicable configuration.
  • Castilla y León’s 2025 list includes projects later authorized; electrolysis is not specified for every entry.
  • Commercial project-level results were not checked; the stated national total is the provider’s count.
