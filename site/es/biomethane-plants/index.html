Spain biomethane plants data sources

Where can I get a list of biogas and biomethane plants in operation in Spain, with location, production capacity, feedstock, operator and whether they inject into the gas grid?

Answer

The Enagás GdO public API and Excel list registered renewable-gas facilities with gas type, status, capacity, energy source and production-point logistics, but registration is incomplete. Andalucía publishes separate geolocated biogas and biomethane layers; SEDIGAS lists grid-injecting biomethane plants in a January 2026 PDF. No checked source covers every operating plant and attribute.

Main caveats

  • GdO Alta is a registration state, not independent proof of current operation; the API also contains Baja and hydrogen.
  • Injection-point coordinates can differ from plant coordinates; one of 103 checked points has a suspicious longitude sign.
  • Andalusia biogas power in MW and biomethane annual production in GWh are different quantities.
  • SEDIGAS January 2026 table covers grid-injecting biomethane, not all biogas or later plants.
