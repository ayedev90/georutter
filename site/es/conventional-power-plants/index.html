Spain conventional power plants data sources

Where can I get a list of thermal and nuclear power plants in Spain, with location, fuel or technology, capacity (MW), operator, status and annual generation?

Answer

No single open Spanish dataset lists all thermal and nuclear power plants with electrical capacity (MW), operator, status, coordinates, and annual generation. MITECO's statutory registry (RAIPEE: REPROv2 and PRETOR) is gated by CAPTCHA/login, while REE's e-sios unit list requires an API token and hits WAF blocks. Best open options: combine EEA Industrial Reporting v16 (thermal >=50 MWth through 2024, coords, thermal MWth and fuel TJ, not GWh), MITECO/IAEA PRIS plus Foro Nuclear 2025 PDF (nuclear MW, operator, status, 2025 GWh), and ENTSO-E (>=100 MW, free registration) for thermal MWe and annual MWh, or GEM GIPT (request form, Sept 2026) for current >=20-30 MW plant lists.

Main caveats

  • MITECO RAIPEE (REPROv2/PRETOR) is CAPTCHA/login-gated, REE e-sios requires a token and hits Incapsula WAF, and Electra CSV drops technical fields.
  • EEA Industrial Reporting v16 (F5_2) and MITECO WFS report rated thermal input (MWth) and fuel input (TJ), NOT electrical MW or GWh, and mix industrial boilers with power plants.
  • Official MITECO/EEA reporting completeness gaps for Spain are explicitly flagged in EEA v16 metadata; MITECO WFS ei_gp_combustion is frozen at 2018-2019 (still marks closed coal plants functional).
  • ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (14.1.b, 16.1.a) covers electrical MW and actual generation per unit, but requires registration and excludes units below 100 MW.
  • Non-official WRI GPPD (2021, 2 coord errors in Spain, 0 reported ES generation) and JRC-PPDB-OPEN (2019, coords on 119/648 thermal/nuclear rows) are outdated.
