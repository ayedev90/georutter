Spain flood zones data sources

Where can I get flood hazard zones (geometry) in Spain, with return period or probability and the issuing authority?

Español

Answer

MITECO's SNCZI WFS (gis.miteco.gob.es/geoserver/agua/wfs) serves flood-zone polygons for T=10, 50, 100, 500 years for all 25 demarcaciones, with promoting authority (organismo) per feature, CC BY 4.0. Coverage is studied river reaches only (about 22-36% of main rivers), fluvial only. Producers (CHE, CHC, CHT, CHG, IDEIB, ACA, La Palma) add newer or extra layers. Coastal T100/T500 exists only as raster (WMS).

Main caveats

  • Absence of a polygon is not absence of hazard: SNCZI covers only studied reaches (T100: 33,3 % of main rivers; T500: 36,2 %).
  • Bulk shapefile downloads on gis.miteco.gob.es sit behind an altcha captcha; the WFS (CC BY 4.0) is the scriptable route. Full q500 is huge (200 features = 65 MB).
  • organismo is the promoter of the study, not necessarily the current issuer; spellings vary and dates can be empty. Producer services (CHT shapefiles, ACA WFS) lack an authority field; cycles 1 to 3 coexist (field ciclo).
  • Coastal T100 is described as 'probabilidad baja' by IDEE coverages, while Directive scenarios call T100 medium; treat the label with care.
  • Layer bbox property is lat,lon while GeoJSON geometry is lon,lat; WFS layer is EPSG:4258.
