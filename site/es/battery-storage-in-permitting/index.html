Spain battery storage in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of stand-alone battery storage (BESS) projects currently in permitting in Spain, with location, power (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

Español

Answer

No complete current national list was established. Vitreous Labs publishes a downloadable gazette-derived inventory with a stand-alone filter, but it omits some regional gazettes and a checked promoter is wrong. BOE and regional notices provide official project details and dated milestones; Catalonia describes a project viewer. A notice or old milestone alone does not prove today’s stage.

Main caveats

  • The non-official CSV mixes stand-alone, hybrid and unspecified configurations; filter and verify each project.
  • The CSV misidentifies the BESS Cuevas promoter as “modificación”; BOE names Soner Picasso SLU.
  • Regional gazettes are incomplete in the CSV; its location is a municipality, not a precise facility point.
  • MW of installed power, MVA of inverters, MWh of energy and requested grid access are distinct.
  • A notice’s publication date and grid-access request do not prove present administrative status.
