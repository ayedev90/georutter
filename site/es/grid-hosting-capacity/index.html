Spain grid hosting capacity data sources

Where can I get the available grid connection capacity per substation or node in Spain, for generation and for demand, with the latest value and its history?

Answer

CNMC publishes national monthly generation and demand capacity layers for transmission and distribution; its tested period is October 2026. REE provides newer transmission CSV files, while e-distribución and Curós publish partial historical archives. A complete national time series was not established.

Main caveats

  • Capacity is indicative and cannot be added across interdependent nodes.
  • Current dates differ: the CNMC period table showed October 2026 and the REE CSVs are dated 3 November 2026.
  • Distribution archive completeness and a unified nationwide time series have not been verified.
  • CNMC distribution capacity is published for substations above 1 kV; 0* may have a distinct meaning.
