Spain grid expansion plans data sources

Where can I get the planned new power lines and substations in Spain, with location, voltage, planned date and status?

Answer

No structured national file of planned lines and substations was found. The official lists are PDF tables with voltage and planned year: the approved Plan 2021-2026 (consolidated July 2026) and the initial 2025-2030 proposal (Annex I). Coordinates and a 3-state status exist only in Red Eléctrica's project map (385 substations, 77 lines, updated September 2024). Distribution-grid plans were not obtained.

Main caveats

  • Plan PDFs have no coordinates; match by substation name to the REE map points. The map lines are straight segments between two substations, not routes.
  • Planned years are best estimates; several are already past in a 2026 consolidation. The approved plan is 2021-2026; 2025-2030 is only a proposal under revision.
  • The revised 2030 proposal (September 2026: 193 substation parks, 6,706 km of lines) was announced but its documents were not found; Annex I here is the October 2025 version.
  • REE map status is dated September 2024; projects completed since may still show 'En proceso'.
  • Distribution-grid (below 220 kV, regional) planned assets were not obtained from any source.
