No public national list of drinking water treatment plants (ETAP) with location, capacity and operator exists. The national registry (SINAC, 2,625 ETAP in 2024) is restricted to professional users. Public pieces: EIEL (rows per treatment, not plant, small municipalities, no name or capacity), Andalucía DERA WFS (870 points) and Madrid WFS (14 plants with capacity, Canal de Isabel II), Navarra (2 plants), plus operator pages (MCT, Aigües de Barcelona).

Confidence reason: Negatives rest on opened ministry, IDE and open-data pages; SINAC content is unseen, OSM operator counts failed and several regional portals were only probed by catalogue search.

Main caveats

  • EIEL rows are potabilization treatments, not plants: in Asturias 2024, 1,106 of 1,111 rows are disinfection-only; ORGANISMO is the quality-control body, not the operator.
  • EIEL excludes municipalities of 50,000 or more and País Vasco and Navarra; in the 2024 download only 15 of 52 provinces have rows.
  • Capacity units differ or are missing (Madrid NM_CAPACIDAD is m3/day by inference, Navarra none, MCT m³/seg, Aigües de Barcelona l/s).
  • The Andalucía layer named ETAP includes 364 deposits and 333 chlorination points among 870 features; last update 2022-02-02.
  • Catalonia (more than 250 ETAP, three operators), Valencia, Galicia, Canarias and País Vasco have no ETAP layer found; regional portals were not each opened.
