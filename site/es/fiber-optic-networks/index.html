Spain fiber optic networks data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of fiber optic networks in Spain, with operator and status?

Answer

No national open dataset of fibre routes was found. Best official source: Catalonia CTTI cables (2,216 lines, exact geometry, owner CTTI, no status). ITU BBMaps WFS gives national but schematic 2-point links with status and no operator. Operators (Reintel, Digi, lyntia) publish pictures only; the state PIU is restricted to accredited operators.

Main caveats

  • Gencat coordinates are UTM metres (EPSG:25831 inferred) in a file called GeoJSON; reproject before use. All 2,216 rows have owner and manager CTTI.
  • ITU links are 2-vertex straight lines between nodes and the Spain bbox also returns Portugal, France and North Africa; they are not cable routes.
  • RD 330/2016 art. 5 obliges infrastructure owners to give location and layout on written request; the PIU (Orden ECE/529/2019) lists contacts and works only. The 2026 draft order is not approved.
  • OSM telecom lines in three regions sampled (La Rioja, Navarra, Cantabria) have no telecom:medium=fibre and almost no operator tags.
  • CNMC and SETID coverage data are municipal or province aggregates of homes passed, not routes.
