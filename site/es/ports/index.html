Spain ports data sources

Where can I get a list of commercial ports in Spain, with location, port authority and annual cargo, passengers and vessel calls?

Answer

For the 46 state-owned ports of general interest (28 Port Authorities), Puertos del Estado publishes everything asked, per port: an ArcGIS FeatureServer (port points, AP, cargo t, TEU, vessel calls, passengers, 2010-2024) and the Anuario 2025 XLSX (2025 data, coordinates in degrees-minutes). Regional (non-state) commercial ports have no national list; only Catalonia, Galicia and Canarias (passengers) were found, partially.

Local search terms

  • puerto de interes general — State-owned commercial port listed in annex I of the TRLPEMM (46)
  • Autoridad Portuaria (AP) — Public body managing one or more state ports (28)
  • puerto autonomico / de competencia autonomica — Port not of general interest, run by a region
  • escala — Ship call (statistics count calls, not unique ships)
  • trafico portuario — Tonnes of goods handled (loaded, unloaded, transit, transhipment)
  • zona de servicio / DEUP — Service zone; Delimitacion de Espacios y Usos Portuarios (ministerial order)
  • Anuario Estadistico / Memoria anual — Yearbook of Puertos del Estado / annual report of each AP
  • GT (arqueo bruto), TEU — Gross tonnage; 20-foot container equivalent

Main caveats

  • The ArcGIS traffic table stops at 2024; the Anuario 2025 XLSX (embedded in the PDF) has 2025. Monthly summaries exist per Port Authority, not per port.
  • Puertos del Estado data covers only the 46 state ports; commercial ports run by regions (e.g. Palamos, Garrucha, Ribadeo) are in separate regional sources.
  • Port coordinates are whole or decimal arc-minutes (about 1-2 km); Anuario sheet 2_1 has Arinaga at 25º24W (wrong, about 737 km offshore).
  • 'Vessels' are merchant ship calls, not unique ships; passengers are movements. Glossary says gross weight excludes tares, tables footnote says tares included.
  • No open licence: puertos.es aviso legal requires prior written authorisation for reproduction/exploitation; the ArcGIS item states no licence.
