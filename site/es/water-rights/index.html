Spain has a central administrative water database, the Base Central del Agua, for which information can be requested, and registers maintained by basin authorities. Júcar publishes a March 2023 set of PDFs with holder, use, volume and intake coordinates; Balearic groundwater files are catalogued as CSV and GeoJSON. Later entries and current status require verification; the national database and Balearic data could not be inspected.

Confidence reason: Primary administrative sources and a real listing were opened; complete national access and other basin registers remain untested.

Main caveats

  • The central database is a repository, not a verified public bulk download; information is obtainable on request.
  • Júcar PDFs omit subsequent entries, so an entry cannot establish current legal status.
  • Individual natural persons are anonymised in Júcar PDFs. A right may have multiple intakes; authorised volume is not measured abstraction.
  • Júcar says dates through 2014 use ED50, later ETRS89; some entries have missing coordinates or volume.
  • Balearic catalogue includes pending applications and historic entries; downloaded files and their fields were not inspected.
