No single source was found for operating PV plants in Spain. ELECTRA provides capacity and dates across technologies, but lacks technology, holder and municipality fields. REE gen_spain supplies PV points with MW and municipality. Castilla y León adds 1,044 PV features with municipality and power, 377 with start dates and 94 with holder. Repeated names prevent treating these features as a plant census. Operators and current operating status remain unverified. WRI is historical and mixes PV with concentrated solar power.

Confidence reason: Three inherited sources were retested and two added. The regional download supports feature-level observations. Plant counts, operators and current operating status remain unverified.

Main caveats

  • ELECTRA was still stamped 27/01/2026 on 2026-11-18, despite documented daily updates.
  • ELECTRA lacks technology, holder and municipality fields. Matching PV by name is uncertain and its kW unit is inferred.
  • Castilla y León has 1,044 PV features and 412 distinct recorded names. Deduplication and current operation are unverified.
  • A holder or owner does not establish operator identity. WRI mixes PV and concentrated solar power and does not establish current operation.
  • Preliminary registration is not proof of operation. REE alta/baja dates are undocumented, and community statuses are unknown or fuzzy-matched.
  • SOWISP and the Castilla y León XLSX were inaccessible. The REE point near (0,0) concerns a different layer that was not retested.
