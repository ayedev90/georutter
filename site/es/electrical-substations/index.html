Spain electrical substations data sources

Where can I get the locations of electrical substations in Spain (transmission and distribution), with voltage level and operator?

Español

Answer

CNMC consolidates official transmission and distribution substation points in public ArcGIS services, with voltage in kV and operator code/name. The October 2026 layers contain 937 transmission rows (787 substation keys) and 6,182 demand / 6,227 generation distribution rows from 59 / 61 operators. These are access-capacity busbars above 1 kV, with separate layers that can be queried without registration. Coverage of every distribution substation is unverified, some distribution coordinates are wrong, and planned assets are not separately flagged.

Operator operatorCovered
  • s001
GESTOR_RED and DESCRIPCION give operator code and legal name; transmission is R1-000 / Red Eléctrica. These identify the network manager, not every owner.

Main caveats

  • Counts are busbar records, not installations. Deduplicate within each operator using SUBESTACION and retain all voltage levels; shared sites can have more than one operator.
  • The regulatory scope is substation busbars above 1 kV. The distributor register includes operators exempt because they have no such substations; its company count is not a missing-substation count.
  • The reporting schema admits existing and planned substations. The public layers have no explicit commissioning/status flag, so they do not establish an operational-only inventory.
  • The tested distribution geometry contains misplaced points: 2 i-DE demand rows outside Spain and 65 generation rows far from their declared Catalan provinces. Details are in source 1.
  • Generation and demand differ in coverage; use both. CNMC points do not supply enclosure polygons, and no reuse licence was stated in the checked layer metadata.
