No single un-gated official dataset covers all Spanish hydropower plants with MW, operator, reservoir volume, and annual generation. For dams, MITECO's UP_Presas_y_embalses ArcGIS REST service covers 3,208 dams and 3,170 reservoirs with coordinates, volume (hm3), and operator. For hydro plants, MITECO's WFS (876 plants) lacks MW in 88% of rows and omits operators; PRETOR/REPROv2 use CAPTCHAs; Electra's bulk dump omits technology. Non-official datasets are also partial (JRC 342 plants and WRI 124 plants lack small-hydro operators and current annual MWh); pair them with basin/regional sources (CH Duero, CH Jucar, CH Cantabrico, INEGA, DERA).

Confidence reason: Directly queried MITECO ArcGIS REST, GeoServer WFS, Electra, REData, CNMC, 9 basins, 5 regional portals, JRC, WRI, ENTSO-E, GeoDAR, GEM, SEPREM, ohsome, and BOE.

Main caveats

  • MITECO UP_Presas_y_embalses USO mislabels major hydro dams (ALDEADAVILA, ALCANTARA, ALMENDRA, CORTES II) as Abastecimiento; never filter USO to find hydro dams.
  • In UP_Presas_y_embalses Layer 0, multi-dike dams repeat rows (ALMENDRA x4) each repeating full NMN_CAPAC; sum volume from Layer 1 (Embalses) and filter FASE='Explotación'.
  • MITECO WFS agua:aprov_hidro_central (876 plants) has potencia_t=0 in 770/876 rows, lat=0 in 34/876, and no operator; Electra dump lacks tech, >50 MW phases, and froze 2026-01-27.
  • Observed annual generation per plant is not published nationwide; MITECO, REE REData, and CNMC publish only aggregates, leaving CH Duero, CH Jucar, and ENTSO-E (>=100 MW).
  • MITECO static dam Shapefiles use an Altcha anti-bot gate and RAIPRE queries (PRETOR/REPROv2) use image CAPTCHAs; use MITECO ArcGIS REST UP_Presas_y_embalses instead.
