Spain ev charging stations data sources

Where can I get a list of public electric vehicle charging points in Spain, with location, operator, power (kW) and number of connectors?

Answer

Yes: the Ministry (MITECO) keeps a national census of public-access charging points (RIPREE), republished daily as a DATEX II XML feed on the DGT National Access Point. The feed has 12,114 sites, 35,905 points and 43,097 connectors with exact coordinates, operator (CPO) and max power per connector. Reserve: no live availability or price in it; completeness against the real network is not checked.

Local search terms

  • punto de recarga — Charging point (EVSE); one point can have several connectors
  • electrolinera — Charging station; the NAP feed file is named electrolineras.xml
  • CPO / operador de punto de recarga de acceso público — Charge point operator, the company that reports to the registry
  • EMSP / empresa proveedora de servicios de movilidad eléctrica — Mobility service provider (tariffs, not locations)
  • RIPREE — Census of public-access charging points run by MITECO
  • Punto de Acceso Nacional (PAN/NAP) — DGT national access point for traffic and mobility data
  • estació de recàrrega — Charging station (Catalan open data)
  • recarga rápida / semirrápida — Fast (about 50 kW) and semi-fast (about 22 kW) charging

Main caveats

  • Power unit differs by file: DATEX maxPowerAtSocket is in watts (350000.0 = 350 kW); RIPREE CSV/XML carries text like '350,00 kW'. Divide by 1000 only for the DATEX feed.
  • Rows are not points: RIPREE CSV/XML has one row per connector (43,098); DATEX nests connectors in 35,905 refill points inside 12,114 sites. 4,999 points have 2+ connectors.
  • Only public-access points reported by registered CPOs are included. Availability and price (needed only for points of 43 kW or more) are not in these files; REVE shows them as a viewer only.
  • Completeness against the real network is unverified: registered operators number 163 and no independent total was opened. Per-site lastUpdated dates range from 2023 to 2026.
  • In the DATEX feed postcodes lose the leading zero (3,388 sites have under five digits) and typeOfSite is missing for 1,217 sites; the RIPREE CSV/XML keep the zero.
