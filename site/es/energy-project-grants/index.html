Spain energy project grants data sources

Where can I get the public grants awarded to energy projects in Spain, with beneficiary, project, amount, programme, location and date?

Answer

Spain’s BDNS/SNPSAP consolidates publicly disclosable grants from all levels of government, with awardee, amount, call and award date; the API was not verified from this environment. Individual grant decisions, including IDAE decisions, add project names and municipalities for some programmes. There is no verified nationwide project-level location field or complete historical public series.

Main caveats

  • BDNS publication periods limit historical availability; natural-person grants have shorter publicity periods.
  • Awarded maximum amounts are not necessarily actual payments or proof that a project was built.
  • Energy classification requires identifying relevant calls; their geographic impact is not necessarily a project site.
  • Regional authorities award end-user grants; transfers to regions and final awards are separate transactions.
