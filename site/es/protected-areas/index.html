Spain protected areas data sources

Where can I get the boundaries (geometry) of protected natural areas in Spain, at every designation level (international, national and regional), with category and designation date?

Español

Answer

The IEPNB/MITECO GeoServer WFS (iepnb.gob.es/geoserver) serves geometry for all Spanish protected areas: ENP (1785, includes regional figures), Red Natura 2000 (1865) and international sites (178), each with figure, IUCN category and designation YEAR (anio_alta). Full dates: MITECO MAB files, EEA Natura 2000 tables, Ramsar RSIS and 9 of 17 regional layers; other regions give a norm or only the year.

Main caveats

  • anio_alta and EEA legalFoundationDate are a YEAR. For Donana RN2000 the WFS says 1997 but the EEA Natura table has DATE_SPA 1987-09-01 and DATE_SAC 2012-09: they are not the same event.
  • WFS list_normas comes out as 'Java String[]@hash' (unreadable); norms must be read elsewhere (Natura SPA/SAC_LEGAL_REFERENCE, Ramsar RAM_BOE, Andalucia NOR_DECLA).
  • ENP layer mixes figures that overlap (Natura 2000 entries, Zonas Humedas, Cuevas, Arbol Singular...). Do not add areas by feature; 1785 is the official count.
  • Catalonia ENPE mirror (gissrv.diba.cat) returns empty rings and ANY_APROV in only 4 of 389 rows; the IDEC OGC API has the Generalitat data. MITECO Ramsar GeoJSON zip is empty (22 bytes); use the shp zip.
  • EEA CDDA service is v21 (data to March 2023, 1852 ES sites); MITECO/IEPNB are Dec 2025. Counts differ between sources (ENP 1785 vs 1852).
