Spain land use plans data sources

Where can I get the current land-use and zoning plans of Spain as geometry, with land classification and the planning instrument they belong to?

Answer

There is no single national zoning geometry. The ministry SIU (WFS/REST) gives national land-class polygons and the planning instrument in force per municipality. Real zoning (calificacion) and the instrument per polygon exist only in regional services; ten autonomous communities were tested (Valencia is the most complete), six have no usable public geometry service found. Data are informative, not legally binding.

Main caveats

  • Urbanismo is a regional competence: each region has its own schema, class codes and refresh date, so nationwide results need a per-region mapping.
  • Regional and SIU layers are informative; legal force lies in the approved instrument published in the regional registry or official gazette.
  • SIU instrument is municipal only (Plan General, Normas Subsidiarias, Delimitacion de Suelo, Sin Planeamiento); 1357 of 8217 municipal polygons have no planning.
  • mivau.gob.es answers 403 to automated clients and its shapefile zips returned 404 on 2026-11-16; use the mapas.fomento.gob.es services instead.
  • Six regions lack a found public geometry service (Andalucia, Galicia, Cantabria, Asturias, La Rioja, Castilla-La Mancha viewer-only); Extremadura listed service is dead.
