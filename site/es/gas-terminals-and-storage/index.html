Spain gas terminals and storage data sources

Where can I get a list of LNG terminals and underground gas storage facilities in Spain, with location, operator, capacity and status?

Answer

No single official dataset covers all 7 Spanish LNG terminals and 4 basic underground gas storage (UGS) facilities with coordinates, operator, capacity, and status. Officially, combine Enagas POI JSON endpoints (exact coordinates and capacities for 6/7 LNG and 3/4 UGS), Enagas ISG 2025 Excel (capacities for all 7 LNG), ACER REMIT (all 7 LNG operators), and BOE Order TED/1149/2026 (all 4 UGS and holders). Non-official GIE Excel databases cover all 7 LNG and 4 UGS capacities and status without coordinates; GEM GGIT adds LNG coordinates.

Main caveats

  • Enagas POI JSON omits non-Enagas Spanish assets (Mugardos LNG and Marismas UGS) while including 3 foreign terminals (Altamira MX, Revithoussa GR, Ravenna IT) with no country field.
  • GIE Storage DB row ES01V1 (35.93 TWh) is a virtual sum of the 4 physical Spanish UGS (summing all 5 rows double-counts); it also still lists Enagas/Gas Natural Fenosa for Marismas instead of Trinity.
  • El Musel LNG storage is reported as 130,000 m3 in Enagas ISG 2025 and GIE LNG DB vs 300,000 m3 physical (2x150,000 m3, with 170,000 m3 offered for logistics) on Enagas/Musel E-Hub pages; GIE LNG DB Sheet12 also contradicts itself on status.
  • Marismas capacity differs across sources (0.831 TWh / 831 GWh in GIE Storage DB vs 1,615 GWh approved max capacity on Trinity's site); BOE-A-2026-8574 dismantles old production wells, not Marismas UGS.
  • Underground storage commercial data in GIE AGSI+, Enagas ISG 2025 (p. 37), and Enagas storagetracing JSON is aggregated nationally (VGS / Almacenamiento unico) rather than per facility.
