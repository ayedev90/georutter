No official downloadable list exists: the Ministry's InfoAntenas viewer (VCTEL) shows each station's position, operator and assigned bands (MHz) for mobile stations in harmonised bands, and its database (the Registro Nacional de Frecuencias) is restricted by law. The viewer's GeoJSON layer can be queried per bounding box; a community GPX copy (58,506 waypoints, May 2026) gives a national file. Technology is not labelled officially; OpenCelliD labels it but is crowdsourced and needs a free token.

Confidence reason: Layer, detail pages and GPX copy were tested live and the access denial is documented by official texts; official national total, update date and GPX licence were not obtained.

Not published: No technology field in the VCTEL layer, its detail page, the service page or the datos.gob.es reply; OpenCelliD radio values are crowdsourced, not per official site.

Main caveats

  • The official viewer says station positions are 'orientativa' and lists only stations of current mobile operators in harmonised bands; it is not the full restricted RENAFE register.
  • No official national file: the layer is queried per bounding box. A whole-country pull was not done, so the official national station total is unknown.
  • One site appears once per operator with different IDs, and operator names are legal entities (TELEFONICA MOVILES ESPAÑA, S.A.U.; VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. and S.A.; ORANGE ESPAGNE, S.A.U.).
  • Technology is not a field: only assigned bands (MHz) on a per-station detail page, one request per site. Mapping bands to 2G-5G is left to the reader.
  • The GPX copy and AntenasMoviles are third-party snapshots of ministry data (licence unstated); OpenCelliD counts logical cells, not physical towers.
