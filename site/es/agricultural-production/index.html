MAPA is the official source. Its 2025 provisional workbooks by crop group give national, comunidad and province tables plus a long-format database (sown, cultivated and harvested area; harvested production; provincial yields for secano and regadío). The Anuario de Estadística 2025, chapter 7, holds the consolidated 2024 data and series from 2014. The July 2026 monthly advance gives provisional 2026 cereal estimates. Eurostat's national and NUTS 2 series and the ESYRCE area survey cross-check the figures. MAPA documents its municipal crop-area survey as not disseminated (IME 2014); Catalonia publishes municipal woody-crop areas as open data.

Confidence reason: Core figures were recomputed from the official files (national sums match the tables; yields reproduce). Unresolved: 2025 provisional status, olive national yield cells, and the Eurostat 2025 gap.

Main caveats

  • 2025 figures are provisional. MAPA says the Anuario consolidates them; 2024 is the consolidated edition.
  • Olive national TOTAL row yields (1,427 and 4,069 kg/ha) repeat in 2024 and 2025 and do not match production over harvested area. Compute yields from the tables.
  • Yields reproduce as production over harvested area (checked for cereals, olive, Jaén, Spain). The 2013 notes define yield over cultivated area.
  • The olive series changes in 2023: double-purpose groves are added and harvested area is redefined. Do not splice across 2023 without adjustment.
  • MAPA's IME (2014) says municipal crop areas are not disseminated. Catalonia's municipal open data covers woody crops, as area only (no tonnes or yields).
  • Eurostat's Spanish 2025 production is about 3% below MAPA's provisional figure (Castilla y León 3.9%). For 2024 the two agree within 0.1%.
