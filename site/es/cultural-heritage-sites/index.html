No single national dataset of protected heritage geometry was found. The national register (Ministerio de Cultura, RBIC) is a search form with category, code and dates but no coordinates. Registers are regional: 13 regions or cities publish geometry (polygons in Andalucía, Aragón, Asturias, Cantabria, Castilla y León, Castilla-La Mancha, Cataluña, Galicia, Madrid city, Murcia, Navarra, Valencia, País Vasco; Canarias view only). IGN has 1,245 BIC points without category.

Confidence reason: Live services tested in 14 regions and the national forms; negatives (Baleares, La Rioja, Ceuta, Melilla) rest on opened catalogues and pages, La Rioja only through an archived copy.

Protection category protection_categoryPartially covereds002 s001MCU gives categoría per record nationally; regional layers carry category fields with different vocabularies.

Not published: MCU database is under review, has no bulk download and omits BIC declared by law (castles, rock art, hórreos...).

Register authority register_authorityCovereds002 s001MCU keeps the register for state-level BIC; each autonomous community keeps the rest and reports to it (RD 111/1986 art. 21).

Main caveats

  • Geometry exists only regionally: formats, CRS (EPSG:25829, 25830, 25831, 32628, 4326), categories and scope (BIC vs local interest vs catalogued/inventoried) all differ and must be harmonised.
  • The Ministry database is marked "en revisión", is a per-record web form (no download) and does not contain BIC declared by law (castles, rock art, hórreos, crosses).
  • Several layers mix legal levels: Murcia 3,428 catalogados vs 1,985 BIC; Cataluña BCIL 14,406 vs BCIN 2,161; Cantabria includes BIL and inventariados; Andalucía 207 of 2,425 are catalogación general.
  • Gaps: Baleares, Ceuta and Melilla have no geometry service; La Rioja portals block automated clients; Canarias and País Vasco give view services or point layers; Madrid polygons cover Madrid city only.
  • Cataluña WFS rejected curl default identification (403) and answered via WebFetch; Aragón server was very slow; opendata.jcyl.es zip has a broken SSL chain.
