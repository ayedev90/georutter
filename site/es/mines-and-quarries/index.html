Spain mines and quarries data sources

Where can I get a list of active mines and quarries in Spain, with location, mineral or rock extracted, operator and annual production?

Español

Answer

IGME documents a 2017 map of active mines and quarries and links a 2019 list; the map is no longer updated. A non-official list names selected metal and industrial mines; Atalaya publishes Riotinto annual output. A current national facility list with all requested fields was not verified, and ESMIN publishes aggregate production.

Main caveats

  • The IGME map reflects 2017, not present activity; some mapped positions are municipality coordinates.
  • ESMIN collects mine-level production but publishes aggregated tables; actual production cannot be inferred from mining rights or planned output.
  • The 2019 spreadsheet could not be inspected in this environment; its fields and completeness remain unverified.
  • The non-official list excludes gypsum, ordinary aggregates and ornamental stone and uses older inventories.
  • The Catalonia and BDMIN services were discovered but their data could not be queried; field values and activity status are unverified.
