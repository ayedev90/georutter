BOE publishes consolidated individual laws and energy codes, including electricity authorization, environmental assessment, gas, water, offshore and nuclear rules. Technology, installed capacity, location and regional competence determine which texts apply; there is no tested complete nationwide matrix covering every technology and size. Regional regulations and authentic publications require separate checks.

Confidence reason: Principal sources and examples were opened, but regional coverage and all technology-specific thresholds remain incomplete.

Main caveats

  • BOE consolidation is informative; check original official publications and pending or implicit amendments.
  • Installed electrical MW, thermal MW, surface and other thresholds measure different things; do not interchange them.
  • Regional, local, environmental, water and grid-connection procedures can apply alongside the energy authorization.
  • More than 50 MW is not the only state competence criterion; offshore and cross-regional projects and network classifications also matter.
