Spain environmental assessment decisions data sources

Where can I get the environmental assessment decisions on energy projects in Spain, with project, developer, location, date and outcome?

Español

Answer

No complete structured Spanish inventory was found. The BOE publishes state decisions. Castilla y León has an index of 5,388 ordinary EIA files (797 energy), with publication dates and no promoter or outcome. Andalucía lists granted unified authorizations. An unofficial UAM compilation of BOE records for PV and hybrid projects covers 2014–2025 (570 rows, 470 labelled favorable and 100 unfavorable). Official coverage and the latest available decisions remain unverified.

Main caveats

  • Environmental decisions are distinct from final construction or operating authorization.
  • The UAM compilation is non-official. Its description says favorable DIAs only, but 100 of its 570 rows are unfavorable; it covers PV and hybrid projects, not all energy.
  • The BOE API has no DIA keyword search. A full enumeration needs a day-by-day scan of the summaries, which was not run; only 2025-12-29 and 2014-11-19 were checked.
  • Castilla y León declares an annual update for its case index. Its BOCYL field is a publication date and its rows have no promoter or outcome.
  • Andalucía lists granted authorizations only, so it cannot establish the number of adverse decisions. Its first-page dates are from 2009.
  • BOE and SABIA are not verified as complete inventories of regional decisions, and regional gazettes outside Castilla y León and Andalucía were not searched in this pass.
