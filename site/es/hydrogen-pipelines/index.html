Spain hydrogen pipelines data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of hydrogen pipelines in operation or planned in Spain, with operator, capacity and status?

Español

Answer

Spain has no national open GIS registry of hydrogen pipelines (checked MITECO, CNMC, IGN/IDEE, datos.gob.es, OSM). Only two short local pipelines operate today: Nortegas Abanto (1.86 km, 2022) and Redexis Mallorca (3.2 km, Sep 2024), neither having public polyline geometry. For planned transmission, ENTSOG/GIE/EHB Joint Map's ArcGIS FeatureServer (h2inframap.eu) provides indicative polylines and operators, while ENTSOG TYNDP 2026 Annex A/C2 XLSX adds engineering specs, status, and GWh/d capacity. CINEA PCI Fiches and Enagas PDFs add official schematic maps, permitting status, and BOE/Delegacion del Gobierno RBDA notices with cadastral parcels.

Main caveats

  • Spain has only 2 operational dedicated H2 pipelines (Nortegas 1.86 km; Redexis 3.2 km): Redexis Mallorca is absent from every dataset, and Nortegas Abanto appears only as a point (~18 km offset) in h2inframap Layer 0.
  • h2inframap.eu geometries are indicative/schematic (~12 km vertex spacing on Backbone), omit the Apr 2026 Alcazar de San Juan-Montesa PCI extension, lack ISO country and status columns, and lack capacity on 8/11 ES lines.
  • No official Spanish GIS file exists: MITECO, CNMC, IGN/IDEE, datos.gob.es and OSM Spain have zero H2 pipelines; EC PCI Transparency Platform viewer redirects to sorry.ec.europa.eu.
  • Official Enagas route files are PDFs and BOE permitting tables list affected cadastral parcel references (reconstructible only at parcel corridor level via Catastro WFS, not final as-built centerlines).
