Spain land ownership data sources

How can I find out who owns a given land parcel in Spain: which register holds ownership, whether it is open, and the legal route and cost to obtain it?

Answer

Legal ownership is held by the Registro de la Propiedad (Colegio de Registradores, under the Ministerio de Justicia). It is not open data: a nota simple (9,02 EUR per finca online, plus VAT) names the registered titular if you state a legitimate interest; the registrar decides within 24 h. Catastro shows the fiscal 'titular catastral' only to the owner or authorised persons (free online); its open data has no owner. No bulk owner dataset was found.

Local search terms

  • nota simple informativa — Informative registry extract naming registered titular, rights and charges
  • certificacion registral — Registry certificate signed by the registrador, with legal faith
  • nota de localizacion — Tells in which registry a titular has rights (by NIF)
  • interes legitimo / interes conocido — Requirement to access registry data (Ley Hipotecaria art. 221)
  • titular catastral / datos protegidos — Catastral owner; protected datum under TRLCI art. 51
  • referencia catastral (RC) — Parcel identifier of the Catastro
  • CRU / IDUFIR — Unique registral code of a finca
  • cedula parcelaria — Navarra/Basque certificate with owner, area and plan

Main caveats

  • Registro and Catastro can disagree: Catastro data are presumed true only 'sin perjuicio del Registro' (TRLCI art. 3.3). Land never inscribed has no registry entry; Catastro then names only a fiscal titular.
  • Price conflict: BOE-consolidated arancel (RD 1427/1989) sets 3,005061 EUR per nota simple; the Colegio charges 9,02 EUR online (doctrine DGRN). A 2026 draft RD would codify 9,02; not confirmed in force.
  • Registry information is for the requester's own use for the stated interest; the sede terms forbid redistribution or loading it into searchable databases.
  • Navarra (Registro de la Riqueza Territorial) and Araba, Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa run their own cadastres: the state Catastro certificate does not cover them and rules differ (Bizkaia: valor catastral not protected).
  • Public-notice route (BOE expropriation lists) gives owners only for parcels hit by a project, for the notice period, and with 'Desconocido' for some parcels.
