Spain battery storage data sources

Where can I get a list of battery storage (BESS) installations in operation in Spain, with location, power (MW), energy (MWh), operator and commissioning date?

Answer

A tested August 2026 copy of the European Energy Storage Inventory identifies 26 Spanish electrochemical projects labelled operational with power, energy and coordinates, but lacks operator and commissioning fields. Operator announcements supply those details for selected installations. A national complete, current public BESS list with all requested fields was not verified.

Main caveats

  • The snapshot is dated 2026-08-16 and does not establish current status or full coverage.
  • The JRC reports that some MWh capacities are estimated; the meaning of the snapshot flag needs confirmation.
  • The snapshot provides no operator or commissioning-date field; coordinates are not validated to the facility.
  • A construction authorisation, grid permission or market registration does not alone establish operation.
  • Behind-the-meter and hybrid storage may be absent or recorded under a host facility.
