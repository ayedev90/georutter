No single national registry publishes biomethane injection capacity by zone or connection point in Spain. Under CNMC Circular 2/2025 (Art. 40), operators must publish capacity by network segment, but only Gas Extremadura publishes granted and in-process injection capacity plus flow percentiles (2023-2025 PDFs for Extremadura). Major DSOs (Nortegas, Nedgia, Redexis, Madrileña Red de Gas) only publish >=16 bar schematics and historical demand/flow proxies, while Enagás Transporte requires registration on ServiceNow; remaining gaps require prior-information requests to each operator.

Confidence reason: Directly verified BOE Circular 2/2025 & Res. 13/06/2025, CNMC census/reports, MITECO, Enagás GTS/Transporte, all 5 major DSO portals with downloaded PDFs, 4 minor DSOs, and non-official maps in this session.

Main caveats

  • Despite Circular 2/2025 Art. 40.3 mandating capacity by segment, 4 of 5 major DSOs tested (Nortegas, Nedgia, Redexis, Madrileña) only publish >=16 bar schematics and historical demand/flows, omitting granted or free injection capacity.
  • Historical gas demand/flows act only as an upper-bound proxy: already-granted or queued injections, downstream sensitive consumers, and reverse-flow limits can reduce or block actual injection capacity.
  • Enagás Transporte (national primary transmission grid, P > 60 bar) places network schemes, historical consumptions, and biomethane pre-requests behind mandatory company registration on ServiceNow.
  • Enagás GTS publishes conditional capacity by area ONLY for hydrogen (a gas requiring mixing, Circular 2/2025 Art. 40.2); biomethane does not require mixing and is governed per operator under Art. 40.3.
  • Published data live in unstandardized PDFs without machine-readable coordinates; Gas Extremadura omits an explicit unit label on its granted and in-process columns (implicitly Nm3/h matching flow rows).
