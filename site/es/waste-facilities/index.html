Confidence
Medium

Spain has a national RPGR of authorized waste installations and managers, but the public search was not exercised. PRTR España provides facility coordinates, activity and operational status for a regulated subset. Capacity is held in RPGR but is absent from the public fields specified by the 2026 order; individual permits provide examples, not a complete current inventory.

Confidence reason: The national register and one PRTR record were opened; the public RPGR search and regional downloads were not executed.

Main caveats

  • PRTR thresholds exclude many smaller facilities and inert landfills from activity 5.d.
  • RPGR search returns rows per waste code and operation and caps a query at 10,000 results.
  • The 2026 public-information order does not list treatment capacity among public RPGR fields.
  • A historical authorization is not evidence of current capacity or operating status.
