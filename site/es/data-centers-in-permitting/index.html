Spain data centers in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of data center projects currently in permitting in Spain, with location, power (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

Answer

Regional and municipal authorities publish individual permits and supporting documents. An independent downloadable Spanish inventory tracks sites, stages, dates and reported MW with references, but is incomplete and its statuses may lag. Check each current permitting stage against the latest official act.

Main caveats

  • A consultation closing date is not a decision or proof that a file remains pending.
  • IT load, grid connection, thermal input and building power are different quantities.
  • Environmental review may apply to auxiliary equipment or grid works, rather than every data centre.
  • The commercial or community status labels must be checked against the latest official acts.
