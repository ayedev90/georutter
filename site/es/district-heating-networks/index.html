No open file with routes or service areas of Spanish district heating and cooling networks was found. Best source: the ADHAC census (industry association, with IDAE), a 2025 PDF list of 585 networks with municipality, supply type and fuels, but no operator, MW or coordinates; MW exist only as totals (1,753 MW). IDAE's page cites a networks layer, yet its user manual and viewer code show only demand and waste-heat points. OSM holds 20 tagged pipe ways in Spain. A public register exists only as a draft decree.

Confidence reason: Census, AVEBIOM, manual and operator PDFs downloaded and read; viewer live layer and OSM geometry not seen; decree status checked by searches and BOE pages only; operator sample is 8 of 585.

Main caveats

  • The ADHAC list is built from member and public data; the association itself reports gaps ('Opacidad de explotadores', refusals by some companies), so it is a floor, not an official register.
  • Counts differ: ADHAC 2025 lists 585 networks (Cataluña 246); AVEBIOM 2024 counts 584 biomass networks (Cataluña 278). ADHAC still lists La Valldan (Berga), which AVEBIOM reports removed after a fire.
  • IDAE's web page says the Mapa de calor includes existing and planned networks, but its 2021 user manual describes only demand and waste-heat points, and its data are estimates 'no pueden considerarse como reales'.
  • Location is municipality only; rows include single-building systems (hospitals, campuses). Operator figures may be design or phase values (Txantrea: 24,5 MW phase 1 in 2024, 'hasta 39 megavatios' in a 2022 article).
  • The public national register of urban heating and cooling systems exists only in a draft royal decree (2026 consultation); no approval found as of 2026-11-16.
