Official prices exist, but not per municipality. Best: MAPA 'Precios Medios Anuales de las Tierras de Uso Agrario' (EUR/ha, 18 crop groups; regions and provinces 2020-2024 in xlsx; 2025 provisional exists but was not opened). Municipality level: Catastro IAMIR 2026 gives EUR/ha per crop group for areas (ATH) that group municipalities. Transaction counts by province and month: INE ETDP. Per-deal prices are not public.

Confidence reason: Main sources opened and tested; MAPA live site and 2025 PDF unreachable, 2024 provinces read from a provisional xlsx; IAMIR parsed by text extraction.

Main caveats

  • MAPA series breaks in 2024: source moved to Catastro notarial sales and base 2020; values before 2020 (base 2016, survey) are not comparable with 2020 onward.
  • Regional surveys differ: Andalucia 2022 survey 19,934 EUR/ha vs MAPA base-2020 Andalucia 2022 15,748; Catalonia 2024 survey 13,905 vs MAPA 13,030.
  • IAMIR 2026 uses sales 2023-04-01 to 2026-03-31, excludes built, mixed-crop and flagged sales, and omits Navarra and Pais Vasco; ATH modules are central values per area, not municipal prices.
  • MAPA values are empty or '..' when not significant; Navarra and Pais Vasco come from regional sources, not Catastro; no Navarra or Pais Vasco land price publication found in Nastat, Eustat or Navarra's foral cadastre.
  • www.mapa.gob.es fails TLS here; MAPA files read from Internet Archive. 2025 provisional exists (national 10,367 EUR/ha per a news site) but its official PDF was not opened.
