Spain mining rights data sources

Where can I get mining rights in Spain (exploration permits and mining concessions) as geometry, with holder, commodity, status and dates?

Answer

The national Mining Cadastre documents rights searches and SHP/GeoJSON exports; its data are under review and may lag regional records. Regional sources include a Canary Islands map explicitly covering exploration permits, and a Navarre section C dataset. Actual vector exports and full field coverage were not tested here; a BOE notice supplies an example boundary and historical grant information.

Main caveats

  • The national viewer warns that information is under review and may not match the actual legal situation; regional mining authorities hold authoritative files.
  • The national WFS and vector exports were not successfully queried here; coverage and joins cannot be confirmed from actual records.
  • A permit or concession is a legal area, not an operating mine; an old grant notice does not establish current validity.
