Spain electricity consumption local data sources

Where can I get electricity consumption by municipality or postal code in Spain, by sector and year?

Answer

Spain publishes no national file of electricity use by municipality or postal code. Best: Catalonia (ICAEN, 947 municipalities, 6 sectors, kWh, 2013-2024, API) and Canarias (ISTAC, monthly, 3 sectors, MWh). Postal codes: Barcelona city by sector, and E-REDES/Viesgo/Begasa areas as totals. Other regions give totals, residential only, or per-capita figures. National bodies (CNMC, REE, MITECO) stop at region or province.

Local search terms

  • consumo eléctrico municipal / por municipios — electricity use by municipality
  • energía eléctrica facturada — billed electricity, the wording used by Madrid and IECA
  • consumo por código postal — postal-code level use, as in distributors' open data
  • consum d'energia elèctrica per municipis i sectors — Catalan statistic title
  • usos domésticos / servicios / industria — typical sector labels
  • secreto estadístico / secret estadístic — cell suppressed to protect individual consumers
  • CUPS — supply-point code; customer counts in ISTAC are CUPS
  • concello / concejo / udalerri — municipality in Galicia, Asturias, Basque Country

Main caveats

  • Units differ: Catalonia kWh, Canarias and Andalusia MWh, Basque kWh per inhabitant; Barcelona is labelled MWh but behaves as kWh.
  • Sector categories are not comparable across sources (6 in Catalonia, 3 in Canarias and Barcelona, industrial vs non-industrial in the Basque Country).
  • Andalusian figures cover only the Endesa network; municipalities served by others show 0 MWh.
  • Catalonia leaves cells empty under statistical secrecy (10,099 of 54,611 rows); sums by municipality can be incomplete.
  • No municipal data found for Galicia, Aragon, Valencia, Asturias, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha, Extremadura, Baleares; Murcia only a 2014 PDF.
