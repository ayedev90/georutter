Spain wind farms in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of wind projects currently in permitting in Spain, with location, capacity (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

Español

Answer

Regional authorities publish project lists and maps, but the sources tested do not establish a complete, current national list. Aragon, Asturias, Cantabria, Catalonia, Galicia and Navarre provide complementary data. BOE notices include onshore and offshore projects and dated milestones; a notice alone does not establish the current stage.

Main caveats

  • No single source tested covers all autonomous and state permitting, including offshore projects.
  • Cantabria’s active layer included 2 operating parks among 13 records; check Estado_Adm.
  • Catalonia had 42 municipal rows for 30 applications; avoid counting rows as projects.
  • Navarre covers AAP/AAC projects not yet in operation, rather than all early applications.
  • The GEM wind tracker requires a form and uses broad development statuses; project records were not tested.
