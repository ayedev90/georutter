Spain has no national vector zoning and no designated renewables acceleration areas (ZAR). MITECO's national sensitivity model for wind and PV (5 classes) is a 25 m raster (GeoTIFF, WMS, WCS). ZAR are designated by regions (RDL 7/2026; decree pending); only Canarias publishes them (La Gomera, El Hierro, WMS). Vector zoning exists in Asturias, Baleares, Castilla y Leon, Navarra, Euskadi, Valencia; Madrid is raster.

Confidence reason: All 19 regions and cities were checked; 8 have data I queried or downloaded, 7 returned nothing after opened pages, and 3 maps could not be reached by my client.

Main caveats

  • The national ISA is a 25 m raster: values are pixel values (0-10000 or classes 0-4); there are no polygons or attributes unless you vectorise it yourself.
  • MITECO calls the model orientative; it does not designate suitable zones. Class 0 means 'Maxima (no recomendado)'.
  • Canarias ZAR are proposals under island protocols (La Gomera 2025-07-30, El Hierro 2026-05-19); the WMS forbids commercial use and bulk download; Lanzarote is not in it.
  • Regional layers differ in age and legal meaning (Baleares 2014 plan, Asturias 2008 directives, Navarra 2019, Euskadi 2021, CyL DL 2/2022 exclusions) and are not comparable.
  • The WMS addresses in the 2021 MITECO access PDF (wmts.mapama.gob.es) return 404; use gis.miteco.gob.es/geoserver/evaluacionambiental.
