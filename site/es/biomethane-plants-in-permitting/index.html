Aragón publishes files in public participation, and Galicia lists designated strategic projects with their stage. A community table adds Navarra projects. These are partial regional sources; capacity and current status often require checking project documents.

Confidence reason: The research is incomplete and regional and national coverage have not yet been checked.

Main caveats

  • Aragón data cover only projects in public participation and may not identify technology in every description.
  • Navarra project statuses need updating against later decisions and the regional moratorium.
  • Waste input tonnage is not gas production capacity.
