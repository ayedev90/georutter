Spain wastewater treatment plants data sources

Where can I get a list of urban wastewater treatment plants in Spain, with location, design capacity, treated volume and treatment level?

Answer

Spain reports its plants to the EU under Directive 91/271/CEE. The EEA public layer (ArcGIS REST, 2,138 Spanish plants, status 31/12/2022) has coordinates, design capacity (p.e.), treatment flags and treated volume (m3/year, filled for 1,666). MITECO publishes the same report as GeoPackage with 2,357 plants but only name/id/status. Plants below the reporting threshold exist only in regional lists.

Main caveats

  • EEA and MITECO layers are Spain's UWWTD report at 31/12/2022 (Q2023); the EEA output is flagged provisional pending the Commission compliance check.
  • uwwCapacity and load are in population equivalents (p.e.), not m3/day; Spain notes capacity is a mean project capacity while load is the weekly maximum, so load can exceed capacity (227 plants).
  • MITECO GPKG (INSPIRE form) has no capacity, volume or treatment fields; the SHP with those fields sits behind an Altcha download page and the WMS host fails TLS validation.
  • Capacity differs between sources: BOE 2025 gives 19,632 p.e. for the Mazagon expansion, EEA layer 53,140 p.e. for MAZAGON; reason not established.
  • Regional codes differ: ACA codi_aglomeracio ES09080010001010 equals EEA uwwCode ES9080010001010E (leading 0 dropped, E added).
