Spain hydrogen plants data sources

Where can I get a list of hydrogen production plants (electrolysers) in operation in Spain, with location, capacity (MW), operator and commissioning date?

Answer

The Spanish GdO register exposes participating renewable gas installations through a public API; registration status must be checked and its gas capacity is not necessarily electrical MW. The European Observatory provides operating electrolysers of at least 0.5 MWel as of December 2025, with start years. Neither covers every current plant; use operator reports to verify dates and status.

Main caveats

  • The GdO API returned provisional and rejected records even with the COMPLETED filter; filter and verify status.
  • Barcelona coordinates differ between GdO and the European workbook; GdO Lloseta coordinates appear displaced.
  • Production-point capacity in kW and electrolyser input MWel are different quantities.
  • The 2025 European dataset excludes electrolysers below 0.5 MWel and omits operator names.
