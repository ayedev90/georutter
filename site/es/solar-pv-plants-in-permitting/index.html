Spain solar pv plants in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of solar PV projects currently in permitting in Spain, with location, capacity (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

Español

Answer

Spain publishes regional project information and official notices, but the sources checked do not establish a complete current national list. Catalonia, Aragon, Valencia and Navarre provide regional project data. The ministry describes a state map as under construction; BOE notices provide dated milestones. National coverage and current-stage dates remain unverified.

Main caveats

  • State and autonomous authorities process different projects; the 50 MW threshold has territorial exceptions.
  • A public notice records an event, not necessarily the present status; check subsequent decisions.
  • Navarre includes projects with AAP and/or AAC; its BEGINLIFE field is a database insertion date.
  • Panel MWp and nominal installed MW are different; regional sources use different fields.
  • GEM has coarse project statuses, size thresholds and a download request form; individual Spanish records were not tested.
