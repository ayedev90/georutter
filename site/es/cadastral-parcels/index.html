Spain cadastral parcels data sources

Where can I get cadastral parcel boundaries (geometry) for all of Spain, with parcel identifier and area, as a bulk download or as a queryable service?

Answer

Free official GML parcel polygons (with national cadastral reference and area in m2) exist for all of Spain, but in five places: the state Cadastre (DGC) ATOM feed (7,611 municipal zips) and WFS cover everything except Navarra and the three Basque provinces; those four foral cadastres run their own ATOM/WFS, linked from the DGC ATOM index. No single national file; CRS and ID formats differ.

Main caveats

  • Navarra and the three Basque provinces are NOT in the DGC ATOM/WFS data; they come from four separate foral services with different CRS (4258/25830/3042) and ID schemes.
  • DGC WFS rejects bboxes above 1 km2 or 5,000 parcels ('Area of extension out of limits'): use ATOM for bulk. Catastro municipality codes differ from INE (Madrid 28900).
  • Gipuzkoa WFS and ATOM include superseded versions: 292 of 300 sampled WFS parcels had endLifespanVersion filled. Filter for it to be empty.
  • Licence: DGC permits own use or transformed derived products only, not redistribution of the original data; foral data is CC BY 4.0 (Navarra) or attribution (others).
  • DGC ATOM refreshes twice a year (feed dated 2026-08-21); WFS is live. Gipuzkoa says data is not an official map and not for certificates.
