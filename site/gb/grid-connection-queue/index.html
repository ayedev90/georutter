NESO publishes GB TEC contracts and a separate Scottish embedded register; SONI publishes Northern Ireland connection agreements and effective applications. Distribution operators publish their own registers. These datasets mix connected and future projects and do not provide a complete list of all UK applications.

Confidence reason: Both NESO CSVs and SONI workbook were downloaded; a distribution CSV is blocked and full UK coverage remains unchecked.

Local search terms

  • Transmission Entry Capacity — Contracted TEC, not all applications.
  • Gate 2 — Prioritised connection agreement after applicable assessment and countersignature.
  • Embedded Capacity Register — Distribution operator register; distinct from NESO Scottish Embedded register.

Main caveats

  • TEC is a contract register, not all requests; NESO reports no public demand connection register.
  • An empty Gate field is not Gate 1 or Gate 2: NESO populates it after an agreement is countersigned.
  • Effective capacity dates are not application dates; connected MW and cumulative stage MW are different.
  • NESO warns repeated capacity across stages or technologies can cause duplicates.
  • Distribution coverage varies: Northern Powergrid ECR omits unaccepted enquiries; SONI omits cluster agreements.
