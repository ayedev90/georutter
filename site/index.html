  • Where can I get the list of grid connection requests in Germany, with project, technology, capacity (MW), connection node, status and date?

    Germany publishes no current open list of grid connection requests: the § 9 KraftNAV register (>=100 MW generation, excluding storage) is restricted except for a stale 2020 TenneT PDF, while 2025-2026 releases are aggregates only.

    Research reviewed: 2026-11-26 · Card JSON

  • Where can I get the planned new power lines and substations in Germany, with location, voltage, planned date and status?

    BNetzA and BSH map transmission and offshore assets. NEP PDFs add dates and 871 substation measures. VNBdigital lists 95 active distribution plan services, with coordinates absent from the XLSX examples checked.

    Research reviewed: 2026-11-26 · Card JSON

  • Where can I get the available grid connection capacity per substation or node in Germany, for generation and for demand, with the latest value and its history?

    Fragmented across 4 TSOs and ~865 DSOs: 50Hertz (75-node JSON API) and TransnetBW (52-node HTML map) give generation and demand MW; Saarland maps 6 DSOs' feed-in MW; only paid GridChecker keeps history.

    Research reviewed: 2026-11-26 · Card JSON

  • Where can I get a list of solar PV projects currently in permitting in Germany, with location, capacity (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

    Partial official data: MaStR lists 63,257 voluntary 'In Planung' solar units (1,094 ground-mounted) without sub-stages, BNetzA XLSX lists EEG tender awards, and 16 Länder Bauleitplanung portals link to municipal zoning PDFs.

    Research reviewed: 2026-11-26 · Card JSON

  • Where can I get a list of wind projects currently in permitting in Germany, with location, capacity (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

    MaStR lists 8,808 wind turbines in 'In Planung', with permit identifiers on 8,571 units. Six Länder publish structured data on pending applications (3,249 turbines).

    Research reviewed: 2026-11-26 · Card JSON
