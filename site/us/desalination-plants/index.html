United States desalination plants data sources

Where can I get a list of desalination plants in the United States, with location, capacity, technology, operator and status?

Answer

No current national list of desalination plants was found. Reclamation's 2018 survey (Report 207) counts 406 municipal plants of 0.025 mgd or more and details 58 to 84 of them with owner, technology, start year and design and average production in mgd, but no coordinates, no status and no industrial plants. Texas (TWDB, 60 operating plants in August 2024) and California (2019 maps of 12 existing and 6 proposed seawater facilities) add state views, and operators such as Tampa Bay Water publish plant status. Capacity is stated as design, permitted, average or deliverable depending on the source.

Main caveats

  • Reclamation's inventory is municipal only and dated December 2018: 406 plants are counted but per-plant files cover 84 (TX, FL, CA built since 2010) and 58 summaries.
  • Reclamation mixes design, permitted and average capacity and does not flag closed plants; it has no coordinates and no status field.
  • Capacity is not one measure: Carlsbad shows design 54 mgd and average 50 mgd; Tampa Bay Water states up to 12 mgd at restart and 20 mgd when complete.
  • Texas's database lists operating public-water-supply plants above 0.025 mgd only; its web application could not be opened because TLS verification failed.
  • California's seawater maps are from 2019 and also name power-plant, aquarium and oil-facility sites besides municipal plants.
  • Industrial and brackish plants outside Texas, Florida and California were not covered by any per-plant source found.
