Confidence
Medium

No national official list exists. Two Virginia counties publish open ArcGIS layers of data-center projects by stage: Loudoun County (319 parcel records; 177 proposed, snapshot 1 Sep 2026, edited 2026-11-16) and Prince William County (247 buildings: 68 Pending, 91 Planned, 28 Under Construction, 60 Completed). Both give location, stage and floor area but no MW, no applicant and no stage dates. A federal example, NNSA's Savannah River Site selection of Amentum (1 GW, 20 Jul 2026), shows the DOE route. ERCOT and Virginia DEQ pages were blocked; Cleanview's commercial API needs a key.

Confidence reason: Two county layers and one federal release were verified live, but they lack MW, applicant and dates; state and utility sources were blocked.

Local search terms

  • Legislative application (LEGI) — Loudoun rezoning or special-exception request decided by the Board of Supervisors.
  • Site plan (STPL/STMP/EPLAN) — Loudoun administrative plan for a building after land-use approval.
  • GFA — Gross floor area in square feet, used by Prince William County.

Main caveats

  • Loudoun and Prince William publish floor area (sq ft) and buildings, not MW; one 'In Legislative Process' parcel list holds 72 rows but only 20 distinct applications and 54 buildings.
  • Loudoun 'Owner' is the parcel owner from land records, populated for 2 of 177 proposed rows; it is not the applicant.
  • Stage dates are absent: Loudoun is a 1 Sep 2026 snapshot and PWC LastEditDate (last 2026-11-16) is a record edit date, not a permit event date.
  • Only the county zoning/building track is covered; no environmental permit or utility interconnection list was reached (Virginia DEQ and ERCOT returned HTTP 403 to automated requests).
  • DOE's 1 GW for Savannah River is a proposed lease at the selection stage; the basis (IT, facility or grid) is not defined.
  • Cleanview/commercial datasets give capacity_mw and developer, but need an API key and could not be tested.
