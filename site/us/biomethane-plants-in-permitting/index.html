United States biomethane plants in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of biogas and biomethane projects currently in permitting in the United States, with location, capacity, developer, permitting stage and date?

Answer

No national list of biogas/RNG projects in permitting was found. Stage and date are documented only in local records: CEQAnet (California) follows projects through dated filings, e.g. Orland Power Solutions, Manteca (MND 2026-06-02, county approval 2026-07-16, air district 2026-09-09) and Couco Creek, Turlock (approval 2025-08-21). EPA LMOP (102 landfill RNG projects) and AgSTAR (73 farm digesters) give construction status without permit dates; CDFA lists California grants. Capacity is not published.

Partial coverage

Local search terms

  • CEQAnet / Notice of Determination (NOD) — California portal and the notice filed after a project is approved; open the SCH number's documents 1, 2, 3.
  • Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Use Permit Application — County zoning permit for a digester or injection site; appears as the CEQAnet title.
  • Authority to Construct (ATC) project number — Air-district permit key such as N-1261215 in San Joaquin Valley APCD records.
  • Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) — CEQA document type posted for state review before county approval.
  • LMOP under-construction or planned projects — EPA file of landfill-gas projects not yet operating.
  • DDRDP — CDFA Dairy Digester Research and Development Program; project-level grant list.
  • WARP permit tracking / construction permit (MIN, BAP) — Wisconsin DNR air permit pages with numbers such as 24-MIN-280.
  • Biogas pipeline injection site — Facility that off-loads trucked biogas into a utility pipeline, as at Couco Creek.

Main caveats

  • Approval is not construction: CEQA notices of determination show decisions (Manteca 2026-07-16 and 2026-09-09); no start-of-construction or operation document was found.
  • LMOP (September 2024) and AgSTAR (June 2024) status fields are stale: 8 planned LMOP rows and 49 of 73 AgSTAR construction rows have expected years already past.
  • LMOP 'Planned', AgSTAR 'Construction' and CDFA funding data are not permitting stages; their dates are anticipated starts or grant dates.
  • No project capacity is published in the records opened; LMOP and AgSTAR give landfill-gas or raw-biogas volumes, not RNG output.
  • Wisconsin WARP documents are behind page postbacks and were not read; Oregon and county portals outside California were not checked.
