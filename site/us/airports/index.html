Confidence
Medium

No single file answers it. FAA's NASR 28-day APT file lists 19,434 landing facilities (13,005 airports) with coordinates, owner name and manager name. FAA publishes CY2025 enplanements (516 commercial-service airports, 1,682 in all) and cargo landed weight (155 airports) as Excel. BTS T-100 gives 2024 passengers, departures, freight and mail for 1,279 origin codes on US-carrier domestic flights. FAA TAF gives operations by category to FY2024 for about 3,300 airports. Join on the airport identifier; definitions differ.

Confidence reason: NASR, FAA Excel files, BTS T-100 and TAF were downloaded and joined by identifier; ADIP and ATADS were only viewed and the totals definitions across FAA and BTS stay unreconciled.

Local search terms

  • 28 Day NASR Subscription — FAA page with the cycle downloads, including APT.zip (airport records).
  • Passenger Boarding (Enplanement) and All-Cargo Data — FAA page with yearly Excel files of enplanements and cargo landed weight per airport.
  • T-100 Domestic Market and Segment — BTS airline traffic tables; the NTAD table gives airport totals by origin.
  • Terminal Area Forecast (TAF) — FAA history and forecast of operations and enplanements by airport and fiscal year.
  • Airport Master Record (Form 5010) — FAA record holding owner, manager and facility data; viewed in ADIP.
  • ATADS — Air Traffic Activity System, the FAA tower operations counts report form.
  • Locid / ARPT_ID — FAA 3 to 4 character location identifier used to join files (ORD).
  • NPIAS — National Plan of Integrated Airport Systems; defines the airports in the TAF.

Main caveats

  • Enplanements are passenger boardings, not total passengers. FAA CY and BTS T-100 numbers for the same airport differ: ATL CY2024 52,511,402 (FAA) vs 45,406,031 (BTS 2024); ORD 38,575,693 vs 31,613,389.
  • BTS T-100 totals cover domestic airports served by US carriers only, by origin code; 37 of 516 FAA commercial-service airports have no matching BTS code and 343 BTS codes are not NASR airport IDs.
  • TAF operations are fiscal year (Oct-Sep). Towered airports are counted by controllers; at non-FAA facilities operations are estimates. FY2024 is the last actual year in the 2025 TAF; later years are forecasts.
  • NASR APT.txt is fixed width (230 MB); its annual operations and based-aircraft fields are declared in the layout but empty in all 19,434 records.
  • Airport, heliport and seaplane base share one file and one counting unit: 13,005 airports among 19,434 facilities. Terminals and runways are separate records.
  • ADIP and ATADS are form-driven viewers: ADIP is a JavaScript app with a login for airport users, and a scripted ATADS report request returned an empty page.
