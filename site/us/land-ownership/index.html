United States land ownership data sources

How can I find out who owns a given land parcel in the United States: which register holds ownership, whether it is open, and the legal route and cost to obtain it?

Answer

There is no national owner register found: parcel ownership is looked up county by county. In Denver and Travis County (TX), the assessor/appraisal-district search shows the tax-roll owner by address, parcel or account number, while the county recorder/clerk holds the recorded deeds. Both web searches are open; published fees are for copies (Travis: name search free, $1/page, $5 certification; Denver: certified $1 + $0.25/page). The tax-roll owner is not the deed. Bulk owner files exist (TCAD free export; Regrid paid); federal original patents are at BLM GLO.

Local search terms

  • deed / recorded instrument — Document transferring or encumbering land, filed with the county recorder or clerk
  • Clerk and Recorder / County Clerk (Recording Division) — County office that records and indexes deeds
  • Assessor / Central Appraisal District — Office that values property and keeps the tax roll with owner of record
  • Parcel ID / schedule number / account number — Identifiers used to search an assessor roll (Denver schedule number; TCAD account/property ID)
  • chain of title — Sequence of recorded transfers of a parcel
  • reception number / instrument number — Identifier of a recorded document (Denver / Travis)
  • land patent — Original federal title document, held in BLM GLO Records

Main caveats

  • Only Denver (CO) and Travis County (TX) were tested; other states use towns, parishes or registries of deeds and may differ. They were not checked.
  • The tax-roll owner is not the title: TCAD says its data is 'purely for ad valorem taxation purposes' and it corrects owners from deeds recorded with the County Clerk.
  • No recorded-document search was executed: Travis's search returned a Cloudflare challenge (403) to curl and Denver's guest entry could not be completed by script.
  • Owners can be withheld: TCAD's export layout has confidential-owner flags and Travis says records restricted by law or court order are not online.
  • Search keys differ: Denver's recorder search is by owner name (last, first); the assessor is by address, Parcel ID or schedule number. Look up the parcel first.
  • This card explains lookup of a specified parcel; do not build owner inventories from bulk exports (TCAD, Regrid).
