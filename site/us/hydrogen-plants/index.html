Best source is DOE Hydrogen Program Record 24001 (May 2024): 113 existing and planned US electrolyzer rows, 70 installed rows totalling about 116 MW (largest Woodbine GA, 40 MW), with city, kW and status only. It names no operator and gives no commissioning date; those came from company documents for two sites (Plug Power Woodbine, FPL Okeechobee). IEA's June 2026 project database needs an account and was not opened. Newer plants are missing from DOE's edition.

Confidence reason: Table counts reconcile with DOE's totals and two sites were traced to operator releases, but the list is 18 months old and the IEA file and most operators were not checked.

Local search terms

  • Electrolyzer Installations in the United States (DOE Hydrogen Program Record) — DOE record series 20009, 22001, 23003, 24001; Table 1 is the site list.
  • Hydrogen Production and Infrastructure Projects Database (IEA) — IEA global project file; download needs a free IEA account.
  • Installed/Operational vs Installed/Commissioning — Status values in DOE Table 1; plain 'Installed' has no qualifier.
  • Hydrogen Projects in the US (Clean Energy Group) — Tableau map of production projects by status and hydrogen colour.
  • PEM / alkaline / SOEC electrolyzer — Electrolyzer technologies named in DOE Table 1; most capacity is PEM.
  • Green hydrogen / electrolytic hydrogen — Terms used by operators such as Plug Power and FPL for electrolyzer output.
  • Kilowatts (kW) vs tons per day — DOE lists kW; operators quote MW or tons per day; do not convert one into the other.
  • NextGen Hydrogen Hub (FPL Cavendish) — Name of FPL's Okeechobee electrolyzer pilot.

Main caveats

  • DOE Record 24001 is dated May 2024 and is still the newest electrolyzer-installations record on DOE's program page; later plants and status changes are missing.
  • Table 1 gives kW per installation row without stating the electrical or output basis; hydrogen output (for example 15 tons per day at Woodbine) is a different measure.
  • No operator or commissioning date in the DOE table; 'Installed' rows include 46 units of 120-180 kW and 4 'Commissioning' rows, so installed is not proof of operation.
  • Woodbine GA appears three times (40 MW and 5 MW operational, 40 MW planned); rows are installations, not unique plants.
  • IEA's June 2026 database and Clean Energy Group's April 2025 map were not inspected beyond their pages; Plug Power's own release page blocks automated clients.
