United States data centers data sources

Where can I get a list of data centers in operation in the United States, with location, operator and capacity (IT power in MW or floor area)?

Answer

No unified U.S. federal registry of operating data centers exists. For free national structured data, start with FracTracker's U.S. Data Centers Tracker API (536 Operating sites with US coords, 353 operators, 423 MW/sq ft; non-commercial terms) and PNNL's IM3 Atlas GeoJSON (1,850 US centroids, 1,732 exterior sq ft, 1,199 operators, ODbL 1.0, no MW). Add Epoch AI (60 US operational AI sites with isolated IT MW, CC BY 4.0) and PeeringDB (1,381 US colocation facilities with operator/coords, no capacity). Authoritative official layers are localized (Loudoun GIS, VA DEQ, TX Comptroller). Paid Compute Atlas ($150/mo) and Cleanview ($4,900+) improve IT MW coverage.

Main caveats

  • Official data gap: U.S. federal agencies (DOE/LBNL, EIA, OMB) publish no public national facility inventory; DOE/LBNL's 2025 Update explicitly relies on proprietary market trackers (Omdia, IDC, S&P Global).
  • Capacity definition traps: FracTracker 'mw' reports total facility/build-out power demand rather than operational IT MW; IM3 'sqft' measures exterior OSM building/campus footprint, not interior IT floor space.
  • IT MW isolation: Among free sources, only Epoch AI (60 operational US frontier AI facilities) isolates IT power (MW) from total facility power; Compute Atlas ($150/mo) and Cleanview ($4,900+) cover broader IT MW.
  • License and completeness: FracTracker prohibits commercial use in its Terms of Service and undercounts smaller/older colocation facilities; PeeringDB covers 1,381 colocation sites but lacks MW/sq ft and private hyperscalers.
  • Sub-national fragmentation: Official county/state datasets cover high-density pockets (Loudoun GIS 187 built buildings with sq ft; VA DEQ 98 operating sites; TX Comptroller 182 tax-certified sites without coords/capacity).

Local search terms

  • Term
    IT power / critical IT load (MW)
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Electrical power delivered specifically to compute/server racks, excluding cooling and PUE overhead. Isolated in Epoch AI (free) and Compute Atlas / Cleanview (paid), unlike FracTracker gross facility MW.
  • Term
    Total facility power / gross MW
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Full substation or behind-the-meter electric load serving both IT and cooling systems (FracTracker 'mw', utility load requests); typically 1.15x-1.5x critical IT MW and often reported at full multi-phase build-out.
  • Term
    White space vs. gross building sq ft
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Gross permitted building floor area (Loudoun GIS SQUARE_FEET) includes mechanical/power halls, whereas IM3/OSM 'sqft' measures exterior building or campus parcel polygon footprints rather than net raised-floor IT white space.
  • Term
    Qualifying Data Center
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Texas Comptroller tax-exemption status requiring >=100,000 sq ft plus capital investment and job commitments; registration does not guarantee current online operation.
  • Term
    Development of Regional Impact (DRI)
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Georgia DCA regional planning filing for large proposed developments (including hyperscale data centers), reporting planned building square footage prior to construction.
  • Term
    DCOI (Data Center Optimization Initiative)
    Language
    en
    Meaning
    Defunct OMB program (M-16-19 / M-19-19, ended Oct 2020) tracking federal government-owned data centers; ITDashboard.gov is sunsetting effective April 2026 and WhiteHouse/datacenters was archived in 2022.
