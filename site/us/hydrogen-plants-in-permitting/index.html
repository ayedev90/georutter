United States hydrogen plants in permitting data sources

Where can I get a list of hydrogen production projects currently in permitting in the United States, with location, capacity (MW), developer, permitting stage and date?

Answer

No single list gives stage and date for electrolysis projects in permitting. DOE Record 24001 (May 2024) lists 43 planned rows without developers or dates; stage, developer and MW come from dockets: Lancaster Clean Energy Center (CEQA approval 2024-03-11, 400 MWe) and Plug Power's Graham, Texas project (DOE FONSI 2025-01-10, twelve 10 MW; agreed for sale July 2026). DOE hub pages are hub-level and stale; the PNWH2 award was reported rescinded in October 2025.

Local search terms

  • CEQAnet / Notice of Determination (NOD) / Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) — California portal and documents; open each document of the SCH number for stage and date.
  • DOE/EA-#### Final Environmental Assessment and FONSI — DOE NEPA documents for financed projects; energy.gov/nepa pages carry the applicant, site and permits.
  • Regional Clean Hydrogen Hubs (H2Hubs) — Seven DOE-selected hubs; hub pages are consortium-level.
  • Hydrogen Program Record 24001 — DOE table of installed and planned electrolyzers; planned rows have no developer.
  • Conditional Use Permit (CUP) No. ... — City or county use permit that appears as the CEQAnet title (for example CUP 23-019).
  • Loan Programs Office (LPO) conditional commitment / loan guarantee — DOE financing step referred to in the Graham EA; a FONSI is not the financing decision.
  • Permit by Rule (PBR), TCEQ — Texas air authorisation noted in the Graham EA (coverage began September 2023); no public docket was found.
  • retain/modify list — DOE's 2026 list of awards it may keep; CATF reports it covers five of seven hubs.

Main caveats

  • DOE Record 24001 is dated May 2024: it lists Graham, TX as Planned/Under Construction although Plug Power agreed in July 2026 to sell that project; it has no developer or stage date.
  • A DOE FONSI (Graham, 2025-01-10) and a CEQA approval (Lancaster, 2024-03-11) are not financing, construction or operation: no building or start-of-construction document was found for either.
  • MW bases differ: 120 MW of electrolyzers, 45 tpd of hydrogen and 164 MW of grid interconnection describe Graham; Lancaster's '400 MWe' is undefined. None converts into another.
  • DOE hub pages predate the 2025 cuts (Canary Media: awards to two hubs rescinded) and show no electrolyzer-level project, MW or permit; DOE's EIS-0571 dates are anticipated only.
  • Only California and Texas dockets were opened; Washington, Oregon and other state portals and the IEA project file (account required) were not checked.
