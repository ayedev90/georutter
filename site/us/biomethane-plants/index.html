United States biomethane plants data sources

Where can I get a list of biogas and biomethane plants in operation in the United States, with location, production capacity, feedstock, operator and whether they inject into the gas grid?

Answer

No single national plant-level list was obtained. Best structured sources: EPA LMOP (110 operating landfill-gas RNG projects, 101 pipeline injection, exact landfill coordinates, Sept 2024), EPA AgSTAR (400 operating livestock digesters, 56 listing pipeline gas, June 2024) and EPA's Part 80 registry (502 registered biogas-producer facilities with address; registration is not operation). No source publishes RNG output capacity; wastewater and food-waste plants lack plant-level data; Argonne's RNG list blocks automated access.

Local search terms

  • Landfill Methane Outreach Program (LMOP) database — EPA landfill and landfill-gas-energy project files; filter Project Type Category 'Renewable Natural Gas'.
  • AgSTAR Livestock Anaerobic Digester Database — EPA list of livestock-farm digesters; filter Biogas End Use 'Pipeline Gas'.
  • LCFS Current Fuel Pathways / Bio-CNG — CARB pathway list; Fuel Category Bio-CNG or Bio-LNG shows biomethane producers by facility.
  • Renewable natural gas (RNG) — Biogas upgraded to pipeline quality; the US term for biomethane.
  • Pipeline Injection / Local Use — LMOP 'RNG Delivery Method' values.
  • Anaerobic digester (AD) — Digester on a farm, wastewater plant or food-waste site that produces biogas.
  • Landfill gas (LFG) — Biogas from municipal solid waste landfills; LMOP counts it in mmscfd.
  • Upgrader / high-Btu gas plant — Plant that cleans biogas to pipeline quality (for example 'Milam High Btu Gas Plant' in CARB).

Gas-grid injection grid_injectionPartially covered

  • epa-agstar
  • carb-lcfs-pathways

LMOP flags Pipeline Injection (101) vs Local Use (9); AgSTAR 'Pipeline Gas' on 56 operating rows; CARB/Oregon text gives routes. Actual injection not confirmed.

Not checked: Injection was not confirmed against utility or interconnection records; the Rosendale check shows injection can occur at a terminal away from the farm.

Main caveats

  • LMOP is dated September 2024 and AgSTAR June 2024; both say they are not exhaustive. Status can lag: AgSTAR still lists Rosendale RNG as under construction.
  • Capacity is not published as RNG output: LMOP gives landfill-gas input and AgSTAR raw biogas estimates; neither is upgrading-plant capacity.
  • Digester, upgrading plant and injection point can be different places (Rosendale: farm digester, upgrader at Pickett WI, injection at DTE's Newton terminal).
  • AgSTAR map points are approximated from city and state; LMOP coordinates are the landfill's, not the RNG plant's.
  • CARB and Oregon lists certify pathways and EPA Part 80 lists registrations, not operating plants; wastewater and food-waste plants have no capacity or feedstock source here.
