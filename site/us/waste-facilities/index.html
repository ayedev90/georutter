Confidence

EPA publishes separate national lists: LMOP for MSW landfills (2,641 with coordinates, operator, open/closed status and design capacity in short tons; file of September 2024), GHGRP Subpart HH for larger landfills (capacity in metric tons, open/closed per year; 1,125 in 2023), and RCRAInfo for hazardous waste treatment, storage and disposal facilities (636 operating TSDFs typed by process, no capacity or operator in the ECHO download). Non-hazardous treatment sites (transfer, composting, processing) are in state systems such as California's SWIS.

Confidence reason: EPA LMOP, GHGRP and ECHO RCRAInfo were downloaded/queried and cross-checked on one landfill; non-hazardous treatment facilities and TSDF capacities were not reached nationally.

Local search terms

  • LMOP Database — EPA Landfill Methane Outreach Program list of MSW landfills.
  • Subpart HH (GHGRP) — EPA greenhouse gas reporting for MSW landfills; Envirofacts table HH_LANDFILL_INFO.
  • RCRAInfo / OPERATING_TSDF — EPA hazardous waste database and its operating treatment, storage and disposal facility flag.
  • SWIS — California Solid Waste Information System of permitted sites and activities.
  • TSDF — Treatment, storage and disposal facility (hazardous waste).
  • MSW landfill — Municipal solid waste landfill.
  • design capacity / waste in place — Authorised/design volume as mass vs accumulated waste.
  • permitted throughput — Maximum daily intake allowed by a permit, distinct from actual tonnage.

Main caveats

  • No single national list: MSW landfills (LMOP, GHGRP), hazardous TSDFs (RCRAInfo) and other solid waste facilities (states) are separate universes.
  • Capacity units differ: LMOP short tons vs GHGRP metric tons (Sunshine Canyon 114,148,537 short t vs 103,536,088.2 metric t); design capacity is not annual throughput.
  • LMOP is not exhaustive and its newest file is September 2024; GHGRP latest year on Envirofacts is 2023 and covers only larger landfills.
  • RCRAInfo rows are sites (handler IDs); operating TSDF status summarises units; capacities and operators need the RCRAInfo permitting/handler modules.
  • Operator names differ by source: EPA lists parent companies (Republic Services, Inc.), California SWIS the permitted entity (Browning-Ferris Industries of California, Inc.).
