USAspending.gov is the official register of federal grants: each award has recipient, FAIN, Assistance Listing and funding opportunity, net obligation, outlays (since FY2022), action date and place of performance (API from FY2008, archives from FY2001). Energy projects must be filtered by programme (e.g. DOE 81.254, USDA 10.868). DOE programme pages separate selections from awards (GRIP: 60 awarded, 44 selected). State grants are separate, e.g. NYSERDA's solar incentives on data.ny.gov.

Confidence reason: USAspending API, award detail, transactions and archive tested; definitions read in its data dictionary; DOE list reconciled with one award. State coverage sampled (New York only).

Local search terms

  • Assistance Listing (formerly CFDA) number — Programme code used to filter energy grants in USAspending, e.g. 81.254, 10.868.
  • Federal Award Identification Number (FAIN) — Award ID of financial assistance; key to match agency lists with USAspending.
  • Funding Opportunity Announcement (FOA) — Call under which the award was made, e.g. DE-FOA-0003195.
  • Selected for award negotiations — DOE selection stage before an award is signed; not an obligation.
  • Obligation / de-obligation — Legally binding commitment of federal funds and its reduction, recorded per transaction.
  • Outlay — Payment made to liquidate an obligation; reported per award since FY2022.
  • Primary place of performance — Location where the award is predominantly performed, distinct from recipient address.
  • Cooperative agreement — Assistance award type (05) with substantial federal involvement, counted with grants.

Main caveats

  • Award Amount in USAspending is the net obligation; selections announced by DOE are not obligations and payments (outlays) are a separate field reported only since FY2022.
  • DOE terminated 321 awards (223 projects) announced 2025-10-02; terminated awards remain in USAspending with their transaction history.
  • Grants (types 02-05) must be separated from loans and other assistance; DOE research cooperative agreements are not energy projects.
  • State-funded grants (e.g. NYSERDA) are outside USAspending and vary by state.
