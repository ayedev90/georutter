No official national list of mobile antenna sites with operator and technology was found. The FCC publishes two partial files: ULS Cellular-service licenses (24,366 transmitter locations with licensee, coordinates and ASR link; market-based bands list almost no sites) and the Antenna Structure Registration (142,039 standing registered structures with owner, usually a tower company, but no tenants or technology). States and cities fill gaps locally, e.g. Connecticut's Siting Council (carrier, structure owner, 5G flag; no coordinates) and city small-cell datasets.

Confidence reason: FCC ASR and ULS files downloaded and joined; state and city sources tested. FCC documentation was blocked (403) so field codes were inferred from data and the HIFLD schema.

Local search terms

  • ULS complete files (l_cell.zip, l_market.zip) — FCC Universal Licensing System weekly bulk downloads on data.fcc.gov.
  • r_tower.zip — FCC Antenna Structure Registration weekly bulk file.
  • Antenna Structure Registration (ASR) — FCC registry of structures requiring FAA notification (47 CFR 17.4).
  • LO record — ULS location record with coordinates and ASR number.
  • small cell / DAS — Low-power nodes on poles; tracked in city permit datasets, not ASR.
  • neutral host — Infrastructure provider hosting several carriers (Boston field value).
  • Cellular (CL) radio service — FCC site-based mobile license service code with location records.
  • Siting Council — Connecticut state body that approves tower sites and tower sharing.

Not published: No national per-site LTE/5G field found in FCC files; OpenCelliD (per-cell data) requires a token and was not tested.

Main caveats

  • ASR registers structures needing FAA notice (over 200 ft or near airports): no antennas, tenants or technology; owner is usually a tower company.
  • ULS site locations exist only for Cellular (CL) licenses; PCS/AWS/700 MHz market licenses are area-based and list almost no sites.
  • Shared sites appear once per license/call sign (ULS) or per Council action (Connecticut); deduplicate by coordinates or address.
  • Licensee and carrier names are historical legal entities (Cellco Partnership, New Cingular, Sprint), not current brands.
  • fcc.gov pages and tower company site locators (American Tower, SBA) block automated clients; data.fcc.gov bulk downloads work.

Document access

Per-site tenants and technology appear mainly in state and local siting records (dockets, petitions, permits); Connecticut publishes them as a dataset. Other states' dockets and FAA OE/AAA filings were not tested, so no manual recipe is given.
