No single list of energy-project environmental decisions was found. The best structured sources are BLM's National NEPA Register (federal land: applicant, decision date, EA/EIS type), California's CEQAnet (CSV with state notices of determination) and DOE and BOEM decision documents (FONSI, ROD). EPA's EIS database is national but lists only Draft and Final EIS filings, with no applicant or decision. A filing, draft or 'Completed' status is not an approval.

Confidence reason: Five sources queried or read and one decision reproduced end to end; FERC blocked, other agencies and states unchecked; decision documents were opened only for DOE and BOEM.

Local search terms

  • Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) — Detailed federal NEPA analysis; Draft then Final, then a Record of Decision
  • Record of Decision (ROD) — Agency decision document after a Final EIS
  • Environmental Assessment (EA) — Shorter NEPA analysis that ends in a FONSI or an EIS
  • Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) — Finding that no EIS is required; a NEPA finding, not a permit
  • Notice of Availability (NOA) — Federal Register notice that a Draft or Final EIS is available
  • Notice of Determination (NOD) — California CEQA notice recording that an agency approved a project
  • Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) — California CEQA document proposing a finding of no significant impact; its review period precedes the decision
  • Right-of-way (ROW) application — Request to use federal land, e.g. for a transmission line, assessed in a BLM EA or EIS

Main caveats

  • EPA's EIS database lists filings (Draft or Final) with a Federal Register date, not decisions: Lava Ridge Wind Final EIS is dated 2024-06-07, while BLM's decision date is 2024-12-04.
  • BLM 'Completed - Completed' means the NEPA process ended; the outcome (FONSI, decision record, ROD) is in the documents, and a later action can reverse an approval, as with Lava Ridge.
  • CEQAnet 'Received' is the filing date: San Luis West Solar's NOD shows approval on 2026-02-27 but was received 2026-11-05; MND and NOP entries are notices, not decisions.
  • A FONSI records a finding of no significant impact. A ROD records the agency's decision after an EIS. Verify any separate permits or decisions required from other agencies.
  • Coverage is by agency and state: FERC returned a 403 challenge, and other states, NRC and USACE were not checked; EPA's NEPA compliance database covers only EPA's own actions.
