No national list of solar PV projects in permitting exists in the sources examined; permitting is split across federal (BLM) and state or local bodies. Best entry points: state registers (NY ORES, CEC Opt-In) and BLM ePlanning for federal land, plus EIA-860M as a national lead (1,100 planned PV generators with coordinates and approval-status codes, not a permit file).

Confidence reason: Two state registers and a national lead were read; BLM records and the federal dashboard were not retrievable and most states were not examined.

Local search terms

  • Major Renewable Energy Facility Siting Permit — New York Article VIII permit issued by ORES.
  • Opt-In Certification — California route where the CEC certifies a project instead of local permits.
  • NEPA / EIS / EA / ROD / FONSI / DNA — Federal environmental review documents and decisions.
  • Right-of-way (ROW) — BLM authorisation to use public land for a project.
  • Notice of Intent (NOI) — Start of an EIS process.

Main caveats

  • No single federal body lists all projects; BLM covers only projects needing BLM action and state or county permits sit elsewhere.
  • EIA-860M status is survey-reported and undated, and the entity is not always the developer; the same applies to interconnection queues.
  • NY list and docket can disagree (Addison Solar 120 vs 105 MW); read the docket for the authoritative figures.
  • Only NY, California opt-in and two BLM pages were examined; Texas, Midwest, Southeast, Arizona and counties are pending.
  • BLM register records and the federal permitting dashboard could not be read from this environment.
