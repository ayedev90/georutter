USGS MRDS is the national point inventory: 266,599 US mines, prospects and occurrences with commodity and development status, but systematic updates stopped in 2011 and position precision varies. Tonnage and grade exist only in its relational tables for about 2,100 US records. USGS USMIN (13 critical-mineral commodities) and the Critical Mineral Deposits compilation (1,271 deposits, ver. 3.0, 2026) add dated status, resources and grade; Alaska's ARDF and state surveys such as Nevada's add regional detail.

Confidence reason: MRDS, USMIN WFS and ARDF were downloaded and counted; ScienceBase files, OSM and most state surveys were not tested.

Local search terms

  • MRDS — Mineral Resources Data System, USGS national inventory of mineral sites (frozen 2011).
  • USMIN — USGS mineral deposit database project; also the topographic-map mine-feature layer.
  • ARDF — Alaska Resource Data File.
  • Earth MRI — USGS Earth Mapping Resources Initiative, behind the critical-mineral deposit compilation.
  • occurrence / prospect / past producer — MRDS development-status classes.
  • critical minerals — USGS list of mineral commodities used to scope USMIN and the deposit compilation.

Main caveats

  • MRDS has had no systematic updates since 2011; its dev_stat is the status at data entry and does not show whether a site is operating today.
  • MRDS positions range from 7.5-minute map plots to imprecise report locations; USGS says about 4,000 records have no reliable coordinates.
  • Size and grade are in separate relational tables (Resources, Resource_detail) for about 2,100 of 266,599 US MRDS records; the flattened CSV has neither.
  • Grades can be sample, production or resource figures; USMIN states older estimates may not follow NI 43-101 or JORC, so they are descriptive only.
  • USMIN deposits cover 13 critical-mineral commodities only; its WFS layer lists features (deposits, mines, districts) without status or resources.
  • ScienceBase (hosting USMIN packages and Critical Mineral Deposits ver. 3.0 files) returned HTTP 403 to automated curl requests in this test.
