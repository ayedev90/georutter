Start with EIA-860M. Its September 2026 Planned sheet had 77 wind generator rows across 71 plants. Only 17 generator rows carried code L (regulatory approvals pending). The file includes capacity, coordinates and planned operation dates. Check the actual permitting stage and date in the relevant agency files, such as BLM, BOEM or New York ORES.

Confidence reason: EIA and BOEM data downloaded/queried; BLM and ORES read from pages and a portal endpoint. Only four jurisdictions checked; other states and the FAST-41 dashboard were not readable.

Local search terms

  • NEPA Register — BLM public list of NEPA projects (EA, EIS, CE, DNA) and their decision documents
  • Record of Decision (ROD) — Federal decision document after an EIS; the stage a NEPA 'Completed' status usually refers to
  • Construction and Operations Plan (COP) — BOEM-reviewed plan whose approval authorises offshore wind construction
  • Notice of Intent (NOI) — Start of a federal EIS process or a New York ORES filing; not an approval
  • Article VIII / Section 94-c permit — New York siting permit for major renewable energy facilities issued by ORES
  • Planned generator — EIA-860M term for a proposed unit, with status codes L, T, P, U, V, TS
  • OCS-A lease — BOEM commercial wind lease number on the outer continental shelf
  • Repower — Replacing turbines at an existing wind farm; may be listed as a project without being new capacity

Main caveats

  • EIA-860M only lists generators reported to or researched by EIA; early-stage and unreported projects can be absent, and capacities are 'preliminary' and silently revised.
  • EIA status (L, T, P, U, V, TS) is not a permit record: Heritage Wind (NY) shows 'P regulatory approvals not initiated' at 126 MW while ORES lists a permit for 184.8 MW.
  • BLM 'Completed - Completed' means the NEPA process ended, not that a project stands approved: Lava Ridge has ROD 12/4/2024 and a later DOI statement on cancelling it.
  • BLM, BOEM and ORES each cover only their jurisdiction (federal land, outer continental shelf, one state); wind on private land with no federal or state siting nexus is not in them.
  • Queue entries, leases and lease areas (BOEM lease layer: 4,892 block polygons) are leads, not authorisations.

Document access

Stage dates, applicants and approvals sit in agency project pages and documents, not in the national list. Build the list from EIA-860M, then confirm each project in the agency record that has jurisdiction.
