EIA's Form EIA-860 (final 2025 data, released 2026-09-10) is the national generator-level table: plant coordinates, technology and fuel, nameplate and net MW, status and operator. Tested: 11,194 fossil and nuclear generators at 3,128 plants (96 nuclear units at 56 plants). EIA-860M (September 2026) updates status monthly, including planned and retired units. EIA-923 gives 2025 net generation in MWh by plant, prime mover and fuel, nuclear by unit. All join on plant code; Vogtle (649) and Scherer (6257) were cross-checked. NRC pages blocked scripted access.

Confidence reason: EIA-860, 860M and 923 files were downloaded, parsed and cross-checked on two plants; the thermal subset is my technology filter and NRC pages were unreachable.

Local search terms

  • Plant Code (Plant ID / Plant Id) — EIA's plant identifier, same across EIA-860, 860M and 923.
  • Nameplate vs Net summer/winter capacity — Nameplate is the generator rating; net summer/winter are net capacities in those seasons.
  • Prime mover — Machine type driving the generator (ST steam turbine, GT gas turbine, CC combined cycle...).
  • Utility (EIA-860) — Company responsible for day-to-day operations, i.e. the operator, not necessarily the owner.

Main caveats

  • Counting units differ: EIA-860 is per generator (27,919 operable), EIA-923 per plant x prime mover x fuel (nuclear by unit), so MWh do not map one to one to generators.
  • Capacity bases differ (nameplate vs net summer/winter MW); EIA-923 reports net generation only, no gross column seen.
  • Operator is the day-to-day operator (Utility ID); owners appear only in Schedule 4 for joint or third-party ownership.
  • EIA-860M capacities are preliminary and corrected silently; latest published file was September 2026 (October-December names redirect).
  • No thermal-only table exists: 11,194 generators / 3,128 plants come from my Technology filter (fossil + nuclear; biomass, waste, geothermal excluded).
  • NRC reactor pages refused scripted access (403); licensee and thermal-power data from NRC were not tested.

eia-860-annualAnnual Electric Generator Report, Form EIA-860 (detailed data)

U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) · Official · Zip of Excel (.xlsx) workbooks: Utility, Plant, Generator (Operable/Proposed/Retired and Canceled), Wind, Solar, Energy Storage, Multifuel, Owner, environmental files; Layout and instructions · Direct download · License: Unknown

EIA's annual generator survey: every generator at plants of 1 MW or more with plant coordinates, technology and fuel, nameplate and net MW, status and operator. Tested: 11,194 fossil and nuclear generators at 3,128 plants.

Example request

curl -L -o eia8602025.zip https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/xls/eia8602025.zip && unzip eia8602025.zip -d eia860_2025  # then join 2___Plant_Y2025.xlsx and 3_1_Generator_Y2025.xlsx (sheet Operable) on Plant Code (e.g. 649)
