No public US dataset gives full transmission and distribution routes with diameter, pressure, and status. For open national transmission geometry, operator, and operating status, use EIA's ArcGIS FeatureServer (32,892 lines, no diameter/pressure/distribution). PHMSA's statutory NPMS collects authoritative transmission geometry, DIAMETER, and AF/AU/PA status, but restricts GIS exports and DIAMETER to government/operators (public viewer is scale-capped at 1:24,000 and excludes distribution and MAOP). Select states add transmission detail (Texas RRC exposes diameter and status); GEM GGIT and OSM partially fill transmission diameter; distribution mains and MAOP remain non-public.

Confidence reason: Verified across 49 CFR 191 / 18 CFR 260.8 on eCFR, NPMS standards/data dictionary, live API queries on EIA and 7 states, 2 Geofabrik OSM extracts, GEM XLSX/headers, Rextag landing page, and 32 searches.

Local search terms

  • NPMS (National Pipeline Mapping System) — Mandatory PHMSA federal GIS database for gas transmission and LNG under 49 CFR 191.29; excludes distribution and gathering.
  • PIMMA — NPMS Pipeline Information Management and Mapping Application; restricted MFA web GIS for government officials and pipeline operators.
  • OPID — Unique 5-digit PHMSA Operator Identification number used across NPMS and annual reports.
  • MAOP (Maximum Allowable Operating Pressure) — Maximum operating pressure limit; collected on FERC Form 567 (>100,000 Mcf/d) as non-public CEII, absent from NPMS.
  • CEII (Critical Energy Infrastructure Information) — FERC security classification exempting detailed pipeline engineering/flow diagrams (like Form 567) from public FOIA disclosure.
  • LDC (Local Distribution Company) — Regulated utility operating local natural gas distribution mains and service lines; 1,335 operators in PHMSA GD AR 2024.
  • 811 / One-Call — State-mandated pre-excavation utility locate service; the only mechanism to obtain exact local distribution line markings on the ground.
  • FERC Form 567 — Annual System Flow Diagram filing under 18 CFR 260.8 showing diameter and MAOP for major interstate gas pipelines.

Main caveats

  • NPMS excludes gas distribution and gathering entirely (49 CFR 191.29), restricts GIS downloads to govt/operators, caps Public Viewer at 1:24,000 with +/-500 ft accuracy, and withholds DIAMETER from the public.
  • Open national EIA FeatureServer (32,892 features) derives from FERC Form 567 and public sources (~2020 snapshot reviewed by Esri in 2025): no diameter, no pressure, only Status='Operating', and 0 Gathering despite text claims.
  • Pressure (MAOP) is absent from NPMS, EIA, and TX RRC; FERC Form 567 (18 CFR 260.8) MAOP flow diagrams are withheld as non-public Critical Energy Infrastructure Information (CEII).
  • Exact local distribution geometry is withheld for security nationwide (only CO PUC PSPMS exposes one state in a scale-capped viewer); use 811 One-Call for excavation locates or dockets for individual projects.
