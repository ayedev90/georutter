United States solar pv plants data sources

Where can I get a list of solar PV plants in operation in the United States, with location, capacity (MW), operator and commissioning date?

Answer

EIA-860 (final 2025) lists 7,979 operating PV generators at 7,309 plants (152,848 MW AC) in 49 states and DC, with plant coordinates, operator (Utility Name) and commercial operation month and year. EIA-860M (September 2026): 8,335 generators, 7,651 plants, 170,562 MW AC, plus Puerto Rico on separate sheets. USGS USPVDB v4.0 (14 April 2026), downloaded from USGS, maps 6,611 ground-mounted facilities of 1 MW or more linked to EIA plant codes. Under 1 MW there is no plant list: EIA gives estimates by state; LBNL's Tracking the Sun covers about 4.0 million systems by ZIP code, with no operator.

Local search terms

  • Nameplate Capacity (MW) — EIA AC nameplate of a generator; for PV, the sum of AC ratings of the array.
  • DC Net Capacity (MW) — Net DC capacity under STC (1000 W/m2, 25 °C); solar sheet only.
  • Status OP — Operating – in service (commercial operation).
  • Utility Name — Company responsible for the day-to-day operations of the generator (EIA-860 layout).
  • Plant Code / eia_id — EIA plant identifier; USPVDB calls it eia_id, EIA-860M calls it Plant ID.
  • Small-scale solar — PV systems under 1 MW, typically on rooftops; estimated by EIA, not listed per plant.
  • Large-scale PV facility (USPVDB) — USPVDB scope: ground-mounted PV of 1 MW or more (threshold on DC).
  • Distributed solar (LBNL) — Rooftop systems and ground-mounted systems up to 5 MW AC or 7 MW DC, in Tracking the Sun.

Not published: No per-plant date for under-1 MW PV in EIA; Tracking the Sun has an installation date, sometimes a proxy such as an incentive claim (guide read). Topaz: EIA first generator 04-2013, USPVDB 2014, LBNL COD 2014-10-31.

Main caveats

  • Scope: EIA-860 and EIA-860M cover plants of 1 MW or more combined nameplate; 76 PV plants in the annual file have under 1 MW AC of PV (47 list other generators, 29 none). Small-scale PV has no EIA plant list.
  • Capacity: EIA nameplate is AC; DC at STC is in the solar sheet (198,471 MW DC against 152,848 MW AC). USPVDB threshold is on DC: 52 of 6,611 records have AC under 1 MW, none have DC under 1 MW.
  • Date: EIA 'Operating Month/Year' is initial commercial operation per generator; the plant date used here is the earliest. LBNL COD for Topaz (2014-10-31) matches the latest generator. USPVDB p_year is not independent of EIA.
  • Operator: 'Utility Name' is the company responsible for day-to-day operations, not the owner. 'Entity Name' in EIA-860M differs from it for 190 of 8,307 generators in annual plants.
  • Location: EIA gives a central point of the property; USPVDB v4.0 gives polygons and a representative point (median gap 0.22 km; 213 matched plants above 5 km; maximum 333 km).
  • Version: USPVDB v4.0 contains 6,611 facilities; 57 do not match any operating PV plant in the 2025 or September 2026 EIA files, and the EIA files hold 1,064 PV plants of 1 MW or more that v4.0 does not map.
