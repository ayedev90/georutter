Confidence
Medium

No federal renewable or storage auction or national award list was found; results are published state by state. Best structured source: NYSERDA open data (data.ny.gov dprp-55ye), one row per award with RFP code, technology, county/ZIP, contracted MW, REC strike price and status; NY bulk storage awards are in ugya-enpy (kW, address, no price). Illinois publishes an awards workbook plus ICC notices with average prices; New Jersey BPU orders give per-project prices; California lists utility contracts without price; Massachusetts prices sit in DPU dockets that block automated clients.

Confidence reason: Five state programs tested with real records; other procuring states (CT, RI, ME, MD, VA, utility all-source RFPs) not checked; key NYSERDA and MA DPU pages blocked to automated clients.

Local search terms

  • solicitation / Request for Proposals (RFP) — Name used for state renewable and storage procurement rounds; NYSERDA codes them as RESRFP25-1, ORECRFP23-1, etc.
  • Public Notice of Successful Bidders and Average Prices — Title of the Illinois Commerce Commission notice that publishes each IPA procurement result and its average prices.
  • long-term contract filing (Section 83C / 83D / 83E) — Massachusetts statutory procurements (offshore wind, clean energy, storage) whose contracts are filed for DPU approval.
  • Garden State Energy Storage Program (GSESP) — New Jersey BPU storage incentive solicitation, awarded by board order.
  • Renewable Auction Mechanism (RAM) — California utility auction programme; rounds RAM 1 to RAM 7 appear in the CPUC RPS public data.
  • Index REC strike price — Bid price in NY and IL indexed REC contracts; the payment is the strike minus a market index.
  • OREC (Offshore Renewable Energy Certificate) — Product bought in NY, NJ and MD offshore wind solicitations.
  • Indexed Storage Credit (ISC) — Illinois storage contract settled against energy and capacity index prices.

Main caveats

  • Selection, contract filing, approval and cancellation are separate stages: NY open data keeps 147 cancelled awards; MA 83E selected 1,268 MW but filed contracts for 1,118 MW; MA offshore wind rounds II-III were cancelled.
  • Prices are not comparable across mechanisms: NY Index vs Fixed REC, IL indexed REC/ISC strike (event average, $/MWh), NJ storage $/MW-year, DOE table mixing PPA and OREC levelized prices.
  • NY open data leaves the price blank for RESRFP24-1 and later awards until commercial operation; 83 of 326 rows carry no price.
  • Storage awards are published in MW of power; MWh is absent from NY bulk storage, IL and NJ award records and appears only in MA news and bids.
  • NYSERDA's website, the MA DPU fileroom, FERC and LBNL pages return 403 to automated clients; data.ny.gov, ICC/IPA, NJ BPU and CPUC files were reachable.
