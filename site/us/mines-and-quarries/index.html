MSHA's weekly Mines file is the national list: 6,671 mines with Active status (6,096 metal/non-metal including stone, sand and gravel; 575 coal) with coordinates, operator, county and primary commodity; 6,492 of them reported work hours in 2025. Annual production per mine is published only for coal (its 2025 total matches EIA's 527.6 million short tons); quarries have hours but no tonnage. Pennsylvania DEP lists tons for 898 noncoal mines (2025).

Confidence reason: MSHA, Pennsylvania and California files were downloaded and counted and coal totals matched EIA; USGS NMIC and most states were not opened.

Local search terms

  • MSHA — Mine Safety and Health Administration, issues the 7-digit mine id.
  • M/NM (metal/non-metal) — MSHA class that includes stone, sand, gravel and other nonmetal mines.
  • Legal ID form (LID) / Mine Information Form (MIF) — Forms from which MSHA mine name, operator and status are taken.
  • SMARA — California Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975.
  • Canvass code — MSHA industry group code (Coal, Stone, SandAndGravel, Nonmetal, Metal).
  • short ton — 2,000 lb unit used by EIA for coal; MSHA does not state a unit.

Main caveats

  • MSHA 'Active' is a registry status, not proof of extraction: 6,492 of 6,671 Active mines reported hours in 2025, but so did 4,672 Intermittent and 745 Abandoned mines.
  • MSHA ANNUAL_COAL_PROD holds coal only; its unit is not in the definition file (its 2025 sum equals EIA's 527.6 million short tons).
  • Quarry and metal mines appear in the MSHA production file with hours and employees; ANNUAL_COAL_PROD is nonzero in only 113 non-coal rows over 2000-2026.
  • Pennsylvania repeats the same tonnage across permits of one site and its site rows sum to 103,947,203 tons against a printed total of 103,945,203.
  • MSHA 'Facility' mines (482 Active) are mills or plants, and 1,171 Active mines are portable operations, so a point may not be the pit.
  • State registries (CA SMARA, NV) record permit or operator-reported status; California says it cannot guarantee operator-reported coordinates.
