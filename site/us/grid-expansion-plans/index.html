PJM's live XML, CAISO's annual plan and status workbooks, and MISO's MTEP package list regional upgrades and give project descriptions, status and estimated in-service dates. The sources checked give locations by name, state or occasional point. No downloadable route geometry or national dataset was verified. Filter out equipment-only, canceled and completed work when looking for new lines and substations.

Confidence reason: Three operators downloaded and parsed; ERCOT blocked, ISO-NE partial, NYISO, SPP, BPA unopened. Claims are limited to the operators read; no route data was found.

Local search terms

  • RTEP (Regional Transmission Expansion Plan) — PJM's regional plan; its upgrades have b-numbers such as b3780.2
  • MTEP (MISO Transmission Expansion Plan) — MISO's annual plan; Appendix A lists new projects recommended for approval
  • TPP (Transmission Planning Process) — CAISO's annual cycle producing the Board-approved transmission plan
  • TDF (Transmission Development Forum) — CAISO's twice-yearly status review of approved projects
  • PTO (Participating Transmission Owner) — Utility that builds and owns a CAISO-approved project, e.g. PG&E or SCE
  • ISD (in-service date) — Expected date a project enters service
  • Baseline, Supplemental, Network upgrade — PJM project types: regional reliability, owner-driven, and generator-interconnection upgrades
  • Re-scope — A previously approved project whose scope or cost was changed in a later plan

Main caveats

  • No national list was found: PJM, CAISO and MISO were read, ERCOT was blocked (403), ISO-NE only via a PDF update, and NYISO, SPP, BPA and individual utilities were not opened.
  • Files give names and state, not routes: only 16 of 15,816 PJM rows embed a coordinate; CAISO and MISO give substation names and text; CAISO route maps are images in the plan.
  • The 19 Nov 2026 PJM snapshot had 15,816 upgrades and 5,637 pending or active records. Filter out breakers, relays, reconductoring and other work outside the scope of new lines and substations.
  • Listing or approval is not construction: PJM Board approval, MISO 'Appendix A Approved' and CAISO plan approval precede permits; expected in-service dates are revised often.
  • Versions change under one URL: CAISO's main plan URL now serves the 2026-08-27 update, MISO's MTEP26 package is a draft, and PJM's XML is overwritten in place.
