United States hydropower plants data sources

Where can I get a list of hydropower plants and dams in the United States, with location, capacity (MW), reservoir volume, operator and annual generation?

Answer

No single official table links hydro plants and dams. EIA-860 (2025) lists 1,457 hydro plants (3,981 conventional and 150 pumped-storage generators) with coordinates, MW and operator; EIA-923 adds 2025 net MWh, negative at many pumped-storage plants. USACE's National Inventory of Dams (CSV updated 2026-11-20; 92,674 rows, 2,529 with a hydroelectric purpose) gives dam location, owner and normal/maximum storage in acre-feet but no MW or EIA code. A coordinate join matched 967 of 1,457 EIA plants within 1 km of an NID hydro dam. ORNL's EHA database sits behind a form.

Local search terms

  • NID ID — National Inventory of Dams identifier (e.g. MD00097), one per dam or associated structure.
  • Acre-foot (acre-ft) — Reservoir volume unit used by NID; about 1,233.5 cubic metres.
  • Pumped storage (PS) — Hydro plant that pumps water uphill; EIA-923 net generation can be negative.

Plant or dam location locationPartially covered
  • eia-860-hydro
  • nid-usace
EIA-860 plant points and NID dam points exist; no published key links a plant to its dams (967 of 1,457 plants have an NID hydro dam within 1 km).

Not published: NID and EIA carry no shared identifier; ORNL EHA, which may bridge them, was not downloaded (data-use form). One dam can be several EIA plants (Hoover) and one plant several NID structures (Bath County).

Generation capacity capacity_mwPartially covered
  • eia-860-hydro
EIA-860 nameplate/net MW per generator for hydro plants of 1 MW or more (conventional 80,061 MW, pumped storage 22,224 MW nameplate).

Not published: Plants under 1 MW are not in EIA-860 and NID has no MW column, so NID hydro-purpose dams without an EIA plant nearby (852 of 2,528 are more than 5 km from any EIA hydro plant) have no capacity.

Reservoir volume reservoir_volumeCovered

Operator operatorPartially covered
  • eia-860-hydro
  • eia-923-hydro
  • nid-usace
EIA-860/923 give the operating company per plant; NID gives owners, not operators (179 of 2,528 hydro rows have no owner).

Not published: No operator field for dams; owner/operator differences between associated NID structures and the EIA plant are not published (Conowingo: NID owner blank, EIA operator Constellation Power, Inc).

Annual generation annual_generationCovered
  • eia-923-hydro
EIA-923 2025 net generation (MWh) by plant, 1,401 hydro plants; negative totals at many pumped-storage plants; dams without generators have none.

Main caveats

  • No common key between EIA plant codes and NID IDs; the coordinate join matched 967 of 1,457 EIA plants within 1 km and is not a published crosswalk.
  • Counting units differ: EIA = generators/plants (a dam may be two plants, e.g. Hoover NV+AZ); NID = dams and associated structures (Bath County has five rows).
  • NID gives owners, normal and maximum storage (acre-feet), but no MW, operator or EIA/FERC IDs; hydroelectric purpose is incomplete (Grand Coulee lacks it).
  • EIA-923 net generation at pumped-storage plants is often negative (22 of 39 plants with pumped-storage generators); do not read it as production.
  • NID storage definitions (normal includes dead storage; max includes surcharge) were found only on mirrored dictionaries, not USACE's own.
  • ORNL's EHA plant database was not downloaded: its page requires a Data Use Policy form with personal details.
