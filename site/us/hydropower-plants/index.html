No single official table links hydro plants and dams. EIA-860 (2025) lists 1,457 hydro plants (3,981 conventional and 150 pumped-storage generators) with coordinates, MW and operator; EIA-923 adds 2025 net MWh, negative at many pumped-storage plants. USACE's National Inventory of Dams (CSV updated 2026-11-20; 92,674 rows, 2,529 with a hydroelectric purpose) gives dam location, owner and normal/maximum storage in acre-feet but no MW or EIA code. A coordinate join matched 967 of 1,457 EIA plants within 1 km of an NID hydro dam. ORNL's EHA database sits behind a form.

Confidence reason: EIA-860/923 and NID were downloaded and cross-checked on four facilities and by a measured spatial join; EHA was not opened and NID definitions rest on mirrors.

Local search terms

  • NID ID — National Inventory of Dams identifier (e.g. MD00097), one per dam or associated structure.
  • Acre-foot (acre-ft) — Reservoir volume unit used by NID; about 1,233.5 cubic metres.
  • Pumped storage (PS) — Hydro plant that pumps water uphill; EIA-923 net generation can be negative.

Main caveats

  • No common key between EIA plant codes and NID IDs; the coordinate join matched 967 of 1,457 EIA plants within 1 km and is not a published crosswalk.
  • Counting units differ: EIA = generators/plants (a dam may be two plants, e.g. Hoover NV+AZ); NID = dams and associated structures (Bath County has five rows).
  • NID gives owners, normal and maximum storage (acre-feet), but no MW, operator or EIA/FERC IDs; hydroelectric purpose is incomplete (Grand Coulee lacks it).
  • EIA-923 net generation at pumped-storage plants is often negative (22 of 39 plants with pumped-storage generators); do not read it as production.
  • NID storage definitions (normal includes dead storage; max includes surcharge) were found only on mirrored dictionaries, not USACE's own.
  • ORNL's EHA plant database was not downloaded: its page requires a Data Use Policy form with personal details.

eia-860-hydroAnnual Electric Generator Report, Form EIA-860 (hydroelectric generators, 2025)

U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) · Official · Zip of Excel (.xlsx) workbooks (Plant, Generator Operable/Proposed/Retired, Owner, Layout) · Direct download · License: Unknown

EIA-860 lists 4,131 hydro generators at 1,457 plants (80,061 MW conventional, 22,224 MW pumped storage nameplate) with plant coordinates and operator.

Example request

curl -L -o eia8602025.zip https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/xls/eia8602025.zip && unzip eia8602025.zip -d eia860_2025  # filter 3_1_Generator_Y2025.xlsx (Operable) on Technology in ('Conventional Hydroelectric','Hydroelectric Pumped Storage'); join 2___Plant_Y2025.xlsx on Plant Code
