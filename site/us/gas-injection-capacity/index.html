No US source publishing grid hosting capacity for new biomethane injection by zone or point was found (federal, CPUC and 5 utilities checked). The closest official data are interstate pipeline informational postings (FERC 18 CFR 284.13): daily design, operating and operationally available capacity in MMBtu for existing RNG/biogas receipt points, e.g. Northern Natural Gas (15 points) and Florida Gas Transmission (5). Gas utilities (PG&E, SoCalGas, Con Edison, NW Natural) give takeaway capacity only in per-project screening or feasibility studies (non-binding under the California tariff); seasonal limits are noted but not published.

Confidence reason: Absence of published LDC capacity checked on 5 utilities in 4 states and in the CPUC tariff; interstate data tested on 2 pipelines with queries; SoCalGas/National Grid sites blocked and 176 pipelines unsampled.

Local search terms

  • operationally available capacity (OAC) — Posted capacity still schedulable at a pipeline point for a gas day and cycle; the field to read at RNG receipt points.
  • informational postings — Public section of an interstate pipeline website where FERC-required capacity and location data are posted.
  • receipt point — Point where gas, including RNG, enters a pipeline (Flow Ind R).
  • takeaway capacity — Utility network capability to move gas away from an interconnection point; reported per project in screening studies.
  • Interconnection Screening Study — First, non-binding utility study for a proposed RNG interconnection under California tariffs.
  • Standard Renewable Gas Interconnection Tariff — CPUC-adopted tariff (D.20-08-035): SoCalGas/SDG&E Rule 45, PG&E Gas Rule 29.
  • design capacity — Nameplate daily capacity of a pipeline point, posted with OAC.
  • MMBtu — Million British thermal units; unit of interstate capacity postings, per gas day.

Main caveats

  • Interstate OAC describes existing receipt points only; it is not available capacity for a new plant and must not be read as hosting capacity.
  • The distribution utilities checked provide takeaway capacity through project-specific studies.
  • The public sources checked lack firm and seasonal capacity figures for new connections. Existing point postings vary by gas day and nomination cycle.
  • Do not infer capacity from plant output (EPA LMOP flows, CPUC injected therms) or from pipeline maps (NPMS, utility maps).
  • Seven interstate pipeline postings were checked. Two yielded capacity figures for existing biomethane receipt points.

interstate-oac-postingsInterstate pipeline informational postings: operationally available capacity and location lists per receipt point

FERC-regulated interstate natural gas pipelines, each on its own informational postings site · Official · JSON and CSV (Northern Natural Gas API); CSV/Excel/ODS/PDF/XML downloads (Energy Transfer ipost) · API · License: unknown

Daily point-level design, operating, scheduled and available capacity (MMBtu) for existing biogas/RNG receipt points on interstate pipelines.

Coverage note
Only interstate pipelines and only points already built; NNG (15 biogas receipt points, Midwest) and FGT (5 RNG receipt points, Florida) confirmed; other pipelines sampled.

Total: 178 · Listed: 7 · Tested: 7 · Data found: 2

FERC estimated 178 interstate pipelines under FERC-551. Seven operators' postings were opened and biomethane receipt-point capacity was confirmed in two (NNG and FGT).

Informational Postings > Capacity > Operationally Available, filtered by location type (NNG 'BIO', FGT 'RNG') or by names containing RNG, BIOGAS or DAIRY; county/state in Informational Postings > Locations.

See member links and checks in the detail (interstate-oac-postings)

Caveats

  • Capacity of an existing RNG meter point, not hosting capacity for new plants.
  • OAC is an estimate per gas day and cycle; delivery (D) rows at the same site are not injection.
