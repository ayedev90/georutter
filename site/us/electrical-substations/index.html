United States electrical substations data sources

Where can I get the locations of electrical substations in the United States (transmission and distribution), with voltage level and operator?

Answer

No open official U.S. national substation dataset exists: EIA and HIFLD explicitly state they do not publish substation locations, DHS HIFLD Open shut down in late 2025 (Data.gov lists it restricted public), and FERC Form 715 (>=100 kV) is withheld as CEII. Official public data is limited to scattered state/regional GIS layers (e.g., MassCZM) and utility distribution hosting-capacity portals across ~26 states + DC + PR. Best free structured options are Oregon State University's June 2022 HIFLD mirror (79,280 U.S. transmission points with MAX_VOLT but no operator) and OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik or TransmissionMap (31,156 CONUS-bbox points, 96.7% NA voltage, 38.2% operator).

Local search terms

  • electric substation — Standard US federal/HIFLD/EIA term for facilities switching, transforming, or regulating grid voltage
  • transmission substation — Bulk-power substation generally operating at >= 69 kV (or >= 100 kV integrated grid under FERC Form 715)
  • distribution substation — Substation stepping voltage down to primary distribution feeders (commonly 4 kV to 34.5 kV / < 69 kV)
  • switching station — Substation that switches transmission lines at a single voltage without voltage-step transformers
  • substation bank (subbank) — Transformer bank inside a substation feeding distribution circuits (key unit in utility Hosting Capacity maps)
  • tap / dead end / riser — Non-switchyard line junction nodes bundled inside HIFLD's Substations layer (22,367 US rows)
  • CEII (Critical Energy/Electric Infrastructure Information) — Statutory category under 18 CFR § 388.113 used to withhold bulk grid locations and FERC Form 715 data
  • hosting capacity map — Utility map showing distribution grid capacity for DER/EV interconnection, often referencing substation IDs

Main caveats

  • DHS HIFLD Open shut down in late 2025; Data.gov (c633a) marks Electric Substations 'restricted public', and neither HIFLD Next nor Source Cooperative includes substations.
  • OSU's live HIFLD mirror (79,280 US records) is frozen at max VAL_DATE 2022-06-03, has 48.9% UNKNOWN* placeholder names (38,744), no OPERATOR field, and incomplete <69 kV distribution.
  • OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik/TransmissionMap carries voltage (96.7% NA CSV) and operator (38.2% NA CSV, 69.0% RI PBF) with 2026 updates, but covers fewer total US sites (~31,156 CONUS bbox).
  • Utility distribution Hosting Capacity maps are fragmented across ~58 utilities/agencies in 26 states + DC + PR (May 2024 DOE count) with incompatible schemas and often polylines rather than points.
  • Official federal FERC Form 715 (>=100 kV) and DHS HIFLD Secure data require CEII NDA or federal authorization; commercial vendors (Rextag, MAPSearch, LandGate, Infrageomatics) are paid.
