United States industrial emitters data sources

Where can I get a list of large industrial facilities in the United States, with location, sector, operator and annual emissions by pollutant, including CO2?

Answer

Three EPA programs answer different parts. The GHGRP (Envirofacts API) gives facility coordinates, NAICS, parent company and annual CO2, CH4 and N2O by sector for 11,281 facility records in reporting year 2023, the latest published year. TRI (API, RY2024) gives toxic-chemical releases by facility in pounds. NEI 2023 (downloadable) gives point-source criteria and hazardous air pollutants by process but no CO2. Facility ids differ across programs, so joins rely on FRS ids, TRI ids carried in NEI, or name and coordinates.

Main caveats

  • GHGRP covers facilities above a threshold (generally 25,000 t CO2e/year, 40 CFR 98.2); its latest published year is 2023. EPA has not published 2024; only EIP's FOIA copy is known.
  • API gas rows hold CO2e; for CO2 it equals tonnes, but CH4 and N2O rows are CO2e (518.22 t CH4 appears as 12,955.5); V_GHG_EMITTER_SUBPART gives ghg_quantity (mass).
  • A facility has several rows per gas (one per sector/subpart): Baytown refinery 12,561,038 + 60,478 t CO2 must be summed; supplier quantities are not emissions.
  • NEI units differ by pollutant (criteria pollutants in tons, hazardous air pollutants in pounds), it is triennial (2023) and has no CO2; TRI is in pounds (dioxins in grams).
  • Operator is not published as such: GHGRP/TRI give parent company with ownership percent; TRI longitude is stored unsigned (95.0069 for -95.0069).
  • Region 6 NEI only was read (partial download); TRI and NEI reporting thresholds were not verified.
