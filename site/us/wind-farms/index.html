Two official US datasets together answer the question. The USGS U.S. Wind Turbine Database (v9.1, 28 Sep 2026) has a verified coordinate per turbine, project name, project-phase turbine count and MW, and commissioning year, via API and CSV. EIA-860 (final 2025 data) adds operator (the company running each generator), nameplate MW, turbine count and month/year per generator, at plant level. The two join on USWTDB eia_id = EIA Plant Code; the join is incomplete. USWTDB has no operator field.

Confidence reason: Both official datasets were downloaded and queried on 2026-11-19; one farm (Roscoe, TX) was traced across both and the join rates were computed.

Local search terms

  • Wind farm / wind project — USWTDB 'project' (p_name) and EIA 'plant' (Plant Code) are the farm-level units; a plant can hold several generator records.
  • Nameplate capacity — EIA maximum rated output in MW of a generator.

Requested attributes

Not published: USWTDB has no operator/owner field and GEM no longer updates operators outside China. Operator is available only through the EIA join, for plants with a join.

Main caveats

  • USWTDB counts turbines (77,839 records, 129 offshore); EIA-860 counts generator records (1,573 operable wind) in plants. Aggregate to project/plant before comparing.
  • USWTDB eia_id is null for 5,051 turbine records, and 95 of 1,371 EIA wind plants have no turbine in USWTDB, so the join is partial.
  • USWTDB offshore turbines are mostly state 'NA' and often lack eia_id; USWTDB p_tnum/p_cap describe a phase, not always the whole farm.
  • USWTDB locations are visually checked, not field-verified (~10 m tolerance); EIA gives one plant point, not turbine positions.
  • EIA-860 is annual (latest final = 2025 data); EIA-860M monthly adds status/coordinates but no turbine count or owner shares.

Example request

curl 'https://energy.usgs.gov/api/uswtdb/v1/turbines?select=p_name,p_year,p_tnum,p_cap,t_state,t_county,xlong,ylat,t_conf_loc&eia_id=eq.56593&limit=1'

Example result

Roscoe, Nolan County TX: p_year 2008, p_tnum 209, p_cap 209.00 MW, one point -100.62370, 32.44849 (209 turbine rows for eia_id 56593).
