Confidence
Medium

No national US dataset of available connection capacity exists. Capacity is published utility by utility, mostly as ArcGIS services. Three were queried live: PG&E (GRIP: generation and load capacity in kW per line section, 2.09M sections), SCE (ICA per circuit segment, MAX_REMAIN_CAP per substation, available load capacity per circuit/substation for five plan years) and National Grid New York (PV hosting capacity per feeder and substation bank, no demand). CAISO's transmission heatmap shows substation headroom but offers no data download. No history was found in any service tested.

Confidence reason: Live queries worked for three utilities, but coverage is utility-specific, SCE units and SDG&E were not verified, and history was not found.

Local search terms

  • ICA — Integration Capacity Analysis: California utilities' hosting-capacity method.
  • Hosting capacity — Indicative distribution capacity available for new generation or load.
  • Feeder / line section — Distribution circuit and its segments, the unit of PG&E and SCE ICA.

Main caveats

  • Distribution values are indicative hosting capacity (ICA), not a guarantee of connection; PG&E and others state that all interconnections are subject to engineering review.
  • Level differs: PG&E gives line sections, SCE circuit segments plus substations, National Grid feeders and substation banks; only some of them give an identifiable substation value.
  • Only 3 of about 58 utilities/agencies listed in the DOE atlas (May 2024) were queried; transmission headroom exists only as CAISO's viewer, without data.
  • No service declares units: kW for PG&E and MW for National Grid are inferred from field names; SCE units are unknown (its user guide returned HTTP 403).
  • Queue and project layers (e.g. PG&E queued DG, GNA needs) are not available capacity and were excluded.
  • Services expose only the current snapshot plus per-record analysis dates; no archived series was found.

Example request

curl -G 'https://services2.arcgis.com/mJaJSax0KPHoCNB6/ArcGIS/rest/services/DRPComplianceRelProd/FeatureServer/3/query' --data-urlencode 'where=FeederName IS NOT NULL' --data-urlencode 'outFields=FeederName,CSV_LineSection,LoadCapacity_kW,GenCapacity_kW,limiting_criteria,ICA_Analysis_Date' --data-urlencode 'resultRecordCount=3' --data-urlencode 'returnGeometry=false' --data-urlencode 'f=json'
