United States renewable siting zones data sources

Where can I get the official zoning that classifies land for renewable energy siting in the United States, such as environmental sensitivity maps or renewables acceleration areas, as geometry, with category and the technologies it applies to?

Answer

No nationwide official renewable-siting zoning was found. BLM publishes open ArcGIS services for its western public lands: 153 solar energy zone polygons, California DRECP development focus areas (171, with a technology class) and variance lands (7), and the 2024 Final Solar PEIS analysis (9,966,036 polygons flagged Excluded or Other). The 2024 Western Solar Plan makes over 31 million acres available. California's CEC publishes dissolved solar and wind exclusion footprints for planning, not for siting. Texas CREZ geometry was not found. Resource-potential layers are different objects.

Local search terms

  • Solar Energy Zone (SEZ) — BLM-designated area prioritised for utility-scale solar
  • Development Focus Area (DFA) — DRECP lands where renewable development may occur
  • Variance Process Lands — DRECP lands open only through a variance process
  • REDA (Renewable Energy Development Area) — Arizona BLM areas from the RDEP Record of Decision
  • Western Solar Plan / Solar PEIS — BLM programmatic plan for solar on public lands
  • CREZ (Competitive Renewable Energy Zone) — Texas transmission-planning zones designated by the PUCT
  • techno-economic exclusion — CEC exclusion of areas unsuitable for technical or economic reasons

Main caveats

  • BLM layers cover federal lands in western states only. State, local and private-land rules for renewables were barely examined (only California's CEC).
  • Layers mix object types: preference areas (DFA, SEZ), conditional lands (variance), an exclusion analysis (Solar PEIS), hub points (WGA) and resource potential. They are not one classification.
  • BLM's FAQ says the 2024 plan eliminates variance lands as a category, yet the DRECP layer still lists 7 variance polygons; the final-plan map layer was not identified.
  • CEC land-use screens 'should not be used, on their own, to guide siting'; each layer is one dissolved polygon with no category field.
  • The Solar PEIS analysis layer (9.97 million polygons, edited 2024-08-21) is solar only and its Excluded/Other totals were not reconciled with the official 31 million acres.
