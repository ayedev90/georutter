United States railways data sources

Where can I get the railway network (geometry) of the United States, with line type, electrification, infrastructure manager and traffic?

Answer

FRA's North American Rail Network (NARN) Lines, distributed by BTS in NTAD, is the national line layer: 302,774 polylines (235,703 in the US) at 1:24,000 or better, with owner, dispatcher or maintainer marks, trackage rights, passenger-service type and a network class that marks abandoned and out-of-service lines. It has no electrification or traffic field. Reported train counts exist only at highway-rail crossings (FRA Form 71, 438,962 rows). Electrification was found only in OpenStreetMap tags, sampled at two places.

Local search terms

  • North American Rail Network Lines — Name of the FRA/BTS NARN layer on data.gov and NTAD.
  • Crossing Inventory Data (Form 71) — FRA table of highway-rail crossings with train counts.
  • reporting mark — Short railroad code used in RROWNER and TRKRGHTS fields (AMTK, UP, BNSF, CSXT, NS).
  • trackage rights — Right of one railroad to run over another's track; listed in TRKRGHTS1-9.
  • subdivision — Railroad-defined line section (SUBDIV field) used in NARN and the crossing inventory.
  • STRACNET — Strategic Rail Corridor Network flag in NARN.
  • NARN — North American Rail Network.
  • NTAD — National Transportation Atlas Database published by BTS.

Infrastructure manager infrastructure_managerPartially covered

RROWNER1-3 hold railroad marks for dispatching, maintenance and/or land ownership without saying which role; TRKRGHTS1-9 list railroads with trackage rights.

Not published: Roles are merged in one field (3,076 US rows carry a second or third mark) and train operators are not a field. Marks differ between datasets: AMTK in NARN, ATK in the crossing inventory.

Traffic trafficPartially covered
  • fra-crossing-inventory-form71
Reported trains per day at crossing points (daylight and night through trains, passenger trains per day) with the count year; no per-line tonnage or train total was found.

Not checked: Crossing counts are self-reported points with mixed years. STB waybill data and state density maps were not opened (Iowa DOT page returned 403 to curl).

Main caveats

  • NET separates the active network from abandoned (A, R), out-of-service (X) and trail (T) features. Filter before measuring: US rows total 235,703, of which 81,529 are main network (M).
  • PASSNGR is the type of passenger service on a line, not a train count or an electrification flag. Do not infer either from it.
  • RROWNER1-3 mix dispatcher, maintainer and land owner; TRKRGHTS lists railroads with rights, not the operators that actually run trains.
  • Crossing counts are self-reported: 52% of 438,962 rows give a count year (51,282 rows 2025, 12,274 rows 2019) and 192,201 rows are closed crossings.
  • Subdivision names and railroad codes differ between datasets (Hartford Line in NARN vs New Haven in the crossing inventory; AMTK vs ATK).
  • railroads.dot.gov and publications.iowa.gov refused default curl requests (403) and gis.fra.dot.gov did not connect; NARN and crossing data were reachable.
