United States ports data sources

Where can I get a list of commercial ports in the United States, with location, port authority and annual cargo, passengers and vessel calls?

Answer

USACE's 'port' is a statistical area with a numeric code: its Principal Ports layer gives CY2023 short tons for the top 150 (2.6 billion short tons) and its Port layer gives 370 port polygons with legal descriptions. BTS Port Data adds TEU, tonnage and vessel calls (48 ports, 2023) by the same code; cruise passengers exist for the top 25. USACE Navigation Facilities lists 9,491 docks with owner and operator text. No port-authority field exists; names mix ports and authorities.

Local search terms

  • Principal Ports of the United States — USACE list of the 150 ports with the highest tonnage, published in NTAD as Principal Ports.
  • Port Performance Freight Statistics Program — BTS program whose tables (Port Data, Top 25) sit on data.bts.gov.
  • Port Statistical Area (PSA) — USACE reporting area that groups ports for commerce statistics.
  • Navigation Facilities (Master Docks) — USACE terminal and dock list with owners and operators.
  • WCSC / WCUS — Waterborne Commerce Statistics Center and the Waterborne Commerce of the United States reports.
  • short tons — Unit of USACE and BTS tonnage figures.
  • TEU — Twenty-foot equivalent units, the container count unit.
  • UNLOCODE — United Nations location code carried by Navigation Facilities.

Main caveats

  • A USACE port is a statistical area with a code (Houston 2031, Long Beach 4110, New York & New Jersey 398), not an authority or terminal. Names differ by file: Houston Port Authority, TX vs Port of Houston Authority of Harris County, TX.
  • USACE tonnage is in short tons for CY2023 and covers 150 ports ranked by total tonnage. BTS Port Data uses TEU for containers and short tons for bulk; Top 25 TEU tables carry no year.
  • TEU and ranks differ between BTS tables: Long Beach 2023 is 4,786,383 TEU in Port Data but 6,091,978 TEU (imports plus exports plus domestic) in the undated Top 25 table; not reconciled.
  • Vessel calls in BTS Port Data are fractional (Long Beach container 656.5) and exist for 48 ports; cruise passengers cover the top 25 cruise ports only.
  • Docks have 20,574 features, only 9,491 of type Dock; 3,144 docks have operator text and 6,870 owner text; several Long Beach berths share one coordinate.
  • usace.army.mil, bts.gov and maritime.dot.gov pages refused default curl requests (403); the ArcGIS services and data.bts.gov tables were reachable.
