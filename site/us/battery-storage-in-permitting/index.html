US stand-alone BESS permitting is split across state and local authorities; no complete national list was found in the inherited research. EIA-860M September 2026 (released October) gives a planned-generator proxy with 34 filtered stand-alone cases >=1 MW, coordinates and Entity Name, but lacks permit stages/dates and misses early or local applications. Sampled state portals provide actual stages and dates in CA, WA and MA; linked dockets and PDFs supply further details. Paid trackers have different coverage.

Confidence reason: Verified directly against Sep 2026 EIA-860M/2025 EIA-860 spreadsheets, federal NEPA/FAST-41 portals, 9 state siting portals, NYISO queue workbook, OSM Taginfo, Sandia GESDB, GEM, and commercial trackers.

Local search terms

  • Stand-alone BESS / Front-of-the-Meter (FTM) Energy Storage System — Battery facility not co-located with generation; isolated in EIA-860M by checking that all units sharing Plant ID across Operating + Planned have Technology='Batteries'.
  • Status (L) — Regulatory approvals pending. Not under construction — Form EIA-860/860M planned generator status code indicating approvals/permits are pending prior to construction.
  • Opt-In Certification (CA AB 205 / Pub. Resources Code § 25545) — California Energy Commission optional statewide CEQA/siting certification pathway for >=200 MWh energy storage systems, alternative to county conditional use permits.
  • Site Certification / CPCN / Certificate of Environmental Impact and Public Interest — State siting permit terms used by WA EFSEC, CT CSC, and MA EFSB for state-jurisdiction projects.
  • Conditional Use Permit (CUP) / Special Use Permit (SUP) — County or municipal land-use zoning permit required in states without a state BESS siting board (e.g. Texas) or below state capacity thresholds.
  • NFPA 855 / IFC Chapter 1207 — Model U.S. fire safety codes governing stationary BESS fire permitting, hazard mitigation analysis, and UL 9540A testing.

Main caveats

  • Cross-checking Sep 2026 EIA-860M (L) proves it misses active state siting cases: 4/5 CA CEC Opt-In BESS under review, WA Goldeneye (200 MW), and MA EFSB 26-01/26-02 do not appear in EIA-860M (L).
  • EIA-860 annual 3_4_Energy_Storage 'Independent' column is blank in 89/90 Status='L' rows and labels hybrid Plant 65043 ('Dulles Solar and Storage') as 'Y'; stand-alone BESS must be derived across Plant IDs.
  • Grid interconnection queues (NYISO, ERCOT GIS, CAISO, LBNL Queued Up, Interconnection.fyi) track transmission study status and target COD, NOT siting or environmental permitting.
  • Texas leads EIA-860M stand-alone (L) with 16 of 34 plants nationwide despite having no state BESS siting board, underscoring that most BESS permit locally at county/municipal level.
  • Open non-official portals freeze in Jan 2022 (Sandia GESDB), lack a BESS tracker (GEM), have ~0 proposed BESS (OSM Taginfo), or mirror EIA-860M (US Energy Map); PUC+EIA trackers (Halcyon) are paid.
