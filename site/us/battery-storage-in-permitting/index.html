No public national register of US stand-alone BESS in permitting was found; siting is decided by some state boards and by counties or cities. The October 2026 EIA-860M inventory (released 2026-11-24) reports 33 stand-alone battery plants of 1 MW or more with regulatory approvals pending (status L, 4,799.3 MW), a national proxy with coordinates, MW and entity but no permit dates. Its broader inventory has 250 stand-alone plants across all planned statuses, including projects before approvals, already approved or in construction. State boards (CA, WA, MA, CT, MN) and county or city records (Gillespie County and Baytown, Texas) provide administrative stages and dates.

Confidence reason: EIA counts were reproduced on two editions and every state page and Texas record was reopened in this pass. Coverage is a sample (about 10 states, 3 Texas local bodies); doubtful matches are flagged.

Local search terms

  • Special Use Permit (SUP) / Conditional Use Permit (CUP) — City or county land-use approval for a BESS site (e.g. Baytown, Texas).
  • Site permit (energy storage system) — Minnesota PUC permit required for storage of 10 MW or more.
  • Opt-In Certification (AB 205) — Optional California Energy Commission route for storage of 200 MWh or more.
  • Petition for declaratory ruling (§16-50k) — Connecticut Siting Council route used for small BESS (e.g. Petition 1728).
  • Certificate of Environmental Impact and Public Interest — Massachusetts EFSB certificate sought by BESS applicants.
  • Stop work order / Notice of Violation — County enforcement record (fire marshal, floodplain administrator) that dates a BESS stage in Texas.
  • Reinvestment zone / tax abatement agreement (Tax Code ch. 312) — Texas county or city record that names a BESS project and its site.
  • Status codes P, L, T, U, V, TS — EIA-860/860M planned-unit stages from approvals not initiated to construction complete.

Main caveats

  • The September 2026 EIA-860M approvals-pending subset (L) omitted 4 of 5 CA Opt-In BESS under review, WA Goldeneye and MA EFSB 26-01/26-02. Compare EIA and local dockets by scope and dated snapshot.
  • EIA-860M has no stand-alone flag: exclude Plant IDs with any non-battery generator in the Planned or Operating sheet. Interconnection queues (ERCOT GIS, LBNL) show grid study status, not permits.
  • Statuses can disagree: CA CEC shows Potentia-Viridi (400 MW) as 'Compliance - Pre-Construction' while EIA-860M October 2026 lists it as '(U) Under construction'.
  • Cross-source matching is weak: project LLC names differ from developers and MW can differ (CA Vaca Dixon 157 MW vs EIA 'Vaca BESS' 59.5 MW, 0.33 km apart). Match on coordinates or county plus name; flag doubtful matches.
  • In Texas, checked stage evidence came only from county and city records (Gillespie County, Baytown); Fort Bend County, ERCOT and the PUCT rejected automated requests.
