Confidence

EIA's Refinery Capacity Report (Form EIA-820, 1 Jan 2026) lists 130 operable refineries by company, state and city with crude distillation capacity (18.16 million b/cd; 128 operating, 2 idle) and shutdowns, but no coordinates were retrieved. Pipeline routes are not downloadable by the public: PHMSA NPMS shows hazardous liquid lines one county at a time (+/-500 ft) and gives line data to government and operators only; its public county sheets give active miles by operator and commodity. Open line files found are EIA crude oil (236) and HGL (133) layers on geo.dot.gov.

Confidence reason: EIA workbook and tables and NPMS sheets were downloaded and counted; refinery coordinates and EIA's product-pipeline layer could not be reached.

Local search terms

  • PADD — Petroleum Administration for Defense District, EIA refining regions 1 to 5.
  • b/cd, b/sd — Barrels per calendar day and per stream day, units of refinery capacity.
  • Hazardous liquid (HL) pipeline — PHMSA category covering crude oil, refined products, NGL and other liquids.
  • Non-HVL petroleum product (PRD) — Refined products that are not highly volatile liquids, in NPMS commodity codes.
  • HGL / NGL — Hydrocarbon gas liquids / natural gas liquids.
  • NPMS / PIMMA — National Pipeline Mapping System and its account-based viewer for officials and operators.

Main caveats

  • Pipeline routes: PHMSA NPMS public access is a county-at-a-time viewer with a minimum accuracy of +/-500 feet; line data is released to government officials and operators only.
  • The line files found (EIA crude oil and HGL copies on geo.dot.gov) are layers named _202001 with only operator and pipeline name fields; they are not refined-product pipelines.
  • EIA refinery data are tables by city; no coordinates were obtained, so refineries cannot be mapped from the tested files.
  • EIA counts 130 operable refineries; 124 have crude distillation rows, and the other 6 are non-refinery operators of downstream units.
  • Capacity units differ: refinery capacity is barrels per calendar day or stream day, while NPMS gives pipeline miles, and no pipeline throughput was found.
  • NPMS mileage sheets (data as of 2026-07-16) are totals by county, operator and commodity, not segment geometry or capacity.
