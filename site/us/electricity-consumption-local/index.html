No national dataset: EIA-861 gives sales (MWh) by utility, state and sector, and only lists counties served. Local metered data exist state by state: New York's Utility Energy Registry gives monthly MWh by city/town/village and sector (ZIP only for NYC) via open API; PG&E publishes monthly kWh by ZIP and customer type (download behind a form); some utilities send cities annual reports (e.g. Xcel). ZIP codes are not Census ZCTAs.

Confidence reason: NY registry fully tested via API and EIA checked; California tested only at page level (form), other states sampled with one Xcel report.

Local search terms

  • Utility Energy Registry (UER) — New York registry of utility-reported community energy use on data.ny.gov.
  • Energy Data Request Program (EDRP) — CPUC-ordered utility program publishing usage by ZIP code in California (D.14-05-016).
  • Community Energy Report — Utility report of a city's annual energy use by customer class (e.g. Xcel Energy).
  • Sales to ultimate customers — EIA-861 term for retail electricity sales by utility, state and sector.
  • ZCTA — Census ZIP Code Tabulation Area, an areal approximation of USPS ZIP codes; not identical to ZIP codes.
  • 15/15 rule; 100/15 aggregation — Privacy thresholds for releasing aggregated customer usage (PG&E: 100 residential, 15 non-residential, 15% share).

Main caveats

  • No federal source gives municipal or ZIP consumption; EIA-861 is by utility and state, and its county list is service territory without sales.
  • Local data are privacy-screened: withheld sectors (NY -999), merged ZIPs (PG&E) or merged classes (Xcel).
  • USPS ZIP codes used by utilities are delivery-based and differ from Census ZCTAs and from municipal boundaries.
  • Utility totals cover only that utility's customers; municipalities served by several utilities need all of them.
