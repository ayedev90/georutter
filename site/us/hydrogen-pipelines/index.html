PHMSA's NPMS has restricted GIS data for reported hydrogen transmission lines. Texas RRC offers public line geometry. Its name filter identifies candidate hydrogen segments and can miss or misclassify lines. PHMSA annual reports give operators and mileage without routes. Capacity per pipeline was not verified. Angeles Link documentation contains preliminary corridors.

Confidence reason: Texas, PHMSA, NPMS and Louisiana checks were tested; PHMSA and operator sites block automated clients (PHMSA data read from an archived ZIP); IEA contents and planned projects outside California remain unverified.

Local search terms

  • Hydrogen Gas — PHMSA annual report commodity group (PARTA5COMMODITY) for hydrogen pipeline systems.
  • HG — NPMS commodity code for hydrogen gas.
  • OGT (Other Gas Transmission) — Texas RRC commodity code under which hydrogen lines are permitted and mapped.
  • T-4 permit — Texas RRC pipeline operating permit; T4PERMIT joins the RRC map layer and the hydrogen list.
  • NPMS / PIMMA — PHMSA National Pipeline Mapping System and its restricted web map for officials.
  • PS-48 New Construction Commencement Report — Texas filing under 16 TAC §8.115 listing pipelines about to be built.
  • hydrogen blending demonstration — Utility pilots injecting hydrogen into isolated natural gas sections (CPUC A.22-09-006).
  • merchant hydrogen pipeline — Private lines of industrial gas suppliers (Air Products, Linde, Air Liquide) serving refineries and chemical plants.

Main caveats

  • PHMSA limits access to the NPMS GIS data. Its public viewer shows county-level selections.
  • Texas RRC files hydrogen as 'OTHER GAS'; hydrogen lines are identified only by names containing H2/HYDROGEN, so some lines may be missed or mixed with other gases.
  • Capacity per pipeline was not found in the sources checked. Diameter and annual operator volume measure different attributes.
  • Angeles Link materials show preliminary corridors. CPUC decision D.26-04-034 denied ratepayer cost recovery for Phase 2 engineering and design studies.
  • Blends (Hawaii SNG with 10-12% H2 in service; California pilots of 0.1-20% pending) are not dedicated hydrogen pipelines; no US conversion of a gas line to 100% hydrogen was found.

rrc-tx-pipelinesRailroad Commission of Texas pipeline layer (T-4 permitted pipelines): public ArcGIS map service and county shapefiles

Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC), Pipeline Permitting & Mapping · Official · ArcGIS REST MapServer layer (JSON/GeoJSON); county ZIP shapefiles pipelineNNN.zip (NAD27 geographic) · API · License: unknown

Texas only: statewide ArcGIS service and county shapefiles with line geometry, operator, diameter, T-4 permit and In Service/Abandoned status; hydrogen is identified by 'H2'/'HYDROGEN' in system names.
