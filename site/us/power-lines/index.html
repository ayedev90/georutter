United States power lines data sources

Where can I get the routes (geometry) of electricity transmission and distribution lines in the United States, with voltage level, operator and status?

Answer

No national government layer of US line routes is live: DHS's HIFLD Open, still cited by EIA, was withdrawn in 2025. An Esri archive (data to 2024-09-30) keeps 94,619 transmission segments (about 69 kV+) with voltage, owner and status, though many are inferred or blank. Distribution routes exist only per utility (tested: National Grid NY, 1,913 feeders). California's CEC publishes a current official layer (6,838 segments, with proposed lines). OpenStreetMap adds uneven operator/voltage tags. No national planned-lines layer was found.

Main caveats

  • HIFLD Open (DHS) was sunset in 2025; the usable copy is an Esri archive with data to 2024-09-30, no longer updated, while EIA's FAQ still points to HIFLD.
  • Counts are line segments (polyline features), not circuits or route-km; 59,111 of 94,619 HIFLD segments are flagged INFERRED=Y.
  • HIFLD attributes are incomplete: no OWNER 17,990, no STATUS 20,819, VOLTAGE<=0 14,248. OWNER is not a verified operator.
  • Distribution geometry is utility by utility; only National Grid NY (1,913 feeders) was tested.
  • Planned lines are not systematic: HIFLD has no planned class; CEC's 14 'Proposed' lines include stale 2014-era entries.
  • OpenStreetMap completeness is uneven and the public Overpass server returned 406/502 errors during tests.
