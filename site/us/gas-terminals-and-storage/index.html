Underground storage: EIA's EIA-191 field data (API, monthly to Aug 2026) gives operator, county, Active/Inactive, working gas and max daily withdrawal; PHMSA's annual UNGS file adds exact coordinates. LNG: PHMSA's annual file lists 191 regulated plants (186 in service) with operator, state/ZIP, status and liquefaction/vaporization/tank capacity; EIA's quarterly file gives export trains operating, under construction or approved. Proposed terminals and storage projects are only in FERC PDF maps and Federal Register notices; exact LNG plant positions are restricted (NPMS).

Confidence reason: Core EIA and PHMSA files were downloaded and checked; PHMSA 2025 data came from an archived copy, and the FERC PDF maps and MARAD pages could not be read because they block automated clients.

Local search terms

  • underground natural gas storage facility (UNGSF) — PHMSA term for an underground storage facility; Part B of Form 7100.4-1 is filed once per facility.
  • working gas capacity — Storage volume available for withdrawal (EIA in Mcf, PHMSA in BCF); excludes base (cushion) gas.
  • maximum deliverability — EIA-191 maximum withdrawal rate in Mcf/day; published as 'Maximum Daily Delivery'.
  • peak shaving LNG plant (peakshaver) — Utility LNG storage plant for winter peaks; listed by PHMSA, and by FERC only when FERC-jurisdictional.
  • Section 3 of the Natural Gas Act — Basis of FERC authority over onshore and near-shore LNG import/export terminals.
  • pre-filing (PF docket) — FERC stage before a formal application; PF dockets mark planned projects, CP dockets filed applications.
  • final investment decision (FID) — Milestone after which EIA moves an approved export project to under construction.
  • deepwater port — Offshore LNG terminal licensed by MARAD under the Deepwater Port Act, outside FERC jurisdiction.

Main caveats

  • Different publishers measure LNG capacity differently: PHMSA reports maximum liquefaction rate (Sabine Pass 5100 MMCF/D), EIA nameplate per train (6 x 0.59 Bcf/d baseload, 0.76 peak).
  • PHMSA field TOTAL_CAPACITY_MMCF is maximum vaporization in MMCF per day, not storage volume; LNG tank storage is TOTAL_CAPACITY_BBLS (barrels).
  • EIA Maximum Daily Delivery is a withdrawal rate. PHMSA injected and withdrawn values are annual volumes. Facility-level maximum injection rates were not verified.
  • phmsa.dot.gov, ferc.gov and maritime.dot.gov reject automated clients (403); the PHMSA ZIPs are also attached to data.transportation.gov dataset aemm-7pyq, but that copy lags (2024 data).
  • Offshore LNG deepwater ports are licensed by MARAD, not FERC; FERC lists cover onshore/near-shore terminals only, and most of the 170+ LNG plants (utility peakshavers, satellites) are not FERC-jurisdictional.

Document access
