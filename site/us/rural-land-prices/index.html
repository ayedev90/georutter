United States rural land prices data sources

Where can I get rural land prices or rural land transactions in the United States, by municipality or region and year?

Answer

Best source: USDA NASS. Quick Stats (public bulk file) holds survey estimates of farm real estate value per acre by state from 1850 to 2026, plus cropland and pasture, and Census of Agriculture values by county for 1997-2022; the 2026 values were released 2026-07-31. They are estimated market values, not sale prices. Recorded sales are published by states: Vermont posts quarterly sale statistics by town and category. No national sales dataset was found; tax-assessed values are different.

Local search terms

  • farm real estate value — NASS value of all land and buildings on farms, per acre
  • cropland / pasture value — NASS value per acre of land by use, without buildings
  • Agricultural Land Values Survey — NASS annual survey (April-June) behind the estimates
  • Census of Agriculture — Five-yearly census; source of county land values
  • (D) — Value withheld to avoid disclosing data for individual operations
  • valid sale (PTT) — Vermont's market-transaction sale, excluding straw and family transfers
  • Grand List — Vermont's municipal assessed-value roll (tax value, not price)

Main caveats

  • NASS values are survey-based estimates of what land 'could be sold' for; farm real estate includes buildings. They are not recorded sale prices.
  • Some state cells are withheld: the report shows (D) (e.g. Vermont cropland 2022-2024); the Quick Stats bulk file omits those survey rows. US totals exclude Alaska and Hawaii.
  • County values exist only from the Census of Agriculture (every five years; 1997-2022, 2022 latest); the annual survey is state or region level.
  • The Quick Stats API needs a registered key (not created here); values were reproduced from the public bulk file and the 2026 report instead.
  • Vermont sale statistics follow the department's 'valid sales' rule and its categories; 'Farms' rows had no per-acre figures in the 2025 and 2026 files read.
  • Tax-assessed (Grand List) values and listing offers are different objects and were not used as prices.

Problems
  • Quick Stats API needs a registered key and was not run; an unauthenticated call returned HTTP 401 {"error":["unauthorized"]}.

Example
Example request
curl -sSL 'https://www.nass.usda.gov/sites/default/files/datasets/qs.economics_20261120.txt.gz' | gunzip -c | awk -F'\t' 'NR==1 || ($10=="AG LAND, INCL BUILDINGS - ASSET VALUE, MEASURED IN $ / ACRE" && $1=="SURVEY" && $13=="STATE" && $16=="VT" && $31==2026)'   # columns 1 SOURCE_DESC, 10 SHORT_DESC, 13 AGG_LEVEL_DESC, 16 STATE_ALPHA, 31 YEAR
Example result
Vermont, 2026, farm real estate incl. buildings: VALUE 4,490 ($/acre), annual, reference period YEAR; same figure in NASS Land Values 2026 Summary. Census 2022 Addison County VT: 3,832 $/acre, CV 9.0.
