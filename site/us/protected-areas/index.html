Start with USGS PAD-US 4.1 for US protected-area boundaries. The queried fee layer had 296,456 polygons. Date_Est was filled for about 24% of that layer. USGS separately lists combined web services and a full geodatabase for overlapping designations, easements and marine areas. Protected Planet supplies international and regional designations. Commercial reuse requires permission.

Confidence reason: Service and WDPA layers queried with counts and an identifiable area; USGS pages and the full geodatabase were blocked to curl and read through WebFetch; state sources other than one form-gated database were not checked.

Local search terms

  • PAD-US — Protected Areas Database of the United States, USGS inventory of public and some private protected lands
  • Des_Tp — PAD-US designation type code: NP National Park, WA Wilderness Area, SP State Park, LP Local Park, MIL Military Land
  • GAP status code — 1 to 4 protection level for biodiversity (1 strict, 4 no known mandate)
  • Fee / Easement — Fee is ownership of land; an easement protects land owned by someone else
  • Date_Est — PAD-US Date of Establishment, a four-character year
  • WDPA — World Database on Protected Areas run by UNEP-WCMC; site_id is the WDPA code
  • Ramsar site — Wetland of international importance under the Ramsar Convention
  • Wilderness Area — Federal designation under the Wilderness Act, coded WA

Not published: Most features carry no establishment year; USGS says the field is not fully populated and points to agencies for dates; no per-designation date was found for overlapping layers.

Main caveats

  • The tested endpoint is the fee layer. For overlaps, use the separate combined services listed by USGS. One area can span several rows, as Yellowstone does.
  • Date_Est represents the year of the current name and designation where reported. It was filled for about 24% of the fee features. Protected Planet status_yr was 0 for about 52% of US records.
  • USGS strongly discourages comparing two PAD-US versions to find changes in protection; 4.0 and 4.1 services both stay online, so name the version used.
  • A category or protected-area record does not prove legal prohibitions: PAD-US also holds military land (MIL), public land and generic 'other' designations, and local park data is incomplete in some states.
  • WDPA data may not be put to commercial use without UNEP-WCMC permission; the public layer cites October 2026 while the country page cites November 2026.

Example request

curl -sG 'https://edits.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PAD-US/PAD_US_4_1/MapServer/0/query' --data-urlencode "where=Unit_Nm='Yellowstone National Park'" --data-urlencode 'outFields=Category,Des_Tp,Unit_Nm,Mang_Name,State_Nm,GIS_Acres,GAP_Sts,IUCN_Cat,Date_Est,WDPA_Cd' --data-urlencode 'returnGeometry=false' --data-urlencode 'f=json'

Example result

3 features (ID, MT, WY): Category Fee, Des_Tp NP, Mang_Name NPS, GAP_Sts 1, IUCN_Cat II, Date_Est '1872', WDPA_Cd 377207; GIS_Acres 2003734 for WY. Count query returns 296456 features.
