BLM's MLRS publishes 598,739 non-closed mining claims as polygons (ArcGIS REST and bulk CSV/GeoJSON) with case number, claim type and status, but the polygons are built from PLSS legal descriptions, not staked perimeters, and the layer has no holder, commodity or grant dates. Other BLM layers (notices, plans of operations, leases) add commodity, effective/expiry dates (often blank) and a customer-name field. Alaska DNR publishes 123,061 state claim polygons with status only.

Confidence reason: BLM and Alaska layers were queried and counted; MLRS report contents, other states and the claim-level date fields were not opened.

Local search terms

  • MLRS — Mineral and Land Records System, BLM's case system (replaces LR2000 and ALIS).
  • lode / placer / mill site / tunnel site — Federal mining claim types (BLM_PROD).
  • locatable / leasable / salable minerals — BLM mineral classes: claimed, leased and sold.
  • plan of operations / notice — BLM surface-management authorizations for mining or exploration.
  • PLSS — Public Land Survey System (township, range, section).
  • LAS — Alaska Land Administration System (case files).
  • ADL — Alaska Division of Lands file type prefix.

Main caveats

  • BLM claim polygons come from legal land descriptions on the PLSS: two 20.66-acre sample claims map to 396-acre polygons; 45,527 have staff-edited geometry (score 100).
  • The claims layer has no holder, commodity or grant/expiry date; BLM_PROD is the claim type (lode, placer, mill site, tunnel site), not a mineral.
  • A claim or permit status ('Active', 'Authorized') shows legal standing only; it does not show that a mine is operating or that minerals exist.
  • 14,997 of 598,739 BLM claim rows have no geometry and 11,821 have no quality score; Closed claims are a separate layer of 79,294 records.
  • EFF_DT and EXP_DT are blank in most notice, plan and lease records (EXP_DT filled in 760 of 1,252 notices but only 13 of 1,122 plans).
  • Holder names exist in some BLM layers (CUST_NM_SEC) and in redacted MLRS reports; this card describes availability only and copies no names.
