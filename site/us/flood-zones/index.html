FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL) is the national digital record of flood hazard zones, issued by FEMA under the NFIP; FEMA says it covers over 90% of the U.S. population. FEMA's data hosts (hazards.fema.gov, msc.fema.gov) still reset the connection here, so geometry was tested in an Esri copy of the 5 October 2022 NFHL (4,399,596 polygons, reduced set), which holds the territories too. FEMA's own domain tables and legend define the zones: A, AE, AH, AO, V and VE are 1% annual chance; shaded X is not one probability (0.2%, shallow or small-basin 1%, levee-protected, future-conditions 1%); D is undetermined.

Confidence reason: FEMA's own definitions, domain tables and issuer role were read. Zone geometry was tested only in a 2022 copy; FEMA's NFHL service and panel dates were not reached.

Local search terms

  • Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) — Area with a 1% annual-chance flood hazard; SFHA_TF = T.
  • FIRM / DFIRM — Flood Insurance Rate Map; DFIRM is its digital form.
  • Zones A, AE, AH, AO, A99, V, VE — 1% annual-chance zones. AO gives depth; V and VE are coastal, with waves.
  • Shaded Zone X — Outside the SFHA: 0.2% annual chance; 1% with depth under 1 foot or drainage under 1 sq mi; levee-protected from 1%. Legacy letter B.
  • Unshaded Zone X — Area of Minimal Flood Hazard, outside the 0.2% floodplain. Legacy letter C.
  • Zone D — Flood hazards undetermined, but possible; no flood hazard analysis.
  • Area Not Included — Separate community product or unmapped; not a hazard class.
  • S_FIRM_Pan — FIRM panel table holding the effective date (EFF_DATE).

Main caveats

  • FEMA hosts (hazards.fema.gov, msc.fema.gov, floodmaps.fema.gov) reset the connection again, also from a second client; the NFHL service layers and live data stay untested. fema.gov documents answered only through some clients.
  • Shaded Zone X has several meanings: 0.2% annual chance; 1% with depth under 1 foot or drainage under 1 sq mi; levee-protected from 1%; 1% future conditions. Do not give 0.2% to every X polygon.
  • The tested copy (5 October 2022) drops unshaded X, open water, NP and most D (114 D remain). 69 X polygons carry SFHA_TF 'U' and 60 carry 'T'; FEMA's reference allows T/F and expects false for X.
  • Probability is not a numeric field: it follows from FLD_ZONE with ZONE_SUBTY. Return periods are 1/p conversions; FEMA calls the 1% flood the base or 100-year flood.
  • Effective dates sit per FIRM panel (S_FIRM_Pan) and Letter of Map Change. The panel table was not reached; the NFHL is updated continuously, so a 2022 copy can differ from current maps.
  • Coverage: FEMA says NFHL digital data cover over 90% of the population (page of 3 April 2025); GAO says FEMA reports FIRMs cover about 98% (FY2020). No polygon does not mean no hazard.

Example request

GET https://services.arcgis.com/P3ePLMYs2RVChkJx/ArcGIS/rest/services/USA_Flood_Hazard_Reduced_Set_gdb/FeatureServer/0/query with where=1=1, geometry=-94.82,29.28,-94.78,29.32, geometryType=esriGeometryEnvelope, inSR=4326, spatialRel=esriSpatialRelIntersects, outFields=*, returnGeometry=false, resultRecordCount=5, f=json.

Example result

First 5 features, DFIRM 48167C (Galveston area, TX): FLD_ZONE AO with SFHA_TF T and DEPTH 1 foot; FLD_ZONE X with ZONE_SUBTY '0.2 Percent Annual Chance Flood Hazard' and SFHA_TF F. Box count repeated on 2026-11-19: 120 features (X 98, AE 9, AO 7, VE 6).
