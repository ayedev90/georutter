United States battery storage data sources

Where can I get a list of battery storage (BESS) installations in operation in the United States, with location, power (MW), energy (MWh), operator and commissioning date?

Answer

EIA publishes a complete official inventory of battery generators at plants of 1 MW or more. The monthly EIA-860M (September 2026) lists 1,147 operating battery generator records at 1,090 plants (56,082 MW; 154,260 MWh) with operator (Entity Name), plant coordinates, MW, MWh and operating month/year. The annual EIA-860 energy-storage schedule adds charge/discharge rates and flags for coupling and for direct support of another unit, which separate standalone from co-located batteries. No other source with comparable coverage was usable.

Main caveats

  • The unit is the generator record, not the installation: one plant (e.g. Moss Landing) has several battery records; sum by Plant ID to get an installation.
  • Plants below 1 MW total nameplate are not covered; 483 planned battery records (64,235 MW) are in a separate 'Planned' sheet and must be excluded.
  • EIA-860M is preliminary and corrected without notice; EIA-860 2025 final holds 1,003 battery records (43,643 MW, 121,045 MWh).
  • Standalone vs co-located: only EIA-860 annual flags it (379 of 1,003 records are 'Direct Support of Another Unit'); EIA-860M has no such flag.
  • MWh is reported by operators and is not checked against any independent source; one record (Viejas Microgrid, 17.5 MW) has no MWh.
  • Coordinates are one plant point shared by all generators of a plant; the public GESDB and OpenStreetMap could not be used.
