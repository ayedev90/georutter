United States curtailment and redispatch data sources

Where can I get renewable curtailment and redispatch volumes in the United States, by zone or node and by period?

Answer

Only CAISO publishes a full series. Its daily renewable report gives curtailed wind and solar MWh by hour, five minutes, day and month, by type and local/system reason; a monthly CSV runs May 2014 to October 2026 (2024: 3,423,376; 2025: 3,766,065) and reconciles with the five-minute file and EIA. CAISO states exceptional-dispatch volume monthly (Sep 2026: 64,296 MWh), the nearest redispatch analogue. MISO shows daily average curtailment in MW only as PDF chart labels; ERCOT's market monitor gives annual percentages. No zone or node values were found; ERCOT's SCED disclosure was blocked.

Local search terms

  • Exceptional dispatch (ExDispatch) — CAISO operator instruction outside the market solution, used for non-modeled constraints and reliability; the closest published analogue of redispatch.
  • Local vs System (curtailment reason) — CAISO reasons: local congestion versus system-wide oversupply.
  • VER — Variable energy resource (wind and solar).
  • SCED — ERCOT security-constrained economic dispatch, source of base points and limits.

Main caveats

  • Coverage is uneven: CAISO has MWh series; MISO only daily average MW in PDF charts; ERCOT only annual percentages (market monitor); other operators not opened.
  • Definitions differ: legacy CAISO PDFs measure against forecast, the market report against the dispatch upper limit; ERCOT's figures cover congestion curtailment.
  • CAISO's exceptional dispatch is manual out-of-market dispatch, not market redispatch; no other operator's redispatch volume was checked.
  • No zone or node values: CAISO local/system reason, MISO and ERCOT are system-wide; ERCOT's SCED disclosure blocked scripted access (403).
  • CAISO's PDF reports and five-minute workbook ended 2025-06-01; the daily HTML report holds its data as chart scripts, not a file; 'Operator Instruction' is undefined there.
  • The monthly CSV has no unit; MWh is inferred from EIA's 2024 figure and the five-minute file.
