EPA publishes two national structured sources. The Clean Watersheds Needs Survey 2022 (CSV/Access) lists about 16,450 records with a Treatment Plant type, with point location, design flow in MGD, effluent treatment level and NPDES permit numbers. ECHO (REST API) lists NPDES facilities with a POTW flag, coordinates, permit design flow and measured monthly effluent flow. They join by permit number. Florida DEP adds a state register with design and permitted capacity. Design flow definitions differ between sources, and plant-level treated volume is only available from monitoring reports.

Confidence reason: Counts reproduced from EPA services and a CSV copy, with a three-way plant cross-check; definitions of design flow remain unreconciled and one state register was tested.

Local search terms

  • POTW — Publicly owned treatment works: municipal wastewater treatment plant.
  • NPDES permit — National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System discharge permit; its ID (for example ME0100064) identifies the discharging facility.
  • DMR — Discharge Monitoring Report: periodic measured effluent values reported against permit limits.
  • MGD — Million gallons per day, the unit used for design and measured flow.
  • CWNS ID — 11-digit identifier of a facility record in the Clean Watersheds Needs Survey.
  • Secondary / Advanced treatment — Official CWNS effluent treatment levels (also Raw, Primary and NA).

Main caveats

  • CWNS is a voluntary 2022 snapshot: about 16,450 records have a Treatment Plant type while EPA's summary counts 17,544 POTWs; the difference is unreconciled.
  • ECHO rows are permits, not plants; use CWPFacilityTypeIndicator = POTW and join by permit number, and expect several permits at one site.
  • Design flow is not one measure: CWNS current and future flow, ECHO permit design flow and Florida design or permitted capacity differ; treated volume comes only from discharge monitoring reports.
  • ECHO queries over 100,000 rows are refused, so Florida, Georgia and Texas were not counted statewide.
  • EPA's CWNS bulk download is behind a popup with a usage-survey dropdown; table contents here were read from a third-party HydroShare copy.
  • Only Florida was tested among state registers; other states may publish capacity or treatment type that these sources lack.

U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Office of Water · Official · ZIP of CSV tables or Microsoft Access database, nationwide or by state; XLSX data dictionary · Direct download · License: Unknown

EPA's 2022 Clean Watersheds Needs Survey lists wastewater facilities nationwide with point location, design flow in MGD, effluent treatment level and NPDES permit numbers.

Example request

Open https://sdwis.epa.gov/ords/sfdw_pub/r/sfdw/cwns_pub/data-download, choose 'Nationwide 2022 CWNS Dataset' and 'Download CSVs' (a usage-survey dropdown appears first); the dictionary link on the same page downloads the XLSX directly.

Example result

CWNS ID 23000025001 (Boothbay Harbor WWTF, Maine, NPDES ME0100064): municipal flow current 0.29 MGD, future 0.64 MGD; effluent level Secondary; point 43.8508, -69.6322.
