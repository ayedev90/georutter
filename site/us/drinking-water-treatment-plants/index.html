United States drinking water treatment plants data sources

Where can I get a list of drinking water treatment plants in the United States, with location, capacity and operator?

Answer

No national list of drinking-water treatment plants with location, capacity and operator was found. EPA's SDWIS (Envirofacts API) and ECHO list systems and regulatory facilities: 246,214 treatment-type rows, which are entry or monitoring points without coordinates or capacity. Iowa DNR publishes a point layer of treatment sub-facilities (5,142 rows, duplicated, many at ZIP centroids) without capacity. Capacity appears only in operator documents, such as Metropolitan Water District's five plants. Supply zones and population served were not used as substitutes.

  • mgd — Million gallons per day.
  • wholesaler — System that sells treated water to other systems (SDWIS flag is_wholesaler_ind).

Iowa DNR gives plant points (5,142 rows, 38% ZIP-centroid, 3,300 m); MWD gives only the city; SDWIS and ECHO give no coordinates.

Treatment capacity capacityPartially covered
  • mwd-treatment-plant-documents
Capacity in mgd was found only in operator documents: five Metropolitan Water District plants (220 to 750 mgd). No capacity field in SDWIS, ECHO, California or Iowa.

Not checked: Nothing nationwide; state layers reported to hold capacity (South Florida Water Management District) could not be opened. Other operators and states were not tested.

Operator operatorPartially covered
  • epa-sdwis-envirofacts
  • epa-echo-sdw
  • mwd-treatment-plant-documents
SDWIS and ECHO give the system, owner type and name; MWD's own documents name the operator of five plants. The Iowa layer names the system only.

Not checked: System names and owner types are not per-plant operators: SDWIS has no operator field at facility level and contact fields hold personal data. Operators beyond MWD were not checked.

Main caveats

  • SDWIS 'TP' rows (246,214; 163,265 active) are regulatory entry or monitoring points such as filter effluent or booster stations, not physical plants, and have no coordinates or capacity.
  • Population served and service area describe a system, not a plant: the wholesaler MWD system shows population served 0 in SDWIS.
  • California's dataset named 'Public Water System Facilities' has one row per system (7,603) and its 'Treatment Plant Class' is an operator grade.
  • Iowa's layer duplicates rows (5,142 rows for about 2,603 sub-facilities) and 1,976 positions are ZIP centroids with 3,300 m accuracy.
  • Capacity is nominal and found only in operator documents tested for one operator.
  • Contact fields in SDWIS hold personal data; none were reproduced.
